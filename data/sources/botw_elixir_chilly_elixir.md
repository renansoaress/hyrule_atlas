### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Chilly Elixirs,(BotW, TotK) also known as Elixirs That Stave off the Heat,(Breath of the Wild) and Cooling Elixirs,(Tears of the Kingdom) are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Drinking Chilly Elixirs will grant Link one level of Heat Resistance. They do this by providing a cooling effect, making them important for travelling through the Gerudo Desert.

When spoken to, Guy the Rito will complain about the heat at Kara Kara Bazaar. He explains that he's heard of an Elixir that can help with the heat, but it isn't sold at the Bazaar. However, despite knowing that he can find the insects needed in the Gerudo Highlands, Guy isn't motivated due to the monsters up there. This initiates the Side Quest "An Ice Guy". Link can make a Chilly Elixir for Guy, who will be happy he can cross the Gerudo Desert during the day. He'll give Link 50 Rupees as a reward.

Chilly Elixirs can be made by Cooking any of the following critters with Monster Parts in a Cooking Pot:

* Cold Darner

* Winterwing Butterfly

By adding any of these chilly ingredients, Link can make a Chilly Elixir that will also heal him:

* Hydromelon

* Chillshroom

* Cool Safflina

* Chillfin Trout
