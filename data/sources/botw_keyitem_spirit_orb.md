## Location and Uses
Spirit Orbs are symbols of courage obtained from the Sheikah Monks after completing their Ancient Shrines. They are spherical, translucent items bearing the Goddess Crest in their center. Link can pray to Goddess Statues to upgrade either his total Hearts or Stamina Vessels in exchange for four Spirit Orbs. There are a total of 120 Spirit Orbs to be obtained in the base game, which allows Link to receive 30 upgrades. An additional four Spirit Orbs can be obtained from Ancient Shrines that appear on the Great Plateau during the "EX The Champions' Ballad" Main Quest as part of The Champions' Ballad DLC Pack, allowing Link to receive one additional upgrade.
