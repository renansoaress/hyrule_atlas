### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Copious Seafood Skewers are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Copious Seafood Skewers can be made by Cooking four different types of Seafood in a Cooking Pot. When eaten, they heal some of Link's Hearts. Despite being a heap of random Items shoved onto a skewer, they are known to be a tasty dish.

Because of their specified recipe, very few variations of Copious Seafood Skewers exist. They can only be made by combining a Hyrule Bass, a Sanke Carp, and two different types of seafood that grant the same effect. These variations are as follows:

* Hearty Copious Seafood Skewers: Made by using any combination of Hearty Salmons, Hearty Blueshell Snails, or Hearty Bass. When eaten, it will fully heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.

* Energizing Copious Seafood Skewers: Made by using Staminoka Basses and Bright-Eyed Crabs. If they're eaten, they refill a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Mighty Copious Seafood Skewers: Made using any combination of Mighty Carp, Mighty Porgies, or Razorclaw Crabs. Eating one will grant Link a temporary boost to his attack power.

* Tough Copious Seafood Skewers: Made using any combination of Armored Carp, Armored Porgies, or Ironshell Crabs. When they're eaten, Link's defense will be temporarily boosted.

* Sneaky Copious Seafood Skewers: Made using Stealthfin Trouts and Sneaky River Snails. Eating them increases Link's Stealth for a short period of time.
