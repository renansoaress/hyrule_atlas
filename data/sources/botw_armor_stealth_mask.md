### Breath of the Wild
The Stealth Mask is a piece of Armor which can be purchased at Enchanted in Kakariko Village. According to its description, the Sheikah crafting techniques used to make the Mask suppress the noisy rustling of clothes. When worn, it provides one level of Stealth Up. The Stealth Mask can be enhanced by a Great Fairy and can be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Stealth Mask, alongside the Stealth Chest Guard and Stealth Tights, compose the Stealth Set. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides the Night Speed Up Set Bonus.
