### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Dragonbone Boko Bows,(Tears of the Kingdom) also stylized as Dragon Bone Boko Bows,(Breath of the Wild) are recurring Items and Objects in série The Legend of Zelda. Dragonbone Boko Bows go unnamed in Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity.

### Breath of the Wild
Dragon Bone Boko Bow
Dragon Bone Boko Bows are Bows used by high-tier Bokoblins on Hyrule Ridge and in Hyrule Field. They are Boko Bows that have been reinforced with fossils that were handpicked by Bokoblins. Because of this, they are known to have a respectable firepower. They belong to the Bokoblin Gear equipment archetype, which is a series of Weapons created by Bokoblins.

Due to being made of wood, Dragon Bone Boko Bows will burn and eventually disintegrate if exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. They will also float if dropped in Water. Unlike metal Weapons, they do not attract Lightning during Thunderstorms, nor do they conduct Electricity.

If scanned with the amiibo Rune, the Breath of the Wild Series Bokoblin amiibo has a chance to spawn a Treasure Chest containing a Dragon Bone Boko Bow, along with other Bokoblin Gear.
