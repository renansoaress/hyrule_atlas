### Breath of the Wild
The Radiant Set is an Armor Set consisting of the Radiant Mask, Radiant Shirt, and Radiant Tights. All three pieces can be bought at the Gerudo Secret Club in Gerudo Town for 2400 Rupees and 9 Luminous Stones in total after "The Secret Club's Secret" Side Quest has been completed. Painted using Hateno Dye made from crushed Luminous Stones, the Radiant Set's skeletal markings glow when worn at night. The Radiant Set can be enhanced by a Great Fairy and can be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.

If all three pieces of the set have been upgraded twice at a Great Fairy Fountain, wearing the set grants a "Disguise" Set Bonus against Stalkoblins, Stalmoblins, and Stalizalfos. The upgraded set also provides a "Bone Atk. Up" bonus, increasing attack power when wielding Weapons made of bone.
