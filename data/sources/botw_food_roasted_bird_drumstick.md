## Location and Uses
Roasted Bird Drumsticks are a type of Roasted Food, and they are the result of Raw Bird Drumsticks that have made contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. Roasted Bird Drumsticks are said to have crisp skin and a simple taste, despite their lack of seasoning. They can also be dropped by Black Hinoxes when they are killed. Link can restore one and a half Hearts to his Life Gauge by eating a Roasted Bird Drumstick.
