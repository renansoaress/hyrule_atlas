## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Serpentine Spear
Serpentine Spears are Weapons used by members of the Sheikah tribe. Spear masters are able to catch opponents with their crescent shape and deliver brutal cuts. These Spears belong to the Sheikah Gear equipment archetype, consisting of all Weapons created and utilized by the Sheikah. They are found in East Necluda, particularly around Kakariko Village, as well as in Hyrule Field.

Since Serpentine Spears are made of metal, they will attract Lightning if they are equipped during Thunderstorms and conduct Electricity. They are also unable to burn if they are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. If they are dropped in the Water, they will sink, but they can be picked up using the Magnesis Rune.

## Trivia
* The repeating black, yellow, and red patten on the Serpentine Spear's staff resembles the coloration of a coral snake.
