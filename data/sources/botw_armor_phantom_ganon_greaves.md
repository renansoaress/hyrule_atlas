### Breath of the Wild
The Phantom Ganon Greaves are a piece of Armor exclusive to  Downloadable Content pack. Obtaining the Phantom Ganon Greaves is one part of the objective of the "EX Treasure: Dark Armor" Side Quest. The Greaves were stolen from Hyrule Castle by Misko and a clue to their location can be found in Misko's EX Journal 2 at the Deya Village Ruins. The metallic Treasure Chest that hides the Phantom Ganon Greaves can be found buried in the ground near a bird statue in Ebara Forest. Link must use the Magnesis Rune to dislodge the Chest from the ground to retrieve the Greaves.

When worn, the Greaves provide one level of Stealth Up. The Phantom Ganon Greaves cannot be enhanced by a Great Fairy nor can they be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Phantom Ganon Greaves, alongside the Phantom Ganon Skull and Phantom Ganon Armor, compose the Phantom Ganon Set.

According to their description, it is believed that the Phantom Ganon Greaves are infused with a dark entity that resembles the Great King of Evil. After obtaining the Phantom Ganon Greaves, Granté in Tarrey Town will begin selling them for 50 Rupees.
