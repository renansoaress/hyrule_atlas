### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowls are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowls are Meals made by Cooking Raw Gourmet Meat, Hylian Rice, and Rock Salt together in a Cooking Pot. They can be eaten to restore several of Link's Hearts. The Meats used in this dish are carefully selected to ensure their quality.

Additional Ingredients can be used when making Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowls, creating several different versions of the Meal. These versions are:

* Chilly Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding cold Ingredients. Eating them will grant Link a low-level of Heat Resistance.

* Electro Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding electric Ingredients. If they are eaten, they grant link a low-level Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding enduring Ingredients. They both refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel when consumed.

* Energizing Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding energizing Ingredients. If eaten, they will replenish a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding hasty Ingredients. When eaten, they will grant Link a boost to his movement speed for a short time.

* Hearty Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding hearty Ingredients. Eating them will heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.

* Mighty Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding mighty Ingredients. Eating this Meal will temporarily boost the power of Link's attacks.

* Sneaky Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding sneaky Ingredients. When eaten, they increase Link's Stealth temporarily.

* Spicy Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding spicy Ingredients. They grant Link low-level Cold Resistance when eaten.

* Tough Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding tough Ingredients. They increase Link's defensive abilities if they are eaten.
