Gourmet Meat Stews are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Gourmet Meat Stews are Meals made by Cooking either Raw Gourmet Meat or a Raw Whole Bird, Tabantha Wheat, Fresh Milk, and Goat Butter together in a Cooking Pot. When eaten, they heal several of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge. The Meats in these Stews have simmered for so long that it melts in one's mouth, and they are considered a bucket-list Meal.

By adding a fifth Ingredient, several different variations of Gourmet Meat Stew can be made:

* Chilly Gourmet Meat Stew: Made by adding cold Ingredients. Eating these will increase Link's Heat Resistance by one level.

* Electro Gourmet Meat Stew: Made by adding electric Ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Gourmet Meat Stew: Made by adding enduring Ingredients. These meals completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel when consumed.

* Energizing Gourmet Meat Stew: Made by adding energizing Ingredients. If eaten, a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel will be replenished.

* Hasty Gourmet Meat Stew: Made by adding hasty Ingredients. When eaten, Link's movement speed will gain a temporary boost.

* Hearty Gourmet Meat Stew: Made by adding hearty Ingredients. Eating these stews will heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.

* Mighty Gourmet Meat Stew: Made by adding mighty Ingredients. If they are eaten, Link's attack power will be temporarily boosted.

* Sneaky Gourmet Meat Stew: Made by adding sneaky Ingredients. When they are eaten, Link's Stealth will be temporarily boosted.

* Spicy Gourmet Meat Stew: Made by adding spicy Ingredients. Link gains one temporary level of Cold Resistance when he eats these Stews.

* Tough Gourmet Meat Stew: Made by adding tough Ingredients. Link's Defense will increase if he eats this Stew.
