## Location and Uses
The Old Shirt is a heavily-worn piece of Armor that was made long before Link's awakening from the Slumber of Restoration. Due to its short sleeves and lower hem, it does not appear to have been made for Link at his age. The Old Shirt provides no additional benefits from wearing it other than marginally boosting Link's defense. It can be obtained either from a stone Treasure Chest inside the Shrine of Resurrection or from Granté in Tarrey Town after the "From the Ground Up" Side Quest is completed.
