### Breath of the Wild
Tingle's Shirt can be found as part of the Side Quest "EX Treasure: Fairy Clothes" exclusive to  Downloadable Content Pack. It can be found inside a Hidden Chest buried in the ruins of the Castle Town Prison, which can be lifted from the ground with Magnesis. After obtaining it, Granté will begin to sell Tingle's Shirt in Tarrey Town. It's part of Tingle's Outfit along with Tingle's Hood and Tingle's Tights. Tingle's Shirt offers no additional effects on its own or as part of a set. It cannot be enhanced by a Great Fairy, nor can it be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.
