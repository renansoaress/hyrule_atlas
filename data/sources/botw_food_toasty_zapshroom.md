Toasty Zapshrooms are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Toasty Zapshrooms are a variety of Roasted Food. Link can make Toasty Zapshrooms by burning Zapshrooms with Fire or a Scorching Climate. A completely roasted Toasty Zapshroom is considered to be delicious. When eaten, they restore three-quarters of a Heart to Link's Life Gauge.
