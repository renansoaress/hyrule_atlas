### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Igneo Pebblits are recurring Enemies in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Igneo Pebblit
Igneo Pebblits can be found in many areas in Eldin, often near Lava. They are fairly easy to spot at night, as they glow brightly, seemingly from the veins of Lava that run through their bodies. Link will be burned if he comes into contact with one unprotected.

The main attack of Igneo Pebblits is moving toward Link and swiping at him with an arm. They can be defeated by throwing Remote Bombs, cooling them off with an Ice-based Weapon such as Ice Arrows to allow them to be safely touched, or wearing a full set of Fireproof Armor that has been enhanced twice by a Great Fairy for the Fireproof Set Bonus. Igneo Pebblits explode if Link holds them for too long or if they are Thrown.

When defeated, they may drop Amber, Flint, or a Ruby.
