### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Lightscale Tridents are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.(BotW, TotK, AoC, AoI)

### Breath of the Wild
Lightscale Trident
Lightscale Tridents are a variant of the Zora Spear. They belong to the Zora Gear equipment archetype, which is a group of Weapons created by the Zora.

A Lightscale Trident was given to the Zora princess Mipha on the day she was born. Even though Mipha preferred to use her healing abilities, it is said that she was talented with this Spear as well. When she came of age, Seggin was the one who taught her to fight with her Trident. King Dorephan later had the story of the Lightscale Trident written on the History of the Zora, Part Four, one of the Stone Monuments found throughout the Lanayru Great Spring.

When Mipha did not return after the Great Calamity, the people of Zora's Domain mourned her. They tried to send the Lightscale Trident down Zora River in an attempt to put her Spirit to rest. However, the Trident began to glow, and Mipha's voice was heard. She told the Zora that she was one with the Lightscale Trident, and they should forget their grief and focus on remembering her. As a result, the Zora celebrate the Champion Festival and honor the Lightscale Trident on the anniversary of the Great Calamity. However, if Link speaks with Trello while the Shrine Quest "The Ceremonial Song" is active, he will admit that the Champion Festival uses a replica of the Lightscale Trident instead of the real one. This replica, the Ceremonial Trident, is nearly identical to the Lightscale Trident, but it is not as strong or durable.

Link can obtain Mipha's Lightscale Trident after taming Divine Beast Vah Ruta and saving Zora's Domain from flooding. King Dorephan will gift the Trident to him as a reward and symbol of their friendship. After the cutscene, Link can collect the Lightscale Trident from inside a Stone Chest in the Throne Room. If the Lightscale Trident breaks, Dento will remake it if Link approaches him at the Hammerhead. To do so, he requires a Zora Spear, a Diamond, and five pieces of Flint. If the Lightscale Trident is not in Link's possession but still exists elsewhere, Dento will be aware of its presence and instead recommend that Link locates it.

The Lightscale Trident is made of metal, and therefore it can conduct Electricity and attract Lightning if it is equipped during Thunderstorms. It will also sink if it is dropped in Water, but it can be picked up using the Magnesis Rune on the Sheikah Slate. Finally, it will not burn if it is exposed to a Fire or a Scorching Climate.
