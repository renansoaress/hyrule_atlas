### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Steamed Meats are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Steamed Meats can be Cooked by combining a piece of Meat with any Vegetable, herb, or Flower, with the exception of Fortified Pumpkins, in a Cooking Pot. To make the default version, Hyrule Herbs must be used, as they do not provide any Added Effects. Eating these Meals will heal several of Link's Hearts. The method of preparation used on these Meats preserves their moisture.

Depending on the Vegetables, herbs, or Flowers used, as well as the presence of extra Ingredients, Link can make the following variations of Steamed Meat:

* Hearty Steamed Meat: Made by using Hearty Radishes, Big Hearty Radishes, or other hearty Ingredients. When eaten, they restore all of Link's Hearts and grant him Extra Hearts.

* Energizing Steamed Meat: Made by using energizing Ingredients. They can be eaten to replenish a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Enduring Steamed Meat: Made by using Endura Carrots or other enduring Ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely restored and overfilled when he eats them.

* Hasty Steamed Meat: Made by using Swift Carrots, Swift Violets, or other hasty Ingredients. By eating them, Link's movement speed will be increased.

* Spicy Steamed Meat: Made by using Warm Safflinas or other spicy Ingredients. They'll grant Link one level of Cold Resistance if they're eaten.

* Electro Steamed Meat: Made by using Electric Safflinas or other electric Ingredients. If consumed, Link will gain one level of Shock Resistance.

* Mighty Steamed Meat: Made by using Mighty Thistles or other mighty Ingredients. Eating them will provide Link with a boost to his attack power.

* Tough Steamed Meat: Made by using Armoranths or other tough Ingredients. Link can eat these meats to receive a boost to his defense.

* Sneaky Steamed Meat: Made by using Blue Nightshades, Silent Princesses, or other sneaky Ingredients. By eating them, Link's Stealth will be increased.

* Chilly Steamed Meat: Made by using Cool Safflinas or other chilly Ingredients. These meats will grant Link one level of Heat Resistance when he eats them.
