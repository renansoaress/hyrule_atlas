### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Dusk Bows,(Tears of the Kingdom) also known as Twilight Bows,(Breath of the Wild) are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
The Twilight Bow is a rare Bow that was once used by Princess Zelda in Twilight Princess. It can be rarely acquired from a Treasure Chest that spawns after scanning the Super Smash Bros. series Princess Zelda amiibo with the amiibo Rune, though only after completing at least one of the Divine Beasts. It is said to contain the power of the Spirits of Light.

Like the Bow of Light from Twilight Princess, it fires Light Arrows. The Twilight Bow shares many characteristics with the Bow of Light; they both fire Light Arrows resembling those seen in Twilight Princess, and both have the greatest range of any Bow. However, unlike the Bow of Light, the Twilight Bow has a base attack power of 30 and durability of 100.
