### Breath of the Wild
Razorclaw Crab
Razorclaw Crabs can be found near Water, usually along beaches in the Necluda Sea and East Necluda. They will scurry away if approached, but they can be easily caught by Link when snuck up on. Eating one raw will restore one of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge. They can also be exposed to Fire to make Blackened Crab or chilled by Ice to make Frozen Crab. Meals made in a Cooking Pot using Razorclaw Crabs will temporarily increase Link's attack damage via the Attack Up Added Effect.

When Cooking, either a Razorclaw Crab or any other Crab is required to make the following Meals:

- Crab Omelet with Rice | Hylian Rice, Bird Egg, Rock Salt, Any Crab
- Crab Risotto | Hylian Rice, Goat Butter, Rock Salt, Any Crab
- Crab Stir-Fry | Goron Spice, Any Crab
- Salt-Grilled Crab | Rock Salt, Any Crab
- Seafood Skewer | Any Crab or Snail

Any Crab, a Sneaky River Snail, or a Hearty Blueshell Snail is needed to make the following Meal:

- Seafood Skewer | Any Crab or Snail

Razorclaw Crabs also count as Seafood when making the following Meals:

- Creamy Seafood Soup | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Fish Pie | Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, Rock Salt, Any Seafood
- Glazed Seafood | Courser Bee Honey, Any Seafood
- Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Gourmet Meat, Any Seafood
- Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Meat, Any Seafood
- Monster Stew | Any Meat, Any Seafood, Monster Extract
- Pepper Seafood | Spicy Pepper, Any Seafood
- Prime Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Prime Meat, Any Seafood
- Seafood Meunière | Any Seafood (except Porgy or Salmon), Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter
- Seafood Rice Balls | Hylian Rice, Any Seafood
- Copious Seafood Skewers | Any Seafood (4 different kinds)
- Steamed Fish | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable,, Herb, or Flower
