### Breath of the Wild
The Knight's Bridle is a type of Bridle that can be obtained by Jini at the Mounted Archery Camp. It is given when Link breaks 20-22 targets during the Horseback Archery Mini-Game. Before the Great Calamity, the Knights of Hyrule gave these Bridles to their Horses. Despite claiming that they provide additional armor to a Horse's head and neck, it is purely cosmetic.

The Knight's Bridle belong to the Knight's Gear set of Horse Gear. Workers at Highland Stable, South Akkala Stable, Outskirt Stable, and Woodland Stable are able to equip Link's Horses with the Knight's Bridle. However, they are unable to be placed on Epona or the Giant Horse.
