### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Prime Spiced Meat Skewers are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Prime Spiced Meat Skewers are made by Cooking Raw Prime Meat and Goron Spice together in a Cooking Pot. By eating them, Link can have some of his Hearts restored in his Life Gauge. Though the preparation of these Skewers is simple, they are known to have a complex taste profile.

Link can add more Ingredients when making Prime Spiced Meat Skewers, which can result in the following variations:

* Chilly Prime Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding chilly Ingredients. Link will gain one level of Heat Resistance when he eats these skewers.

* Electro Prime Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding electric Ingredients. If they are eaten, Link will gain one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Prime Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding enduring Ingredients. Eating these skewers will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Prime Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding energizing Ingredients. They can be eaten to refill a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Prime Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding hasty Ingredients. Link can eat them to have his movement speed increased.

* Hearty Prime Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding hearty Ingredients. When eaten, they will heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.

* Mighty Prime Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding mighty Ingredients. Consuming them will grant Link a boost to the power of his attacks.

* Sneaky Prime Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding sneaky Ingredients. By eating them, Link's Stealth abilities will receive a boost.

* Spicy Prime Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding spicy Ingredients. They grant Link one level of Cold Resistance when he eats them.

* Tough Prime Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding tough Ingredients. When these skewers are eaten, Link will gain a boost to his Defense.
