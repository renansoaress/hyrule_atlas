### Breath of the Wild
Blue Moblin
In Breath of the Wild, Blue Moblins are found all over Hyrule, being a stronger variation of the regular Moblin. They are known by their tall and heavyweight stature, and they carry stronger Moblin Gear and other Weapons than their weaker counterparts. When defeated, Blue Moblins drop Moblin Horns, Moblin Fangs, and Moblin Guts.
