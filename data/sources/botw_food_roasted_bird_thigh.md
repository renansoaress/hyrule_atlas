## Location and Uses
Roasted Bird Thighs are a type of Roasted Food, and they are the result of Raw Bird Thighs that have made contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. Black Hinoxes can also drop them upon their deaths. Roasted Bird Thighs are said to have crisp skin and juicy meat inside. Link can restore two and a quarter Hearts to his Life Gauge by eating a Roasted Bird Thigh.
