## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Spiked Moblin Spear
Spiked Moblin Spears are Weapons carried by Blue Moblins, primarily within the Gerudo Highlands and Akkala Highlands. They are made from Moblin Spears by strapping horned Animal skulls to their ends. As is common for Moblin crafts, they are crudely made. Since they are made by Moblins, they belong to the Moblin Gear equipment archetype.

Being made of wood, Spiked Moblin Spears will float on Water if they are dropped. They will also burn if they are exposed to Fire or equipped in a Scorching Climate. They do not conduct Electricity or attract Lightning during a Thunderstorm, and the Magnesis Rune is unable to pick them up.
