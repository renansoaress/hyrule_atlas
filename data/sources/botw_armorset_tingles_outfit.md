### Breath of the Wild
Tingle's Outfit is exclusive to  Downloadable Content pack. It can be found by using Magnesis to pull three Hidden Chests from the ground in various spots in Hyrule Field. Tingle's Hood is located in the Exchange Ruins, Tingle's Shirt is located in the Castle Town Prison on Hyrule Castle West, and Tingle's Tights can be found in the Mabe Village Ruins. Like the Dark Series, Phantom Ganon Set, Phantom Equipment, and Radiant Set, wearing it results in other characters being scared of Link.
