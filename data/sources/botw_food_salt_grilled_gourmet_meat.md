### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meats are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meats are made by Cooking either Raw Gourmet Meat or a Raw Whole Bird together with Rock Salt in a Cooking Pot. Link can eat them to heal some of his Hearts to his Life Gauge. These Meals are considered to be lavish, making using of high-quality Meats.

Link can add extra ingredients while Cooking Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat, which can result in the following variations:

* Hearty Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat: Made by adding hearty ingredients. They can be eaten to heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.

* Energizing Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat: Made by adding energizing ingredients. When consumed, they'll replenish part of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Enduring Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat: Made by adding enduring ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely restored and overfilled when he eats these meats.

* Hasty Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat: Made by adding hasty ingredients. These meals will grant Link a boost to his movement speed when he eats them.

* Spicy Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat: Made by adding spicy ingredients. By eating them, Link will gain one level of Cold Resistance.

* Electro Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat: Made by adding electric ingredients. If they're eaten, Link will receive one level of Shock Resistance.

* Mighty Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat: Made by adding mighty ingredients. By eating them, the power of Link's attacks will be boosted.

* Tough Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat: Made by adding tough ingredients. They can be eaten to increase Link's defensive abilities.

* Sneaky Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat: Made by adding sneaky ingredients. Link's Stealth will be boosted when he eats these meals.

* Chilly Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat: Made by adding chilly ingredients. When eaten, they will grant Link one level of Heat Resistance.
