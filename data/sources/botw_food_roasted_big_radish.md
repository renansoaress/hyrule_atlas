### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Roasted Big Radishes are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Big Radishes are a type of Roasted Food, resulting from Big Hearty Radishes that have made contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. Some consider the making of Roasted Big Radishes to be indecent, as there are better ways to Cook such precious vegetables. Link can heal six Hearts by eating a Roasted Big Radish.
