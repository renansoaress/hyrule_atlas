Soldier's Broadswords are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Soldier's Broadsword
Soldier's Broadswords are Weapons that Link can use to defeat Enemies, destroy Barrels and Jars, and cut down Trees. Soldier's Broadswords will usually deal 14, but that increases to 28 damage when they are thrown and broken. They were once brandished by those who once defended Hyrule Castle prior to the Great Calamity. They are considered a good and durable Weapon to use against Monsters. Link can find them commonly in Hyrule Field and the Faron Grasslands. A Soldier's Broadsword can be found planted in the ground of the cave located behind the Waterfall that feeds the River of the Dead.

Scanning the following amiibo with the amiibo Rune has a chance to spawn a Treasure Chest containing a Soldier's Broadsword:
* Super Smash Bros. Series Link amiibo
* Super Smash Bros. Series Young Link amiibo
* Super Smash Bros. Series Toon Link amiibo
* The Legend of Zelda Series Link (The Legend of Zelda) amiibo
* The Legend of Zelda Series Link (Link's Awakening) amiibo
* The Legend of Zelda Series Toon Link (The Wind Waker) amiibo
* The Legend of Zelda Series Link (Twilight Princess) amiibo
* The Legend of Zelda Series Link (Skyward Sword) amiibo
* Breath of the Wild Series Link (Rider) amiibo
* Breath of the Wild Series Urbosa amiibo

Soldier's Broadswords are made of metal, and they will attract Lightning if they are equipped during a Thunderstorm. This also means that they will conduct Electricity. They will sink if they are dropped in Water, but they can be picked up and manipulated with the Magnesis Rune. Finally, Soldier's Broadswords will not burn near Fire or in a Scorching Climate.

Soldier's Broadswords belong to the Soldier's Gear equipment archetype, which were all utilized by the Hylian Knights prior to the Great Calamity. A Soldier's Broadsword is part of an unnamed set of equipment used by Link in promotional media, alongside a Traveler's Bow and a Traveler's Shield.
