### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Wheat Breads are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.(BotW, TotK, AoC)

### Breath of the Wild
Link can make Wheat Breads by Cooking Tabantha Wheat and Rock Salt together in a Cooking Pot. Eating them will restore some of Link's Hearts. The scent of these soft, springy breads is known to be heavenly.

Adding extra Ingredients to Wheat Bread can result in the following variations:

* Hearty Wheat Bread: Made by adding hearty Ingredients. If eaten, they heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him Extra Hearts.

* Energizing Wheat Bread: Made by using energizing Ingredients. Consuming them will partially refill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Enduring Wheat Bread: Made by using enduring Ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely restored and overfilled when he eats these breads.

* Hasty Wheat Bread: Made by using hasty Ingredients. When eaten, Link will receive a boost to his movement speed.

* Spicy Wheat Bread: Made by using spicy Ingredients. They can be eaten to grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.

* Electro Wheat Bread: Made by using electric Ingredients. One level of Shock Resistance will be given to Link when he eats them.

* Mighty Wheat Bread: Made by using mighty Ingredients. By eating these breads, Link's attack power will be boosted.

* Tough Wheat Bread: Made by using tough Ingredients. If consumed, they increase Link's defensive abilities.

* Sneaky Wheat Bread: Made by using sneaky Ingredients. Link's Stealth will receive a boost if he eats them.

* Chilly Wheat Bread: Made by using chilly Ingredients. Eating these breads will grant Link one level of Heat Resistance.
