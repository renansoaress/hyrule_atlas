### Breath of the Wild
The Snow Boots grant the Snow Speed Up Added Effect, allowing Link to travel more quickly on Snow. They can be worn in Gerudo Town along with the Gerudo Veil and Gerudo Top.

To obtain them, Link must wear the Gerudo Outfit and talk to Bozai outside Gerudo Town. By accepting The Eighth Heroine Side Quest, he will temporarily be given the Snow Boots. Once Link has completed the Side Quest, he will return the Snow Boots in exchange for the Sand Boots. Afterwards, Link can obtain the Snow Boots permanently by talking to Bozai again and completing The Forgotten Sword Side Quest. Once they are acquired, Granté will begin to sell the Snow Boots in Tarrey Town.

Snow Boots can be both upgraded at a Great Fairy Fountain and Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.
