### Breath of the Wild
Snowquill Tunics are pieces of Armor that can be bought for 600 Rupees at the Brazen Beak. When worn, a Snowquill Tunic provides one level of Cold Resistance. Theu can be enhanced by a Great Fairy and Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. Snowquill Tunics, alongside Snowquill Headdresses and Snowquill Trousers, compose the Snowquill Set. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides the Unfreezable Set Bonus.
