### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Frozen Hearty Salmon are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Frozen Hearty Salmon are made by exposing Hearty Salmons to Ice. They restore four of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Heat Resistance. Due to the removal of the excess fat, these Salmon do not taste as fishy as they used to.
