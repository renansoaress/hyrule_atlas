### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Meat and Rice Bowls are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Meat and Rice Bowls are Meals that can be made by Cooking Raw Meat, Hylian Rice, and Rock Salt together in a Cooking Pot. If eaten, they will replenish several of Link's Hearts. Composed of rice and lightly seared Meat, these dishes are considered a mainstay throughout Hyrule.

Additional Ingredients can be used when making Meat and Rice Bowls, which can result in the following variations:

* Chilly Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding cold Ingredients. They can be eaten to increase Link's Heat Resistance by one level.

* Electro Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding electric Ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding enduring Ingredients. These bowls completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding energizing Ingredients. When eaten, they refill part of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding hasty Ingredients. Eating these Meals will temporarily increase Link's speed.

* Hearty Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding hearty Ingredients. Eating these will heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.

* Mighty Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding mighty Ingredients. When eaten, Link will receive a boost to the power of his attacks.

* Sneaky Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding sneaky Ingredients. If eaten, Link's Stealth will be temporarily increased.

* Spicy Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding spicy Ingredients. Link will gain one level of Cold Resistance if he eats these dishes.

* Tough Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding tough Ingredients. They will increase Link's Defense when eaten.
