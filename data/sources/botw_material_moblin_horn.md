### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Moblin Horns are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Moblin Horns are dropped by Moblins and Stalmoblins upon defeat. They are useful as Ingredients in creating various Elixirs. They can be used at the Kochi Dye Shop to make Gray Dye as well as sold to Kilton in exchange for Mon.

The Fireproof Armor set requires Moblin Horns, in addition to a Fireproof Lizard, to upgrade each piece to its first tier at a Great Fairy Fountain.
