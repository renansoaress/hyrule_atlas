### Breath of the Wild
Islander Hawk
Islander Hawks are carnivorous Birds that can be found flying in Hyrule Field and Hyrule Ridge. Their diet consists of smaller Creatures and other Birds, which they search for while flying in the sky. Like most Birds, they can be hunted by Link for Food, dropping Raw Bird Thighs when killed.
