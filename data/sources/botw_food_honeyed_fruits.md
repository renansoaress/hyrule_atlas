### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Honeyed Fruits are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Honeyed Fruits are dishes made by Cooking Courser Bee Honey with any Fruit, aside from Apples, in a Cooking Pot. The regular version can be made by using Fruits with an effect that counteracts that of the Courser Bee Honey. When eaten, they will restore some Hearts to Link's Life Gauge. This Meal combines the sweetness of Courser Bee Honey with the sourness of Fruits.

Only one variation of Honeyed Fruits can be made:

* Energizing Honeyed Fruits: Made by using neutral Fruits, such as Palm Fruits or Wildberries. Eating them will refill a part of Link's Stamina Wheel.
