### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Stone Taluses (Rare),(BotW, TotK) also known as Rare Stone Taluses,(Tears of the Kingdom) and simply as Stone Taluses,(Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity) are recurring Bosses and Sub-Bosses in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Stone Talus (Rare)
Stone Taluses (Rare) are a variation of Stone Taluses found primarily around Hyrule Field and the Tabantha Frontier. The main difference between them is the Ore Deposit on their body, which has been replaced by a Rare Ore Deposit. As a result of this, they tend to drop rarer loot than other Taluses. Stone Taluses (Rare) disguise themselves as natural rock formations, but if Link approaches them, they will begin to move and attack him. The only way to damage them is to attack the Rare Ore Deposit on their body, which are particularly vulnerable to Iron Sledgehammers, as well as other mining tools and Bomb Arrows.

After defeating Calamity Ganon, Link is able to ask Kilton about Stone Taluses. When he does so, Kilton reveals that he has been unable to approach them due to the giant size, so he requests that Link kill every Stone Talus in Hyrule Kingdom and allow him to analyze the scents they leave behind. This figure includes every Stone Talus (Rare) as well. Should Link accomplish this task, Kilton will reward him with the Medal of Honor: Talus. Kilton's research allows him to learn more about the diet and musculoskeletal structures of Stone Taluses.
