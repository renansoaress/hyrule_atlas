### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Vegetable Risottos are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Vegetable Risottos are made by Cooking Hylian Rice, Goat Butter, Rock Salt, and either a Fortified Pumpkin or any Carrot together in a Cooking Pot. Their default version is made by using Ingredients with contradicting Added Effects. Link can eat them to heal some of his Hearts. Due to this Meal's sweetness, it has a mild flavor.

Based on the type of Vegetable used, Link can make the following variations of Vegetable Risotto:

* Enduring Vegetable Risotto: Made by using Endura Carrots. By eating them, Link can restore and overfill his Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Vegetable Risotto: Made by using Swift Carrots. They can be eaten to grant Link a boost to his movement speed.

* Tough Vegetable Risotto: Made by using Fortified Pumpkins. When eaten, these risottos will increase Link's Defense.
