### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Soldier's Claymores are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Soldier's Claymore
Soldier's Claymores are two-handed swords that were designed to be used in combat. They are heavy, and therefore difficult to use, but have a decent quality and durability. They are commonly used by Enemies in Hyrule Field and the Faron Grasslands, though they can also be found abandoned.

Scanning the following amiibo with the amiibo Rune have a chance to drop a Treasure Chest containing a Soldier's Claymore
* Super Smash Bros. Series Young Link amiibo
* Super Smash Bros. Series Ganondorf amiibo
* série The Legend of Zelda Series Link (Link's Awakening) amiibo
* The Legend of Zelda Series Link (Ocarina of Time) amiibo
* The Legend of Zelda Series Young Link (Majora's Mask) amiibo
* Breath of the Wild Series Daruk amiibo

Due to being made of metal, Soldier's Claymores will conduct Electricity and attract Lightning when equipped during a Thunderstorm. They can also be picked up using the Magnesis Rune, and they will sink if dropped in the Water. If they are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate, they will not burn like a wooden Weapon would.

Soldier's Claymores belong to the Soldier's Gear equipment archetype, which were designed for the soldiers of Hyrule Kingdom.
