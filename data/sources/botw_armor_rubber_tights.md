### Breath of the Wild
The Rubber Tights can be acquired in the Qukah Nata Shrine after completing the Shrine Quest "A Song of Storms" in Calora Lake. When worn, they provide Link with one level of Shock Resistance. They, along with the Rubber Helm and Rubber Armor, compose the Rubber Armor set, which makes Link Unshockable when worn. Rubber Tights can be both enhanced by a Great Fairy and Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.
