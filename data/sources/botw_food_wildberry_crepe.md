### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Wildberry Crepes are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Wildberry Crepes can be made by Cooking a Wildberry, Fresh Milk, Cane Sugar, Tabantha Wheat, and a Bird Egg together in a Cooking Pot. By eating them, some of Link's Hearts will be restored to his Life Gauge. The Wildberries in this Meal are noted to be sweet and tart, while the dough is thin and springy.

Since Wildberry Crepes require five ingredients to be made, all of which lack any Added Effects, no variations can be made.
