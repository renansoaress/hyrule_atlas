Royal Guard Uniforms are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.(BotW, TotK, AoC)

### Breath of the Wild
The Royal Guard Uniform appears exclusively in  Downloadable Content pack. It is a part of the Royal Guard Set, along with the Royal Guard Cap and Royal Guard Boots. Wearing the full Armor Set will decrease the amount of Stamina required for a charge attack. The Uniform available through the "EX Royal Guard Rumors" Side Quest, which requires Link to read the Super Rumor Mill V4 at Riverside Stable. The Book claims that pieces of the Royal Guard Set can be found in the Dining Hall, near the Guards' Chamber, and on the second floor.

The Royal Guard Uniform can be found in a Hidden Chest behind a bombable wall in the Hyrule Castle Corridors. This wall is located near the Dining Hall. After collecting it, Granté will begin to sell the Royal Guard Uniform in Tarrey Town.Prior to the Version 1.3.3 Update, Granté would not sell any Armor that is exclusive to the Breath of the Wild Expansion Pass. This meant that the Royal Guard Uniform could not be replaced if sold. The Royal Guard Uniform cannot be enhanced by a Great Fairy, nor can it be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.
