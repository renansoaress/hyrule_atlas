Roasted Whole Birds are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Whole Birds are a type of Roasted Food, and they can be made by burning a Raw Whole Bird with a Fire or Scorching Climate. They can also be dropped by Black Hinoxes when they are killed. After being roasted, the natural taste of Roasted Whole Birds have been brought out. Eating one restores four and a half Hearts to Link.
