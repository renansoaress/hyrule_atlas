### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Snail Chowders,(Tears of the Kingdom) also known as Clam Chowders,(Breath of the Wild) are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Clam Chowder is a dish made by Cooking Fresh Milk, Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, and a Hearty Blueshell Snail together in a Cooking Pot. Their regular version can only be made by including a fifth ingredient with an effect that contradicts that of the Hearty Blueshell Snail. If eaten, they will heal some of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge. The nutritious Hearty Blueshell Snails combine with the other ingredients to create a rich soup.

There is only one variation of Clam Chowder that can be made:

* Hearty Clam Chowder: Made by using the default recipe. Eating this chowder will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.
