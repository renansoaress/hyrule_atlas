### Breath of the Wild
The Lynel Mask is a piece of Armor that can be purchased from Kilton at the Fang and Bone for 999 Mon shop after freeing three Divine Beasts. It cannot be enhanced by a Great Fairy, nor can it be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. When worn, it grants Link the Disguise Added Effect, allowing him to blend in with Lynels, though they are smart enough to eventually see through his disguise and attack. The Lynel Mask does not prevent other Enemies in the same area from attacking Link. It also makes quiet Lynel sounds as Link moves around. While wearing the Lynel Mask, Link adopts a new idle pose to match that of a Lynel.
