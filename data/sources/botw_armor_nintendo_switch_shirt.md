### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
The  is an Item in Breath of the Wild. It is a bonus piece of Armor exclusive to the Expansion Pass. The Shirt is also available in the Wii U version of the game, also featuring the Nintendo Switch logo.

## Location and Uses
The Nintendo Switch Shirt can be found inside a Treasure Chest located atop a wall in the Eastern Abbey on the Great Plateau. After Link obtains the Shirt, Granté will begin selling it for 50 Rupees.Prior to the versão 1.3.3, Granté would not sell any Armor that is exclusive to the Expansion Pass or The Master Trials DLC Pack. This meant that the Nintendo Switch Shirt could not be replaced if sold, which was noted in the Shirt's description. The description of the Nintendo Switch Shirt was changed in the versão 1.3.3 Update as the Shirt could now be replaced.
