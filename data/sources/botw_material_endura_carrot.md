Endura Carrots are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Endura Carrot
Endura Carrots are found at Malanya Spring, near the peak of Satori Mountain, and at the edges of Great Fairy Fountains. Eating an Endura Carrot will heal Link for two Hearts. Endura Carrots can also be roasted by a Fire to make Roasted Endura Carrots. They can also be Cooked into a Meal using a Cooking Pot to temporarily grant Link Extra Stamina.

Like Apples and Swift Carrots, Endura Carrots can be fed to Horses to increase their Bond. Unlike other Foods that Horses can eat, Endura Carrots will temporarily increase the Horse's number of speed boosts granting them three extra yellow speed boosts which have a carrot icon next to them. After using up normal speed boosts and any granted by the Ancient Bridle, these extra speed boosts can be used. However, they are lost afterwards. Roasted Endura Carrots have the same effect if eaten off the ground by a Horse. If the Giant Horse eats an Endura Carrot, only its Bond will be increased, as it has no speed boosts.

An Endura Carrot is required to revive a Horse via the Horse God, Malanya. If Link decides to give him an Endura Carrot, Malanya will agree to revive one of his Horses. Endura Carrots can also be used at the Kochi Dye Shop as an ingredient for Light Yellow Dye. Endura Carrots are not required to upgrade any pieces of Armor at a Great Fairy Fountain.

Endura Carrots and Swift Carrots can be used interchangeably when Cooking. In some cases, they can also be replaced by Pumpkins. They are required for the following Meals:

- Carrot Cake | Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter, Any Carrot
- Carrot Stew | Any Carrot, Fresh Milk, Goat Butter, Tabantha Wheat
- Vegetable Curry | Any Carrot or Pumpkin, Hylian Rice, Goron Spice
- Vegetable Risotto | Any Carrot or Pumpkin, Hylian Rice, Goat Butter, Rock Salt
- Veggie Cream Soup | Any Carrot or Pumpkin, Fresh Milk, Rock Salt

Like other Vegetables, Endura Carrots can be used in a Cooking Pot to make the following Meals:

- Cream of Mushroom Soup | Any Mushroom, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Cream of Vegetable Soup | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Creamy Meat Soup | Any Meat, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Creamy Seafood Soup | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Fried Wild Greens | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Copious Fried Wild Greens | Any Vegetables, Herbs, or Flowers(4 different kinds)
- Glazed Veggies | Courser Bee Honey, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Herb Sauté | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Goron Spice
- Salt-Grilled Greens | Rock Salt, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Steamed Fish | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable,, Herb, or Flower
- Steamed Fruit | Any Fruit, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Steamed Meat | Any Meat, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Pumpkins)
- Steamed Mushrooms | Any Mushroom, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Vegetable Omelet | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Bird Egg, Goat Butter, Rock Salt
- Veggie Rice Balls | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Hylian Rice
