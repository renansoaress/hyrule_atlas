## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Dragonbone Boko Bat
Dragonbon Boko Bats are Boko Bats that have been reinforced with fossilized bone. As a result, they are stronger and more durable than before. They belong to the Bokoblin Gear equipment archetype, a series of Weapons created and used by Bokoblins. They are often wielded by Black Bokoblins, Silver Bokoblins, and Golden Bokoblins, as well as Black Moblins, Silver Moblins, and Golden Moblins. Dragonbon Boko Bats are common on Hyrule Ridge and along the Necluda Sea. The Breath of the Wild Series Bokoblin amiibo has a chance to spawn a Treasure Chest containing a Dragonbone Boko Bat when it is scanned with the amiibo Rune.

Being made of wood, Dragonbon Boko Bats will burn when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. They will also float when dropped on the Water, and they will not attract Lightning during Thunderstorms or conduct Electricity. They are unable to be picked up with the Magnesis Rune.
