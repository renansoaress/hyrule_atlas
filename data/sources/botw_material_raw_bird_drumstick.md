### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Raw Bird Drumsticks are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Raw Bird Drumsticks are dropped by Wood Pigeons, Mountain Crows, Bright-Chested Ducks, Blue-Winged Herons, Pink Herons, and Seagulls when they are killed. They can also be purchased from the traveling Merchant Mezer. Raw Bird Drumsticks restore one Heart when eaten. They can be exposed to Fire to make Roasted Bird Drumsticks or chilled by Ice to make Frozen Bird Drumsticks.

When making Food in a Cooking Pot, a Raw Bird Drumstick is required to make Poultry Pilaf and Poultry Curry. Either Raw Meat or a Raw Bird Drumstick can be used to make Meat Stew and Salt-Grilled Meat. They are also among the Meats that can be used to make Creamy Meat Soup, Glazed Meat, a Meat and Mushroom Skewer, Meat Pie, a Meat Skewer, Copious Meat Skewers, Meaty Rice Balls, Monster Stew, Pepper Steak, Steamed Meat, and a Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin.
