Goron Spices are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Goron Spice can be purchased from the Goron Gusto Shop in Goron City. Alternatively, it is sold by the Traveling Merchant Yammo near the Tabantha Great Bridge. It is made of several different spices, and the initial wave of spiciness turns into sweetness. While it cannot be eaten, it can be used to make certain Foods at a Cooking Pot. At the Kochi Dye Shop, Goron Spice is one of the ingredients that can be used to make Red Dye.

As part of the "Curry for What Ails You" Side Quest, Link must give a bottle of Goron Spice to Lester at the Rito Stable so he can make Curry Rice. Upon obtaining Goron Spice, Lester will ask Link to trade it for 50 Rupees. Afterwards, Link can trade Goron Spice with him at any time for Hylian Rice.

While Cooking, Goron Spice is required to make the following Meals:

- Crab Stir-Fry | Goron Spice, Any Crab
- Curry Pilaf | Hylian Rice, Bird Egg, Goat Butter, Goron Spice
- Curry Rice | Hylian Rice, Goron Spice
- Fragrant Mushroom Sauté | Goron Spice, Any Mushroom
- Gourmet Meat Curry | Raw Gourmet Meat, Goron Spice, Hylian Rice
- Gourmet Poultry Curry | Raw Whole Bird, Goron Spice, Hylian Rice
- Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer | Raw Gourmet Meat, Goron Spice
- Herb Sauté | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Goron Spice
- Meat Curry | Raw Meat, Hylian Rice, Goron Spice
- Monster Curry | Hylian Rice, Goron Spice, Monster Extract
- Poultry Curry | Raw Bird Drumstick, Hylian Rice, Goron Spice
- Prime Meat Curry | Raw Prime Meat, Hylian Rice, Goron Spice
- Prime Poultry Curry | Raw Bird Thigh, Goron Spice, Hylian Rice
- Prime Spiced Meat Skewer | Raw Prime Meat, Goron Spice
- Seafood Curry | Goron Spice, Hylian Rice, Hearty Blueshell Snail or Any Porgy
- Spiced Meat Skewer | Goron Spice, Raw Meat
- Vegetable Curry | Any Carrot or Pumpkin, Hylian Rice, Goron Spice

## Trivia
* The label on Goron Spice contains Hylian writing that translates to "Spice".
* Goron Spice made an accidental appearance in the novel A Traveler at the Gates of Wisdom by John Boyne due to the top Google search result for dyeing red clothes linking to the Breath of the Wild recipe.
