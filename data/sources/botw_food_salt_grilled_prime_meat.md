### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Salt-Grilled Prime Meats are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Link can make Salt-Grilled Prime Meats by Cooking either Raw Prime Meat or a Raw Bird Thigh with Rock Salt in a Cooking Pot. He can eat these Meals to restore some Hearts. These dishes are known to be simple yet exquisite, due to their use of high-quality Meat.

By adding extra ingredients, Link can create the following variations of Salt-Grilled Prime Meat:

* Hearty Salt-Grilled Prime Meat: Made by adding hearty ingredients. When eaten, they'll heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.

* Energizing Salt-Grilled Prime Meat: Made by adding energizing ingredients. These meals will restore part of Link's Stamina Wheel if they're eaten.

* Enduring Salt-Grilled Prime Meat: Made by adding enduring ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely restored and overfilled when he eats these.

* Hasty Salt-Grilled Prime Meat: Made by adding hasty ingredients. By eating these meats, Link's movement speed will be increased.

* Spicy Salt-Grilled Prime Meat: Made by adding spicy ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.

* Electro Salt-Grilled Prime Meat: Made by adding electric ingredients. They can be eaten for Link to gain one level of Shock Resistance.

* Mighty Salt-Grilled Prime Meat: Made by adding mighty ingredients. When eaten, they will provide a boost to Link's attack power.

* Tough Salt-Grilled Prime Meat: Made by adding tough ingredients. These meats will grant Link a boost to his defensive abilities when eaten.

* Sneaky Salt-Grilled Prime Meat: Made by adding sneaky ingredients. Link will receive a boost to his Stealth abilities when he eats these.

* Chilly Salt-Grilled Prime Meat: Made by adding chilly ingredients. By eating them, Link will gain one level of Heat Resistance.
