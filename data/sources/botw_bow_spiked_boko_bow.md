### Breath of the Wild
Spiked Boko Bow
Spiked Boko Bows are Bows used by Blue Bokoblins in the Faron Grasslands and East Necluda. They are Boko Bows that have been upgraded with Animal bone to increase their Durability and strength. Though they are sloppily made, they are light and easy to use. These Bows belong to the Bokoblin Gear equipment archetype, which includes all Weapons created by Bokoblins.

Being made of wood, Spiked Boko Bows will burn if they touch Fire or if they are equipped in a Scorching Climate. They also float if dropped in Water, and they are incompatible with the Magnesis Rune. Spiked Boko Bows do not attract Lightning or Electricity like metal Weapons do during Thunderstorms.

If scanned with the amiibo Rune, the Breath of the Wild Series Bokoblin amiibo has a chance to spawn a Treasure Chest containing a Spiked Boko Bow.
