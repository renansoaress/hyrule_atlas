Pink Herons are recurring Creatures in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Pink Heron
Pink Herons are a large variety of Heron with long legs and an elongated neck, colored predominately white with pink wings and crest. Meanwhile, their legs and beak are black. They often appear in flocks of two or more, strutting slowly about. If Link approaches them, they will fly away. Unlike the Blue-Winged Heron, these Birds inhabit the grassy and arid Regions of Hyrule Ridge and Hyrule Field, where they hunt Insects.

When they are killed, Pink Herons will drop Raw Bird Drumsticks.
