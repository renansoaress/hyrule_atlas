## Location and Uses
Naboris's Emblems are exclusive to the "EX Champion Urbosa's Song" Main Quest from  DLC Pack. They can be obtained by completing Keive Tala Shrine, Takama Shiri Shrine, and Kihiro Moh Shrine and speaking to their respective Monks. They are commemorative symbols of one's completion of a lightning trial.

At the end of the final Ancient Shrine Link completes, the Monk will tell him to go to Divine Beast Vah Naboris. When he arrives, Link will hear the voice of a Monk. In exchange for Naboris's Emblems, he will be given a chance to fight a manifestation of his fear in a realm of memories, the Illusory Realm. He will then find himself inside Vah Naboris and will face off against Thunderblight Ganon once more. After completing the trial, Link can fight Thunderblight Ganon at any time with no cost.
