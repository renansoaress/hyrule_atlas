### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Prime Poultry Curries are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Prime Poultry Curry is made by Cooking a Raw Bird Thigh, Goron Spice, and Hylian Rice together in a Cooking Pot. Eating them will heal some of Link's Hearts. The flavor of these curries comes from adding the spices after they have been taken off the heat.

Link can use additional Ingredients when making these curries, which can result in the following variations:

* Chilly Prime Poultry Curry: Made by adding chilly Ingredients. These curries will grant Link one level of Heat Resistance when eaten.

* Electro Prime Poultry Curry: Made by adding electric Ingredients. If eaten, Link will gain one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Prime Poultry Curry: Made by adding enduring Ingredients. Consuming them will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Prime Poultry Curry: Made by adding energizing Ingredients. Link can eat these curries to have his Stamina Wheel partially refilled.

* Hasty Prime Poultry Curry: Made by adding hasty Ingredients. They can be eaten to increase Link's movement speed.

* Hearty Prime Poultry Curry: Made by adding hearty Ingredients. When eaten, they heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him Extra Hearts.

* Mighty Prime Poultry Curry: Made by adding mighty Ingredients. When eaten, they will grant Link a boost to the power of his attacks.

* Sneaky Prime Poultry Curry: Made by adding sneaky Ingredients. Eating these will increase Link's Stealth abilities.

* Spicy Prime Poultry Curry: Made by adding spicy Ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.

* Tough Prime Poultry Curry: Made by adding tough Ingredients. Link will gain a boost to his Defense by eating these curries.
