### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Tingle's Tights, also known as the Tingle Tights, are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Tingle's Tights are only available when  Downloadable Content pack is active. After beginning the "EX Treasure: Fairy Clothes" Side Quest, Tingle's Tights can be found in the Mabe Village Ruins. They are part of Tingle's Outfit, along with Tingle's Shirt and Tingle's Hood. Wearing the full Armor Set will give Link the Night Speed Up Set Bonus.
