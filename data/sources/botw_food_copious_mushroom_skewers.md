### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Copious Mushroom Skewers are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Copious Mushroom Skewers can be made by Cooking at least four different types of Mushrooms together in a Cooking Pot. Due to the variety of Added Effects granted by Mushrooms, it is impossible to create any different types of Copious Mushroom Skewers aside from their default. These Skewers are popular among fans of Mushrooms.
