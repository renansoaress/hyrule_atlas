Bright-Eyed Crabs are recurring Creatures and Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Bright-Eyed Crab
Bright-Eyed Crabs can be found on freshwater shores in the Lanayru Great Spring and Eldin Canyon. Many of them live in the area surrounding Ne'ez Yohma Shrine in Zora's Domain. They are also sold by the traveling Merchants Chumin and Bugut.

Eating a Bright-Eyed Crab raw will restore one of Link's Hearts. They can also be roasted by a Fire to make Blackened Crab or chilled by Ice to make Frozen Crab. Cooking one in a Cooking Pot will result in a meal that provides Stamina Restoration.

Bright-Eyed Crabs can be used to make the following Meals:

- Crab Omelet with Rice | Hylian Rice, Bird Egg, Rock Salt, Any Crab
- Crab Risotto | Hylian Rice, Goat Butter, Rock Salt, Any Crab
- Crab Stir-Fry | Goron Spice, Any Crab
- Salt-Grilled Crab | Rock Salt, Any Crab
- Seafood Skewer | Any Crab or Snail

Bright-Eyed Crabs count as Seafood when making the following Meals:

- Creamy Seafood Soup | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Fish Pie | Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, Rock Salt, Any Seafood
- Glazed Seafood | Courser Bee Honey, Any Seafood
- Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Gourmet Meat, Any Seafood
- Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Meat, Any Seafood
- Monster Stew | Any Meat, Any Seafood, Monster Extract
- Pepper Seafood | Spicy Pepper, Any Seafood
- Prime Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Prime Meat, Any Seafood
- Seafood Meunière | Any Seafood (except Porgy or Salmon), Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter
- Seafood Rice Balls | Hylian Rice, Any Seafood
- Copious Seafood Skewers | Any Seafood (4 different kinds)
- Steamed Fish | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable,, Herb, or Flower

## Trivia
* According to Beedle, Bright-Eyed Crabs can be Cooked along with Monster Parts to create an Elixir that recovers Stamina. However, this is incorrect, as Bright-Eyed Crabs do not count as "critters" and will produce Dubious Food when combined with Monster Parts.
