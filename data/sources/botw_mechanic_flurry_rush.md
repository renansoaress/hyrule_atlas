### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Flurry Rushes are recurring abilities in série The Legend of Zelda.(BotW, TotK, AoC)

### Breath of the Wild
Link can perform a Flurry Rush in Breath of the Wild after he successfully performs a Perfect Dodge. According to "Daruk's Training Journal", Flurry Rush is a result of Link's intense focus making it seem as if Time has slowed down from his perspective. While focusing, Link is capable of performing multiple hits in quick succession with his Weapon in a short period of time. If Link does not hit the Enemy within a certain period of time after initiating the Flurry Rush, it will cancel, and the flow of time will normalize.
