### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Campfire Eggs are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Campfire Eggs are a type of Roasted Food, and they are the result of Bird Eggs that have made contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. The roasting process is said to retain water within the Egg's shell, which causes a chewy consistency. Link can restore one and a half Hearts to his Life Gauge by eating a Campfire Egg.
