### Breath of the Wild
Soup Ladle

Soup Ladles are a wooden kitchen implement used for serving soup. They can be found in Hyrule Field and on the Tabantha Frontier. One in particular can be found by the Cooking Pot in the Shared Cooking Space of Hateno Village. As they are made from the wood of sturdy Trees, Link can use Soup Ladles to inflict a low amount of damage to Enemies.

Due to their wooden composition, Soup Ladles will burn if they are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. They will not attract Lightning during Thunderstorms, and they do not conduct Electricity. If they are dropped in Water, they will float on its surface.

Soup Ladles belong to the Daily Use Goods equipment archetype, which consists of Weapons that double as tools used in the daily lives of Hyrule residents.
