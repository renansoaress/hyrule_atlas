Fresh Milks are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Fresh Milks originate from Hateno Pasture in Hateno Village, and they can be subsequently purchased from the town's General Store, East Wind.  They can also be purchased from the traveling Merchants Agus and Yammo. When drunk, Fresh Milk will restore half of a Heart to Link's Life Gauge. At the Kochi Dye Shop, Fresh Milk can be used as an ingredient to make White Dye.

At a Cooking Pot, Fresh Milk is required to cook the following Meals:

- Carrot Stew | Any Carrot, Fresh Milk, Goat Butter, Tabantha Wheat
- Clam Chowder | Fresh Milk, Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, Hearty Blueshell Snail
- Cream of Mushroom Soup | Any Mushroom, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Cream of Vegetable Soup | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Creamy Heart Soup | Hydromelon, Voltfruit, Any Radish, Fresh Milk
- Creamy Meat Soup | Any Meat, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Creamy Seafood Soup | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Egg Pudding | Fresh Milk, Bird Egg, Cane Sugar
- Gourmet Meat Stew | Any Gourmet Meat, Tabantha Wheat, Fresh Milk, Goat Butter
- Honey Crepe | Fresh Milk, Bird Egg, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Courser Bee Honey
- Meat Stew | Raw Meat or Raw Bird Drumstick, Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, Fresh Milk
- Monster Soup | Monster Extract, Tabantha Wheat, Fresh Milk, Goat Butter
- Plain Crepe | Fresh Milk, Cane Sugar, Tabantha Wheat, Bird Egg, Goat Butter
- Prime Meat Stew | Any Prime Meat, Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, Fresh Milk
- Pumpkin Stew | Fortified Pumpkin, Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, Fresh Milk
- Veggie Cream Soup | Any Carrot or Pumpkin, Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Warm Milk | Fresh Milk
- Wildberry Crepe | Wildberry, Fresh Milk, Cane Sugar, Tabantha Wheat, Bird Egg
