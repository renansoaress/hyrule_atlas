### Breath of the Wild
The Rubber Armor can be acquired in the Toh Yahsa Shrine after completing the "Trial of Thunder" Shrine Quest on the Thundra Plateau. It provides Link with three Defense and one level of Shock Resistance. It can be enhanced by a Great Fairy and Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Rubber Armor, alongside the Rubber Helm and Rubber Tights, compose the Armor Set of the same name, the Rubber Armor. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides the Unshockable Set Bonus.
