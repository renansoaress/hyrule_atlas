## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Dragonbone Moblin Club
Dragonbone Moblin Clubs are two-handed Weapons wielded by Enemies such as Black Moblins, Silver Moblins, Golden Moblins, and Stalmoblins. They are Moblin Clubs that have had ancient bones attached to them the make them more damaging in battle. They are commonly found in the Hebra Mountains and Eldin Mountains. Dragonbone Moblin Clubs belong to the Moblin Gear equipment archetype, which is a series of Weapons crafted by Moblins.

Dragonbone Moblin Clubs are not made out of metal, therefore equipping one will not attract Lightning during Thunderstorms or conduct Electricity. They are made of wood, however, and will consequently catch Fire when exposed to open flame or equipped in the Scorching Climate of Death Mountain. They will also float if they are dropped in Water, but the are unable to be picked up by the Magnesis Rune.
