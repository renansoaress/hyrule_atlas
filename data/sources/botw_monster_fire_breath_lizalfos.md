### Breath of the Wild
Fire-Breath Lizalfos
Fire-Breath Lizalfos are one of the elemental variants of the Lizalfos race. They are visually distinct from other types of Lizalfos due to their red and orange coloration. They most commonly reside in the Gerudo Desert and Eldin Canyon, though they have also been spotted in certain areas of Akkala. Like other Lizalfos, Fire-Breath Lizalfos are also capable of disguising themselves to blend in with their surroundings.

In addition to the agility typical of Lizalfos, Fire-Breath Lizalfos can breathe a stream of Fire. Hitting them with Ice-based attack, however, will result in them being instantly defeated. If they are defeated, Fire-Breath Lizalfos will drop Lizalfos Talons, Lizalfos Horns, and Red Lizalfos Tails.
