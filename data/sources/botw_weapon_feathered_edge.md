### Breath of the Wild
Feathered Edge
Feathered Edges are lightweight, double-edged swords forged by Rito craftsmen, designed so that Rito warriors can soar into battle unaffected by their weight. They belong to the Rito Gear equipment archetype, a grouping of Weapons with Rito origins.

Feathered Edges are primarily found on the Tabantha Frontier. A Feathered Edge can be found inside Treasure Chests in Rota Ooh Shrine and at Dragon Bone Mire. In addition, two Blue Lizalfos on the Tabantha Frontier equip a Feathered Edge. One can be found just south of Kolami Bridge at a Monster Stronghold, while the other is hiding in a path north of Kolami Bridge. The Lizalfos and the swords reappear every Blood Moon. In Master Mode, another Treasure Chest can be found containing a Feathered Edge at the Waterfall in Hebra Plunge.

Being made of metal, Feathered Edges will attract Lightning if they are equipped during Thunderstorms, and they are capable of conducting Electricity. They will not burn if they are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate, but they will sink if they are dropped in Water. They can be picked up and manipulated using the Magnesis Rune on the Sheikah Slate.
