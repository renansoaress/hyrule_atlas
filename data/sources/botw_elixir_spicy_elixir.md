Spicy Elixirs are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Drinking Spicy Elixirs will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance by warming his body to the core. They're useful in cold environments and snow-covered mountains, such as Mount Lanayru, the Hebra Mountains, and the Gerudo Highlands.

Spicy Elixirs can be made by Cooking any of the following critters with Monster Parts in a Cooking Pot:

* Summerwing Butterfly

* Warm Darner

By adding any of these spicy ingredients, Link can make a Spicy Elixir that will also heal him:

* Spicy Pepper

* Sunshroom

* Warm Safflina

* Sizzlefin Trout
