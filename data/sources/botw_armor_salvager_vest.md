## Location and Uses
The Salvager Vest is a piece of Armor in Breath of the Wild, part of the Salvager Armor Set. It is obtained from a Treasure Chest as part of the "[Xenoblade Chronicles 2]" Side Quest. The Chest crashes on the Akkala Wilds after Link looks to the eastern sky from the left eye of Skull Lake during the night. After obtaining the Salvager Vest, Granté will begin selling it for 50 Rupees. When worn, the Salvager Vest provides one level of Swim Speed Up. The Salvager Vest cannot be Enhanced by a Great Fairy, nor can it be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.
