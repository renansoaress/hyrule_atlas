### Breath of the Wild
The Phantom Helmet is a piece of Armor exclusive to The Master Trials DLC Pack. Obtaining it is one part of the objective of the "EX Treasure: Phantasma" Side Quest. The Helmet was stolen from Hyrule Castle by Misko and a clue to its location can be found in Misko's EX Journal at the Outpost Ruins. The Treasure Chest that hides the Phantom Helmet can be found buried near a wall on the first floor of the Coliseum Ruins. After obtaining the Phantom Helmet, Granté will begin selling it for 50 Rupees.Prior to the versão 1.3.3, Granté would not sell any Armor that is exclusive to the Expansion Pass or The Master Trials DLC Pack. This meant that the Phantom Helmet could not be replaced if sold, which was noted in the description of the Helmet. The Phantom Helmet's description was changed in the versão 1.3.3 Update as the Helmet could now be replaced. According to its description, the Phantom Helmet looks just like the Helmets supposedly worn by Phantoms that tormented heroes. When worn, the Helmet provides one level of Attack Up. The Phantom Helmet cannot be enhanced by a Great Fairy nor can it be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Phantom Helmet, alongside the Phantom Armor and Phantom Greaves, compose the Phantom Equipment.
