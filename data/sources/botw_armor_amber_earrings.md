### Breath of the Wild
Amber Earrings are a type of Accessory, specifically Earrings that can be clipped onto Link's ears. They can be purchased at the Starlight Memories Jewelry Shop in Gerudo Town for 100 Rupees and ten pieces of Amber. The Amber in the Earrings harnesses the land's power, giving them increased defense compared to most other Armor.

Amber Earrings can be enhanced by a Great Fairy at a Great Fairy Fountain. However, they cannot be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Earrings have no other use than to increase Link's defense.
