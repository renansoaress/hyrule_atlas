### Breath of the Wild
Mountain Goat
Mountain Goats are common Creatures that primarily inhabit open fields in hilly and mountainous areas, such as Hyrule Ridge and the Great Hyrule Forest. They are herbivores, and their strong hooves are able to easily climb over rocks. Despite this, Mountain Goats are not fast, making them prime targets for new hunters.

When Mountain Goats spot either Link or Monsters nearby, they will flee. If approached from the front, they may attempt to charge Link down. They only attempt to do so once, and they will flee afterwards regardless of whether they successfully damaged Link or not. When killed, Mountain Goats drop Raw Meat.
