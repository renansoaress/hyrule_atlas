Fragrant Mushroom Sautés are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Fragrant Mushroom Sautés are a meal that can be made by Cooking Goron Spice with Mushrooms together in a Cooking Pot. Regular Fragrant Mushroom Sautés can be made by using a Hylian Shroom in the recipe or by using multiple mushrooms with differing effects. Eating them will heal some of Link's missing Hearts. The aroma of these dishes make mouths water.

Fragrant Mushroom Sautés are favored by Nellie, a resident of Gerudo Town, who makes it whenever she is feeling tired.

Based on the types of Mushrooms used, several different variations of Fragrant Mushroom Sauté exist:

* Hearty Fragrant Mushroom Sauté: Made using Hearty Truffles or Big Hearty Truffles. Eating them will heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum hearts.

* Energizing Fragrant Mushroom Sauté: Made using Stamella Shrooms. When eaten, they will refill a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Enduring Fragrant Mushroom Sauté: Made using Endura Shrooms. They completely refill Link's Stamina Wheel when consumed.

* Hasty Fragrant Mushroom Sauté: Made using Rushrooms. If they are eaten, they will temporarily boost Link's movement speed.

* Spicy Fragrant Mushroom Sauté: Made using Sunshrooms. Consuming one will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance for a short period of time.

* Electro Fragrant Mushroom Sauté: Made using Zapshrooms. Eating them gives Link one level of Shock Resistance.

* Mighty Fragrant Mushroom Sauté: Made using Razorshrooms. When eaten, they will temporarily increase the damage of Link's attacks.

* Tough Fragrant Mushroom Sauté: Made using Ironshrooms. If they are eaten, Link's defense will be boosted for a short period of time.

* Sneaky Fragrant Mushroom Sauté: Made using Silent Shrooms. Eating one increases Link's Stealth for a time.

* Chilly Fragrant Mushroom Sauté: Made using Chillshrooms. When consumed, Link will temporarily gain one level of Heat Resistance.
