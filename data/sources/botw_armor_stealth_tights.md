Stealth Tights are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.(BotW, TotK, AoC) Stealth Tights go unnamed in Super Smash Bros. Ultimate.

### Breath of the Wild
The Stealth Tights are a piece of Armor which can be purchased at Enchanted in Kakariko Village. According to their description, the noise-suppressing cloth used to make the Tights are a hallmark of ancient Sheikah technology. When worn, they provide one level of Stealth Up. The Stealth Tights can be enhanced by a Great Fairy and can be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Stealth Tights, alongside the Stealth Mask and Stealth Chest Guard, compose the Stealth Set. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides the Night Speed Up Set Bonus.
