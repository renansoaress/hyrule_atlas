### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Majora's Mask is a recurring Item, Object, and Boss in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
In Breath of the Wild, Majora's Mask appears as Armor in  Downloadable Content Pack. Obtaining Majora's Mask is the objective of the Side Quest "EX Treasure: Ancient Mask". Majora's Mask is mentioned in Misko's EX Journal in the Outpost Ruins, while the Mask itself can be found in a Hidden Chest at the Kolomo Garrison Ruins. After Link obtains the Mask, Granté will begin selling it for 50 Rupees.Prior to the Version 1.3.3 Update, Granté would not sell any Armor that is exclusive to the Expansion Pass or  Downloadable Content Pack. This meant that Majora's Mask could not be replaced if sold, which was noted in the description of the Item. The description of Majora's Mask was changed in the Version 1.3.3 Update as it could now be replaced. The Mask only offers one defense, but it provides Link with the Disguise Added Effect, allowing him to approach multiple Enemies, including Bokoblins, Moblins, and Lizalfos without provoking them to attack. Lynels are also affected in this manner, but they will attack after a short amount of time.
