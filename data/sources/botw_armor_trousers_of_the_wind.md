### Breath of the Wild
The Trousers of the Wind are a piece of Armor which can be obtained using the amiibo Rune while scanning the Super Smash Bros. series Toon Link amiibo or The Legend of Zelda 30th Anniversary series Toon Link amiibo. Each time the figure is scanned, a Metal Chest will appear that has a small chance to contain the Trousers of the Wind. It can be enhanced by a Great Fairy but cannot be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.

The Trousers of the Wind, alongside the Cap of the Wind and Tunic of the Wind, comprise the Hero of Winds Set which resembles Link's outfit from The Wind Waker. When it has been enhanced by two levels, it provides the Master Sword Beam Up Set Bonus when worn with the full set.
