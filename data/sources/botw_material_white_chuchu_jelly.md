### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
White Chuchu Jellies are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
White Chuchu Jellies can be obtained by killing Ice Chuchus, or by killing regular Chuchus with Ice Weapons. Theu can also be made by freezing regular Chuchu Jellies, which can be done with an Ice Rod or Blizzard Rod or by dropping it into Cold Water. They are cool to the touch and can be squeezed to relieve stress. If hit by a Weapon, the Jelly will burst and release a cloud of Ice that can freeze nearby Enemies.

Elixirs can be made with White Chuchu Jellies by Cooking them with critters in a Cooking Pot. The Kochi Dye Shop accepts White Chuchu Jellies to make White Dye, while Kilton at the Fang and Bone can exchange them for Mon.

White Chuchu Jelly is used to upgrade the Desert Voe Set of Armor to its first and second tiers at a Great Fairy Fountain.
