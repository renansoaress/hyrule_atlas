### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Daybreakers are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.(BotW, TotK) Daybreakers go unnamed in Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity.

### Breath of the Wild
Daybreaker
Daybreakers are the most durable and protective variant of the Gerudo Shield, adorned with a jeweled Crest of the Gerudo. Paired with Scimitars of the Seven, they make the Champion's Arms. They were designed to be both lightweight and durable, and the gold used to make them is handpicked to ensure this. They belong to the Gerudo Gear equipment archetype.

Before the Great Calamity, a Daybreaker was beloved by the Gerudo Champion Urbosa. Both it and her Scimiat of the Seven were made by a Gerudo jeweler. During the EX Recovered Memory "Champion Urbosa's Song", Urbosa can be seen performing a Parry with her Daybreaker. After Urbosa's death, her Daybreaker and Scimitar became treasures of the Gerudo. After defeating Thunderblight Ganon and calming Divine Beast Vah Naboris, Riju will offer Link both pieces. She believes it is right he should have them, as he was friends with Urbosa. Both Items can be found in Stone Chests behind Riju's Throne in the Royal Palace of Gerudo Town. If either Item breaks, Buliara will offer to remake them for Link. To create a Daybreaker, she needs one Diamond, five pieces of Flint, and a Gerudo Shield.

Selmie will take note of a Daybreaker if Link has one equipped when he speaks to her. She will ask if she is dreaming, explaining that she has wanted a Daybreaker so badly that she has seen it in her dreams.

Being made of metal, Daybreakers will conduct Electricity and attract Lightning if they are equipped during a Thunderstorm. They will also sink if they are dropped in Water, but they can still be picked up and manipulated with the Magnesis Rune if it happens. In addition, they will not burn like wooden Shields when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate.
