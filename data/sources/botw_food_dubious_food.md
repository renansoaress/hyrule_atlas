### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Dubious Food is a recurring Meal in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Dubious Food can be made from several different Cooking combinations:

* Food with Monster Parts or critters, but not both

* Monster Parts on their own

* Critters on their own

* Any recipe that would produce an Elixir without an effect (e.g. Monster Part + Chilly critter + Spicy critter)

* Seasonings without Food

* Hylian Rice and Nuts

Dubious Food is noted to be too gross to look at and emits a bizarre smell. However, it can restore a random amount of Link's Hearts depending on the ingredients used.

Moza, who can be found near Ishto Soh Shrine, will give Link three recipes, two of which result in Dubious Food. One is her ancient Meat dish, which involves Cooking Meat with an Ancient Material from a Guardian. When Link calls this gross, she will scold him, saying the only way to Cook Meat is something with history. She asks Link how else she is supposed to make her ancient meat dish. However, Moza admits to Link that her only results have been Dubious Food.

For her other dish, the ultimate survival dish, Moza tells Link to defeat Monsters and to put their parts in a Cooking Pot. This time, if Link calls her recipe gross, she will insist that one has to be willing to eat anything to survive in the wild. Though she always ends up with Dubious Food, she asserts she will not achieve her goal unless she makes some sacrifices. She also admits to Link that she is unsure what is so dubious about using Monster Parts.
