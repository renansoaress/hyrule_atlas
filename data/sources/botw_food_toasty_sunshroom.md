### Breath of the Wild
Toasty Sunshrooms are a type of Roasted Food, and they can be made by exposing Sunshrooms to Fire or a Scorching Climate. It is considered a good idea to keep at least one Toasty Sunshroom on hand for emergencies. They restore three-quarters of a Heart to Link when eaten.
