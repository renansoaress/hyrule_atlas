### Breath of the Wild
Hearty Bass
Hearty Bass are a type of Fish that can be found in the Waters of West Necluda and the Akkala Highlands. Schools of Hearty Bass can be found in ponds located on Firly Plateau and Mount Rhoam. Their large bodies are able to store a lot of nutrients. Exposing Hyrule Basses to Fire results in Roasted Hearty Basses, while chilling it with Ice results in Frozen Hearty Basses. They can be eaten raw to heal two Hearts. Meals made using Hearty Bass will grant Link Extra Hearts when eaten.

Hearty Bass are among the Fish that can be used to Cook the following Meals:

- Fish Skewer | Any Fish
- Fish and Mushroom Skewer | Any Fish, Any Mushroom
- Salt-Grilled Fish | Rock Salt, Any Fish or Snail

Hearty Bass are also counted as Seafood that can be used to make the following Meals:

- Creamy Seafood Soup | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Fish Pie | Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, Rock Salt, Any Seafood
- Glazed Seafood | Courser Bee Honey, Any Seafood
- Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Gourmet Meat, Any Seafood
- Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Meat, Any Seafood
- Monster Stew | Any Meat, Any Seafood, Monster Extract
- Pepper Seafood | Spicy Pepper, Any Seafood
- Prime Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Prime Meat, Any Seafood
- Seafood Meunière | Any Seafood (except Porgy or Salmon), Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter
- Seafood Rice Balls | Hylian Rice, Any Seafood
- Copious Seafood Skewers | Any Seafood (4 different kinds)
- Steamed Fish | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable,, Herb, or Flower

In order to upgrade the Zora Set to its third tier at a Great Fairy Fountain, Link needs to collect five Hearty Bass for each piece.
