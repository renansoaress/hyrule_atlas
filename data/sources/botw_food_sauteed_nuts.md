### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Sautéed Nuts are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Link can make Sautéed Nuts by Cooking at least one Nut in a Cooking Pot. This can be either an Acorn or a Chickaloo Tree Nut. Eating these Meals will restore some of Link's Hearts. They are considered to be the perfect snack for busy adventurers.

No variations of Sautéed Nuts can be made.
