## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Guardian Sword++
Guardian Swords++ are the final class of Guardian Swords, surpassing Guardian Swords+ in both strength and durability. They are larger than both previous iterations and are said to easily slice through Armor. Guardian Scouts IV use them in "A Major Test of Strength" Ancient Shrines, and they can be obtained after their defeat. They also appear in Treasure Chests that spawn after scanning the Breath of the Wild Series Guardian amiibo with the amiibo Rune.

Since they are made from Ancient Technology, Guardian Swords++ will deal additional damage if they are used to attack Guardians. They will not burn if they are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate, they do not conduct Electricity or attract Lightning during Thunderstorms, and they will float if they are dropped in the Water. Finally, they can not be picked up with the Magnesis Rune.

Guardian Swords++ belong to the Guardian Gear equipment archetype, which includes all Weapons used by Guardian Scouts.
