### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Honey Candies are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Energizing Honey Candies are made by Cooking at least one Courser Bee Honey in a Cooking Pot. Since they are composed entirely of Courser Bee Honey, there is no default version of Honey Candies, as all results grant the energizing effect. Honey Candies can be sold for 20 to 140 Rupees, depending on how many Courser Bee Honeys were used. When eaten, Energizing Honey Candies will instantly refill a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel, as well as replenish two to 18 Hearts in his Life Gauge.
