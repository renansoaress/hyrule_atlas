### Breath of the Wild
The Radiant Shirt is a piece of Armor which can be purchased at the Gerudo Secret Club in Gerudo Town after "The Secret Club's Secret" Side Quest has been completed. Fashioned using Hateno Dye made from Luminous Stones, the Radiant Shirt's skeletal markings glow when worn at night. The Radiant Shirt can be enhanced by a Great Fairy and can be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Radiant Shirt, alongside the Radiant Mask and Radiant Tights, compose the Radiant Set.

When each piece of the Radiant Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides a "Disguise" and "Bone Atk. Up" Set Bonus. Similar to disguise masks sold by Kilton at the Fang and Bone, the Radiant Set allows Link to blend in with Stalkoblins, Stalmoblins, and Stalizalfos. In addition, the Radiant Set causes attacks using Bone Weapons (such as the Dragonbone Boko Club) to deal an increased amount of damage.
