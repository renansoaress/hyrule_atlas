### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Prime Meat and Rice Bowls are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Link can make a Prime Meat and Rice Bowl by Cooking Raw Prime Meat, Hylian Rice, and Rock Salt together in a Cooking Pot. When eaten, they will restore some of Link's Hearts. Since these bowls contain high-quality Meat, they are considered to be a serious Meal.

Extra Ingredients can be added to Prime Meat and Rice Bowls, which can result in the following variations:

* Chilly Prime Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding chilly Ingredients. They will grant Link one level of Heat Resistance when eaten.

* Electro Prime Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding electric Ingredients. Link will gain one level of Shock Resistance by eating these bowls.

* Enduring Prime Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding enduring Ingredients. Consuming them will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Prime Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding energizing Ingredients. Link can refill part of his Stamina Wheel by eating these.

* Hasty Prime Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding hasty Ingredients. These bowls can be eaten to increase Link's movement speed.

* Hearty Prime Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding hearty Ingredients. Eating these will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him Extra Hearts.

* Mighty Prime Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding mighty Ingredients. When eaten, Link will receive a boost to the power of his attacks.

* Sneaky Prime Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding sneaky Ingredients. Eating them will increase Link's Stealth abilities.

* Spicy Prime Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding spicy Ingredients. They grant Link one level of Cold Resistance when they're eaten.

* Tough Prime Meat and Rice Bowl: Made by adding tough Ingredients. If Link eats them, he will gain a boost to his Defense.
