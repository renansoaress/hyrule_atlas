### Breath of the Wild
The Tunic of Time is a piece of Armor which can be obtained using the amiibo Rune while scanning The Legend of Zelda 30th Anniversary Ocarina of Time Link Amiibo. Each time the figure is scanned, a Treasure Chest will appear that has a small chance to contain the Tunic of Time. It can be enhanced by a Great Fairy but cannot be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.

The Tunic of Time, alongside the Cap of Time and Trousers of Time, comprise the Hero of Time Set which resembles Link's outfit from Ocarina of Time. When it has been enhanced by two levels, it provides the Master Sword Beam Up Set Bonus when worn with the full set.
