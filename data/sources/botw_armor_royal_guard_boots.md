### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Royal Guard Boots are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
The Royal Guard Boots can be found as part of the "EX Royal Guard Rumors" Side Quest exclusive to  Downloadable Content Pack. As part of this Quest, Link is required to read the Super Rumor Mill V4 at Riverside Stable. In this Book, Traysi claims that pieces of the Royal Guard Set can be found in the Dining Hall, near the Guards' Chamber, and on the second floor.

They Royal Guard Boots are located in an EX Treasure Chest in the Dining Hall of Hyrule Castle. After they have been found, Granté will begin to sell them in Tarrey Town.Prior to the Version 1.3.3 Update, Granté would not sell any Armor that is exclusive to the Breath of the Wild Expansion Pass. This meant that the Royal Guard Boots could not be replaced if sold. They Royal Guard Boots are part of the Royal Guard Set, along with the Royal Guard Cap and Royal Guard Uniform. Wearing the whole set will decrease the amount of Stamina required to perform a Charge Attack. The Royal Guard Boots cannot be enhanced by a Great Fairy, nor can they be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.
