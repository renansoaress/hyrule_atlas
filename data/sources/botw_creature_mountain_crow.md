### Breath of the Wild
Mountain Crow
Mountain Crows are a species of Bird that can be found on Hyrule Ridge and in the Great Hyrule Forest. Despite their name, Mountain Crows, can also be found in forests and villagers. They are noted for their advanced intelligence and distinct claws. They are often hated due to their feathers being seen as ill omens, and they are known to steal crops from farmers. If Mountain Crows are killed, they drop a Raw Bird Drumstick.
