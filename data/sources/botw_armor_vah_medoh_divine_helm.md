### Breath of the Wild
The Vah Medoh Divine Helm is obtained by scanning the Revali amiibo with the amiibo Rune. When worn, the Vah Medoh Divine Helm provides both a basic level of Guardian Resist Up and Cold Resistance, and also allows Link to view an enemy's remaining health via the See Enemy HP effect. When paired with the Ancient Cuirass and the Ancient Greaves and each piece of Armor has been enhanced at least twice by a Great Fairy, the three pieces of Armor provide Ancient Proficiency, a Set Bonus that increases the damage dealt from the Ancient and Guardian archetypes of weapons.

The Vah Medoh Divine Helm is based on the head portion of Divine Beast Vah Medoh, which Revali piloted as the Rito Champion.
