### Breath of the Wild
Silver Lynel
Silver Lynels are a variant of Lynel under the influence of Calamity Ganon's dark magic, resulting in their status as the strongest of the Lynels. They are also known to be rare, and they stand out because of their purple stripes. Like White-Maned Lynels, Silver Lynels carry around the "Savage" set of Lynel Gear, though their Weapons tend to possess various bonuses. As Lynels, they are resistant to elemental attacks.

Silver Lynels will often refrain from attacking for a short period of time after spotting Link, instead opting to stare at him. Using the Lynel Mask or Majora's Mask, Link can temporarily disguise himself, though they will eventually see through it and attack. They start their battles by roaring loudly and slamming their Weapons to the ground, which causes an explosion of Fire. When defeated, Silver Lynels drop Lynel Horns, Lynel Hooves, Lynel Guts, Topazes, Rubies, Sapphires, Diamonds, and Star Fragments.
