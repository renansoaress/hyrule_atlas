### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Copious Fried Wild Greens are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Copious Fried Wild Greens are made by Cooking four different Vegetables, herbs, or Flowers together in a Cooking Pot. When eaten, they will restore several Hearts to Link's Life Gauge. They are known to be healthy Meals made by cooking mixed greens over a strong flame.

Due to the variety and number of ingredients needed to make Copious Fried Wild Greens, only their default version is available. It is impossible to add any effects to this dish.
