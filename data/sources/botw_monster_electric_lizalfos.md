### Breath of the Wild
Electric Lizalfos
Electric Lizalfos are an the elemental variety of Lizalfos that can emit strong Electrical currents from their bodies. As a result of this, they can create arcs of Electricity that can cause Link to drop metal Weapons, Bows, or Shields. Electric Lizalfos typically reside in deserts, such as the Gerudo Desert. Striking their horns with an Arrow causes an Electric discharge. Like all Lizalfos, Electric Lizalfos will not attack Link if he is wearing the Lizalfos Mask. Defeated Electric Lizalfos will drop Lizalfos Horns, Lizalfos Talons, and Yellow Lizalfos Tails.
