### Breath of the Wild
The Soldier's Set is an Armor Set consisting of the Soldier's Helm, Soldier's Armor, and Soldier's Greaves. All three pieces are sold at Ventest Clothing Boutique in Hateno Village. As the standard armor of Hyrule's Soldiers, it has much higher defense than other Armor pieces, but it does not feature any Set Bonuses.

Certain Hylians wear parts of the Soldier's Set, such as the Helm being worn by Hoz and Savelle. Nell also wears the Soldier's Armor.
