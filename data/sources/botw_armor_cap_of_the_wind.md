### Breath of the Wild
The Cap of the Wind is a piece of Armor which can be obtained using the amiibo Rune while scanning the Super Smash Bros. series Toon Link amiibo or The Legend of Zelda 30th Anniversary series Toon Link amiibo. Each time the figure is scanned, a Metal Chest will appear that has a small chance to contain the Cap of the Wind. It can be enhanced by a Great Fairy but cannot be Dyes at the Kochi Dye Shop.

The Cap of the Wind, alongside the Tunic of the Wind and Trousers of the Wind, comprise the Hero of Winds Set which resembles Link's outfit from The Wind Waker. When it has been enhanced by two levels, it provides the Master Sword Beam Up Set Bonus when worn with the full set.
