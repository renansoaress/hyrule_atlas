Roasted Wildberries are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Wildberries are a variety of Roasted Food, and they are created when Wildberries come into contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. The sweet taste of Wildberries is altered when they are roasted. Roasted Wildberries restore three-quarters of a Heart to Link when eaten.
