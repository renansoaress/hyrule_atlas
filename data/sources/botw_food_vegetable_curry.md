### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Vegetable Curries are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Vegetable Curries are made by Cooking Hylian Rice, Goron Spice, and either a Fortified Pumpkin, Swift Carrot, or Endura Carrot together in a Cooking Pot. Their default version can be made by using multiple Vegetables with differing effects. Eating them will heal some of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge. These Curries are healthy, and their mild flavor and spiciness make them popular.

Based on the type of Vegetable used, Link can make the following variations of Vegetable Curry:

* Enduring Vegetable Curry: Made using Endura Carrots. When eaten, these curries will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Vegetable Curry: Made using Swift Carrots. By eating them, Link will receive a boost to his movement speed.

* Tough Vegetable Curry: Made using Fortified Pumpkins. They can be eaten to grant Link a boost to his defense.
