### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Fire-Breath Lizalfos Tails, also known as Red Lizalfos Tails, are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Red Lizalfos Tails are Monster Parts that are dropped by Fire-Breath Lizalfos upon their deaths. Fire-Breath Lizalfos can be found in the Gerudo Desert and the region of Eldin. Their flavor makes them unsuitable for consumption, but they can be combined with critters in a Cooking Pot to make Elixirs. Kilton at the Fang and Bone accepts Red Lizalfos Tails in exchange for Mon, but they can also be used at the Kochi Dye Shop to make Red Dye.

Ten Red Lizalfos Tails, as well as five Rubies, are needed to upgrade each piece of the Snowquill Set to their final tiers at a Great Fairy Fountain.
