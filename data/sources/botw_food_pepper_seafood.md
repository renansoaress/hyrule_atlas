### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Pepper Seafoods are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Link can make Pepper Seafoods by Cooking Spicy Peppers with any type of seafood in a Cooking Pot. The default variation can only be made by using seafood that has contradictory effects to Spicy Peppers. When eaten, they will heal some of Link's Hearts. The seeds from the Peppers draw out the taste of this dish, in addition to providing a pleasant aroma.

Only one variation of Pepper Seafoods can be made:

* Spicy Pepper Seafood: Made by using Hyrule Basses, Sanke Carp, or Sizzlefin Trouts. Eating these meals will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.
