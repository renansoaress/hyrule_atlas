Octorok Tentacles are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Octorok Tentacles are dropped by all types of Octoroks when defeated. Like other Monster Parts, they cannot be used for Cooking, but can still be combined with critters in a Cooking Pot to make Elixirs. They can also be exchanged for Mon at the Fang and Bone or used for Light Blue Dye at the Kochi Dye Shop.
