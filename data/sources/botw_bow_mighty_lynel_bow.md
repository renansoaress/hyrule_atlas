### Breath of the Wild
Mighty Lynel Bow
Mighty Lynel Bows are Bows wielded by Blue-Maned Lynels within Hyrule. In Master Mode, though, White-Maned Lynels use Mighty Lynel Bows instead of their Blue-Maned counterparts. They will become rare as Enemies tier up, but the Blue-Maned Lynel in the First Gatehouse of Hyrule Castle will always remain the same. Therefore, it can become the only source of Mighty Lynel Bows later in the game. Mighty Lynel Bows are a type of Lynel Gear, a series of Weapons that are created by Lynels.

The bowstrings of Mighty Lynel Bows are noted to be made of a metal so tough that regular Hylians have trouble drawing them. When they are drawn, however, they can fire three arrows at once. Since they are made of metal, they conduct Electricity and will attract Lightning if equipped during Thunderstorms. Mighty Lynel Bows also sink if they are dropped in water, but can be picked up and manipulated with the Magnesis Rune. Finally, they do not burn if when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate like wooden Weapons do.
