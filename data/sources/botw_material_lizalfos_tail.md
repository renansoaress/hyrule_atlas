### Breath of the Wild
In Breath of the Wild, Lizalfos Tails are Materials that can be dropped by Blue Lizalfos and Black Lizalfos. They are highly valued as ingredients in Elixirs due to their ability to continue wriggling after being severed from their body. Lizalfos Tails can be exchanged for 12 Mon at the Fang and Bone or sold for 28 Rupees. They can also be used to make Green Dye at the Kochi Dye Shop.

Great Fairies can use Lizalfos Tails to enhance the Soldier's Set and Zora Set.
