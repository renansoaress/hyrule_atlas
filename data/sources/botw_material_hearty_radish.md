### Breath of the Wild
Hearty Radish
Hearty Radishes are found in the ground in Hyrule Ridge and East Necluda, their location indicated by their stalk. Hearty Radishes are also sold by Beedle at the East Akkala Stable and the General Shoppe. Hearty Radishes can be eaten raw to restore two and a half Hearts. If a Hearty Radish is roasted over a Campfire, it becomes a Roasted Radish. If a Hearty Radish is Cooked in a Cooking Pot, the resulting Food will grant Link Extra Hearts, which temporarily increases Link's maximum Hearts. At the Kochi Dye Shop, Hearty Radishes are one of the ingredients that can be used to make Peach Hateno Dye. Hearty Radishes are not required to upgrade any pieces of Armor at a Great Fairy Fountain.

Making Creamy Heart Soup in a Cooking Pot requires Link to use either a Hearty Radish or a Big Hearty Radish:

- Creamy Heart Soup | Hydromelon, Voltfruit, Any Radish, Fresh Milk

They can also be used to make the following Meals:
