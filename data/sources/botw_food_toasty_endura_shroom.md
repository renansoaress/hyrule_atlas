Toasty Endura Shrooms are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Toasty Endura Shrooms are a type of Roasted Food, and they can be made by burning Endura Shrooms with Fire or a Scorching Climate. While they do not grant special effects, they are still considered to be delicious. When eaten, Toasty Endura Shrooms restore one and a half Hearts to Link.
