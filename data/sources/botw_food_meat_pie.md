### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Meat Pies are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Meat Pies are meals made by Cooking any Meat, Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, and Rock Salt together in a Cooking Pot. The resulting dish, when eaten, will restore some Hearts to Link's Life Gauge. These pies are juicy and contain perfectly baked minced Meat.

By adding other ingredients, several different variations of Meat Pie can be made:

* Chilly Meat Pie: Made by adding chilly ingredients. If eaten, they grant Link one level of Heat Resistance.

* Electro Meat Pie: Made by adding electric ingredients. Link gains one level of Shock Resistance when he eats them.

* Enduring Meat Pie: Made by adding enduring ingredients. If they are eaten, they will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Meat Pie: Made by adding energizing ingredients. When eaten, they will refill part of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Meat Pie: Made by adding hasty ingredients. They can be consumed to provide a boost to Link's movement speed.

* Hearty Meat Pie: Made by adding hearty ingredients. Eating these pies will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.

* Mighty Meat Pie: Made by adding mighty ingredients. Eating them temporarily boosts the power of Link's attacks.

* Sneaky Meat Pie: Made by adding sneaky ingredients. Link can eat these pies to gain a boost to his Stealth.

* Spicy Meat Pie: Made by adding spicy ingredients. When eaten, these pies grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.

* Tough Meat Pie: Made by adding tough ingredients. When eaten, Link's defense will be temporarily increased.
