### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Zora Bows,(Tears of the Kingdom) also known as Silver Bows,(Breath of the Wild) are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Silver Bow
Silver Bows are Bows that were originally made for fishing, and because of this, they do not bear high attack power. However, they do have a higher durability due to the special metal they're crafted from. They belong to the Zora Gear equipment archetype, which includes Weapons created by the Zora. Commonly, they are found in the Lanayru Great Spring, particularly around Zora's Domain, as well as in the Akkala Highlands. Many Lizalfos in these areas utilize them.

Being made of metal, Silver Bows will conduct Electricity and attract Lightning if they are equipped during a Thunderstorm. They will also sink if they are dropped in Water, but they can be picked up with the Magnesis Rune on the Sheikah Slate. Finally, Silver Bows will not burn if they are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate.
