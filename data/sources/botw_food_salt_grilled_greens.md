### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Salt-Grilled Greens are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Salt-Grilled Greens are Meals made by Cooking any Vegetable, herb, or Flower with Rock Salt in a Cooking Pot. Their default version can be made by using Hyrule Herbs, which offer no status effects. Eating these Meals will heal some of Link's Hearts. Salt-Grilled Greens are known to be beneficial to one's health, being made with leafy greens and a touch of salt.

Based on the type of greens used, Link can make the following variations of Salt Grilled Greens:

* Hearty Salt-Grilled Greens: Made by using Hearty Radishes, Big Hearty Radishes, or other hearty Ingredients. When eaten, all of Link's Hearts will be healed and he will gain Extra Hearts.

* Energizing Salt-Grilled Greens: Made by using energizing Ingredients. By eating these greens, part of Link's Stamina Wheel will be refilled.

* Enduring Salt-Grilled Greens: Made by using Endura Carrots or other enduring Ingredients. These will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Salt-Grilled Greens: Made by using Swift Carrots, Swift Violets, or other hasty Ingredients. Link can eat them to receive a boost to his movement speed.

* Spicy Salt-Grilled Greens: Made by using Warm Safflinas or other spicy Ingredients. If eaten, Link will gain one level of Cold Resistance.

* Electro Salt-Grilled Greens: Made by using Electric Safflinas or other electric Ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of Shock Resistance.

* Mighty Salt-Grilled Greens: Made by using Mighty Thistles or other mighty Ingredients. Consuming these greens will boost the power of Link's attacks.

* Tough Salt-Grilled Greens: Made by using Armoranths or other tough Ingredients. Link's defensive abilities will be increased if he eats these.

* Sneaky Salt-Grilled Greens: Made by using Blue Nightshades, Silent Princesses, or other sneaky Ingredients. They can be eaten to boost Link's Stealth.

* Chilly Salt-Grilled Greens: Made by using Cool Safflinas or other chilly Ingredients. When eaten, they will grant Link one level of Heat Resistance.
