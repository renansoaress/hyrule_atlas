### Breath of the Wild
Snowquill Trousers are pieces of Armor that can be bought for 550 Rupees at the Brazen Beak. When worn, they provide one level of Cold Resistance. The Snowquill Trousers can be enhanced by a Great Fairy and can be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Snowquill Trousers, alongside the Snowquill Headdress and Snowquill Tunic, compose the Snowquill Set. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides the Unfreezable Set Bonus.
