### Breath of the Wild
Black Lizalfos
Black Lizalfos are a stronger variant of Lizalfos. They are known to carry stronger pieces of Lizalfos Gear, resulting in them being regarded as more dangerous. Like most Lizalfos, Black Lizalfos can be found swimming in bodies of Water, such as lakes or seas. They can also be found within Monster Strongholds.
