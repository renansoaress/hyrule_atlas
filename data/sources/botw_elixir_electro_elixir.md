### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Electro Elixirs are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Link can drink Electro Elixirs to gain one level of Shock Resistance. They're known to be useful against electrical attacks.

Before parting at Inogo Bridge, Sidon will gift Link an Electro Elixir to show his faith in him. He notes that they have no effect on Zora, which he theorizes is due to them being made for Hylians.

Electro Elixirs can be made by Cooking any of the following critters with Monster Parts in a Cooking Pot:

* Electric Darner

* Thunderwing Butterfly

By adding any of these electric ingredients, Link can make an Electro Elixir that will also heal him:

* Voltfruit

* Zapshroom

* Electric Safflina

* Voltfin Trout
