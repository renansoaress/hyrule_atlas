### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Glazed Meats are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Glazed Meats are meals that can be made by Cooking predominantly Meat with  Courser Bee Honey. Their regular version can only be made by adding a third ingredient that differs in effects from the Courser Bee Honey. They heal seven of Link's Hearts and sell for 60 Rupees. The sweetness of the honey permeates the Meat, giving it a complex taste profile.

Only one variation of Glazed Meat exists:
* Energizing Glazed Meat: Made by only using Meat and Courser Bee Honey. Eating these will refill a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel. They heal seven of Link's Hearts, and they are worth 50 Rupees when sold.
