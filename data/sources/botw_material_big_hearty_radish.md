### Breath of the Wild
Big Hearty Radish
Big Hearty Radishes are flora that are found in various grassy areas of Hyrule, characterized by their forked and flowering stalk. The Vegetable itself cannot be seen until it is picked, as it is a root. As their name implies, Big Hearty Radishes are larger versions of the Hearty Radish.

Big Hearty Radishes can be eaten raw to restore four Hearts. If a Big Hearty Radish is roasted over a Campfire, it becomes a Roasted Big Radish. If a Big Hearty Radish is Cooked in a Cooking Pot, the resulting Food will grant Link Extra Hearts. At the Kochi Dye Shop, Big Hearty Radishes are one of the ingredients that can be used to make Peach Dye.

Making Creamy Heart Soup in a Cooking Pot requires Link to use either a Hearty Radish or a Big Hearty Radish:

- Creamy Heart Soup | Hydromelon, Voltfruit, Any Radish, Fresh Milk

Big Hearty Radishes, among other Vegetables, can also be used to make the following Meals:

- Cream of Mushroom Soup | Any Mushroom, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Cream of Vegetable Soup | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt

- Creamy Seafood Soup | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Fried Wild Greens | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Copious Fried Wild Greens | Any Vegetables, Herbs, or Flowers(4 different kinds)
- Glazed Veggies | Courser Bee Honey, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Herb Sauté | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Goron Spice
- Salt-Grilled Greens | Rock Salt, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Steamed Fish | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable,, Herb, or Flower
- Steamed Fruit | Any Fruit, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Steamed Meat | Any Meat, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Pumpkins)
- Steamed Mushrooms | Any Mushroom, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
