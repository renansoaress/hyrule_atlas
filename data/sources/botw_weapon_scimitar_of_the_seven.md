Scimitars of the Seven are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.(BotW, TotK, AoC, AoI)

### Breath of the Wild
Scimitar of the Seven

As part of the Champion's Arms, Scimitars of the Seven are commonly used alongside Daybreaker Shields. They belong to the Gerudo Gear equipment archetype, which is a series of Weapons created and utilized by the Gerudo.

Prior to the Great Calamity, the Gerudo Champion Urbosa wielded a Scimitar of the Seven with a Daybreaker. They were created by a Gerudo jeweler. It is said that Urbosa's movements resembled a dance while she used the Scimitar in battle. During the EX Recovered Memory "Champion Urbosa's Song", Urbosa can be seen using her Scimitar to fight off a pair of Yiga Footsoldiers attacking Zelda.

Riju will gift Link Urbosa's Scimitar of the Seven and Daybreaker after completing the "Divine Beast Vah Naboris" Main Quest. She believes that, since Link was friends with Urbosa, they should be in his possession. They can be found in a pair of Stone Chests behind Riju's Throne in the Royal Palace. If Link loses the Scimitar of the Seven, he can go to Buliara to replace it, though it will cost him one Diamond, five pieces of Flint, and a Gerudo Scimitar.

Scimitars of the Seven are made of metal, and will therefore attract Lightning if they are equipped during a Thunderstorm. This also means that they are capable of conducting Electricity. They will sink if dropped in the Water, but they can be picked up again using the Magnesis Rune. In addition, they will not burn when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate.
