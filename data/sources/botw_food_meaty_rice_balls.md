### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Meaty Rice Balls are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Meaty Rice Balls are Meals that can be made by Cooking predominantly Hylian Rice together with some form of Meat. If consumed by Link, they restore at least four of his Hearts. Meaty Rice Balls have a sweet and spicy taste.

Link can add additional ingredients to the recipe for Meaty Rice Balls, which can result in the following variations:

* Chilly Meaty Rice Balls: Made by adding chilly ingredients. When eaten, they grant Link one level of Heat Resistance.

* Electro Meaty Rice Balls: Made by adding electric ingredients. Link can eat them to gain one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Meaty Rice Balls: Made by adding enduring ingredients. They can be eaten to completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Meaty Rice Balls: Made by adding energizing ingredients. Eating them will refill part of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Meaty Rice Balls: Made by adding hasty ingredients. Eating these rice balls will temporarily increase Link's movement speed.

* Hearty Meaty Rice Balls: Made by adding hearty ingredients. When eaten, they will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.

* Mighty Meaty Rice Balls: Made by adding mighty ingredients. If eaten, Link gains a boost to the power of his attacks.

* Sneaky Meaty Rice Balls: Made by adding sneaky ingredients. Eating them will provide Link with a boost to his Stealth.

* Spicy Meaty Rice Balls: Made by adding spicy ingredients. Consuming them will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.

* Tough Meaty Rice Balls: Made by adding tough ingredients. They increase Link's defense when they are eaten.
