### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Cream of Vegetable Soups are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Cream of Vegetable Soups are made in Cooking Pots by combining Fresh Milk, Rock Salt, and any Vegetable, herb, or Flower. This Recipe, however, excludes Endura Carrots, Swift Carrots, and Fortified Pumpkins, otherwise the resulting dish will be Veggie Cream Soup. Eating them can heal between seven to ten of Link's Hearts, depending on the Ingredients used. They also sell between 40 to 60 Rupees. Cream of Vegetable Soups are healthy dishes, made by simmering Vegetables in milk.

Based on the type of Ingredients used, several different variations of Cream of Vegetable Soup can be made:

* Hearty Cream of Vegetable Soup: Made by using Hearty Radishes, Big Hearty Radishes, or other hearty Ingredients. These soups completely refill Link's Hearts when eaten and will temporarily increase his maximum Hearts. They also sell for 70 Rupees.

* Energizing Cream of Vegetable Soup: Made by including Courser Bee Honey or other energizing Ingredients in the recipe. When eaten, Link's Stamina Wheel will be partially refilled. They restore seven Hearts, and they sell for 50 Rupees.

* Hasty Cream of Vegetable Soup: Made using Swift Violets or other speed-increasing Ingredients. Eating one will temporarily increase Link's movement speed. These soups heal one Heart and sell for 40 Rupees.

* Spicy Cream of Vegetable Soup: Made using Warm Safflinas or other spicy Ingredients. If eaten, they grant Link one level of Cold Resistance for a short period of time. They replenish one Heart and sell for 20 Rupees.

* Electro Cream of Vegetable Soup: Made using Electric Safflinas or other electricity-related Ingredients. When eaten, Link will be granted one level of Shock Resistance. They'll heal Link for one Heart and sell for 20 Rupees.

* Mighty Cream of Vegetable Soup: Made using Mighty Thistles or other mighty Ingredients. Eating one will temporarily increase Link's attack power. They also heal one Heart, and they sell for 30 Rupees.

* Tough Cream of Vegetable Soup: Made using Armoranths or other tough Ingredients. When eaten, they'll increase Link's defense for a short time. They replenish one of Link's Hearts, and they sell for 30 Rupees.

* Sneaky Cream of Vegetable Soup: Made using Blue Nightshades, Silent Princesses, or other sneaky Ingredients. Eating one will temporarily increase Link's Stealth. They replenish one Heart and sell for 20 Rupees.

* Chilly Cream of Vegetable Soup: Made using Cool Safflinas or other chilly Ingredients. They grant Link one level of Heat Resistance when eaten. They heal one of Link's Heart and sell for 20 Rupees.
