### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Salt-Grilled Crabs are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Link can make Salt-Grilled Crabs by Cooking any Crab together with Rock Salt in a Cooking Pot. There are two ways to make their default version. First, Link must use two different types of Crabs. Alternatively, another Ingredient can be added to the Meal with an effect that differs from the Crab used. Eating these dishes will restore some of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge. A majority of fishermen enjoy Crabs most when they are prepared in this way.

Based on the type of crab used, Link can make the three following variations of Salt-Grilled Crab:

* Energizing Salt-Grilled Crab: Made by using Bright-Eyed Crabs. They refill a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel when eaten.

* Mighty Salt-Grilled Crab: Made by using Razorclaw Crabs. When consumed, they grant Link a boost to the power of his attacks.

* Tough Salt-Grilled Crab: Made by using Ironshell Crabs. Link's defensive abilities will be boosted by eating these Meals.
