### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
The Travel Medallion is a recurring Item in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
The Travel Medallion is exclusive to  Downloadable Content pack. It can be found inside a Hidden Chest in the chamber underneath Lomei Labyrinth Island. Its location is hinted at in Super Rumor Mill V2 in South Akkala Stable as part of the "EX Teleportation Rumors!" Side Quest. The Travel Medallion can be used to register Link's current position as a Travel Gate on the Map. Only one location can be registered at a time.
