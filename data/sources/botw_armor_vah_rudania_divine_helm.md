### Breath of the Wild
The Vah Rudania Divine Helm is obtained by scanning the Daruk amiibo with the amiibo Rune. When worn, the Vah Rudania Divine Helm provides a basic level of both Guardian Resist Up and Flame Guard and also allows Link to view an enemy's remaining health via the See Enemy HP effect. When paired with the Ancient Cuirass and the Ancient Greaves and each piece of Armor has been enhanced at least twice by a Great Fairy, the three pieces of Armor provide Ancient Proficiency, a Set Bonus that increases the damage dealt from the Ancient and Guardian archetypes of weapons.

The Vah Rudania Divine Helm is based on the head portion of Divine Beast Vah Rudania, which Daruk piloted as the Goron Champion.
