## Location and Uses
Shards of Naydra's Horn can be acquired by shooting the dragon Naydra on one of its horns. Naydra can be found at Mount Lanayru, the Lanayru Promenade, and Lanayru Bay. The Shard will glow as it falls to the ground.

Meals cooked using a Shard of Naydra's Horn will grant a buff that lasts for thirty minutes when eaten. The Shards can also be used at the Kochi Dye Shop to create Light Blue Dye.
