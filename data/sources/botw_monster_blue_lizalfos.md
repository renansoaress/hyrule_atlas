### Breath of the Wild
Blue Lizalfos
Blue Lizalfos are a stronger variation of the regular green Lizalfos, as they are knwon to have more health and carry stronger Lizalfos Gear and other Weapons. Like other Lizalfos, they can be often be found in or around bodies of Water, such as lakes and rivers. However, they also inhabit much of the Gerudo Desert.
