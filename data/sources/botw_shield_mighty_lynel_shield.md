### Breath of the Wild
Mighty Lynel Shield
Mighty Lynel Shields are Shields made by Lynels. They have been reinforced by both armor and many blades. Compared to Lynel Shields, they are stronger in both defense and offense, and they are capable of tearing through armor when deflecting (dealing 13 damage). They are part of the Lynel Gear equipment archetype, a series of Weapons created by Lynels.

Blue-Maned Lynels use these Shields, however White-Maned Lynels in Master Mode will use these Shields. They become scarce as Lynels tier up, though the Blue-Maned Lynel in Hyrule Castle's First Gatehouse will always stay the same, making it a permanent source of Mighty Lynel Shields.

Being made of metal, Mighty Lynel Shields conduct Electricity and attract Lightning if they are equipped during a thunderstorm, and will also sink if dropped in water. They can also be picked up and manipulated with the Magnesis Rune, and they will not burn if exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate.
