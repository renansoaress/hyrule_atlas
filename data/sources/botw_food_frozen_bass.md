### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Frozen Basses are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Frozen Basses are a type of Frozen Food, and they are made when either a Hyrule Bass or a Staminoka Bass are exposed to Freezing Climates or Cold Water. When eaten, a Frozen Bass will restore two Hearts to Link's Life Gauge and grant one minute of Heat Resistance.
