Frozen Whole Birds are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Frozen Whole Birds are a type of Frozen Food, which can be made by freezing Raw Whole Birds in a Freezing Climate or Cold Water. Though they have been frozen completely solid, the flavor of Frozen Whole Birds remains. When eaten, Frozen Whole Birds heal Link for three Hearts and grant him temporary Heat Resistance.
