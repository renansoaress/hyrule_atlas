## Texto de BOTW
Armor Sets are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda

### Breath of the Wild
Armor Sets are matching collections of Armor. When worn, particular Armor Sets may confer additional effects known as Set Bonuses if every piece of Armor in that Set has been enhanced at least twice by a Great Fairy. As Armors introduced with  and  cannot be enhanced, the Royal Guard Set and Tingle's Outfit will provide their Set Bonuses when worn regardless. The Phantom Equipment, Soldier's Set, and Well-Worn Outfit lack any kind of Set Bonus.
