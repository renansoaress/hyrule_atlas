Molduga,(BotW, TotK, AoC, AoI) also known as Giant Sand Beasts,(Breath of the Wild) are recurring Bosses and Sub-Bosses in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Molduga
Molduga are giant Monsters that swim through the Sand of the Gerudo Desert. They resemble four-legged Fish and patrol their territories in search of prey. Molduga are sensitive to sound, and they will rise out of Sand to eat whatever they can find. As a result, it is considered dangerous to enter the domain of a Molduga.

Once they become aware of Link's presence, Molduga will barrel towards and attempt to ram into him. Molduga can be fought while Shield Surfing with the help of a Sand Seal. Due to their sensitivity to loud noises, Link can strike them with Remote Bombs or Bomb Arrows, causing them to be stunned once they jump out of the Sand. When defeated, Molduga drops one or two Treasure Chests containing various Weapons, Molduga Fins, and Molduga Guts.

As part of the "Medicinal Molduga" Side Quest, Malena requires Molduga Guts in order to cure her husband's illness, but none of the soldiers in Gerudo Town are willing to defeat one for her. When Link brings her Molduga Guts, she gives him a Gold Rupee as a reward.

After Link defeats Calamity Ganon for the first time, he will be able to ask Kilton about Molduga. As part of his research, he has determined that four Molduga can be found in the entirety of Hyrule. Due to their size, Kilton is unable to safely approach them, so he asks Link if he could defeat all of them, allowing Kilton to analyze the scent their fluids leave behind. When Link returns to Kilton after defeating all of the Molduga, he will be able to smell them clearly, and he gives Link the Medal of Honor: Molduga as a reward.

As part of  Downloadable Content Pack, the Molduking will appear in the East Barrens. It is a more powerful subspecies of Molduga, known to build up energy by sleeping underground for hundreds of years.
