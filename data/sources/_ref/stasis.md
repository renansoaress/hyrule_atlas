## Texto de BOTW
is a Rune ability in Breath of the Wild. The Rune allows Link to freeze Objects in time while storing their kinetic energy.

## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
The Stasis Rune is obtained in the Owa Daim Shrine on the Great Plateau. Stasis allows Link to freeze Objects in suspended time, rendering them completely immobile for a short period. When an Object is suspended, a glowing, yellow filter surrounds its form. This filter will eventually disappear, whereupon the Object will revert back to normal and resume any movement it had before it was frozen. Through this, Stasis can be used to freeze dangerous obstacles, such as rolling Boulders, or manipulate Link's surroundings to bypass them, such as turning gears. After Stasis has been used on an Object, a timer gauge will appear which must reacharge before Link can use it again.

While suspended in time, Objects will store any additional kinetic energy that is inflicted upon them. Link can strike a suspended Object with a Weapon to produce a vast amount of kinetic energy, which will cause the Object to suddenly move as it regains mobility. A yellow arrow will appear as an Object is struck to signify which direction the object will move in. This makes it possible for even very heavy Objects like Boulders to spontaneously fly in a certain direction once time is restored. Stasis freezes an Object for a maximum of 10 seconds unless unfrozen early. Its cool-down time is also 10 seconds, though this can be reduced by releasing the Object early.

During the "Slated for Upgrades" Side Quest, Link can upgrade the Rune to Stasis + at the Hateno Ancient Tech Lab by giving Purah three Ancient Cores. Stasis + allows Link to freeze Enemies for a short period of time. Enemies under the effect of Stasis + are immune to being frozen, burned, or shocked. Stasis + will end much faster if used on an Enemy, but its cool-down time is still 10 seconds.
