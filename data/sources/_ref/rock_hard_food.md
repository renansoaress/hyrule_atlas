## Texto de BOTW
Rock-Hard Food is a recurring Meal in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Rock-Hard Food is the result of an invalid Cooking Recipe. It appears when Link attempts to cook food containing Wood or Ores. Rock-Hard Food only restores a quarter of one of Link's Hearts and has no effects. Eating these Meals is noted to be difficult.

Moza, the aspiring chef who can be found near Ishto Soh Shrine, has a recipe called the lavish Meat dish that will only result in Rock-Hard Food. She tells Link to prepare either bird Meat or steak and then to throw in some Ore. If Link calls the recipe gross, she scolds him for being rude and asks how else one is supposed to be a lavish Meat dish. However, Moza admits that the resulting Meal is always Rock-Hard Food, but she does not understand how it gets that rocky.
