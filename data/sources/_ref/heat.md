,(BotW, TotK) also known as Extreme Heat(Breath of the Wild) and ,(HW) is a recurring mechanic in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Heat occurs during the daytime in the Gerudo Desert. When the Temperature rises above 104°F, Link will experience one level of Heat and slowly take damage. When the Temperature rises above 122°F, as in Extremely Hot Zones, he will experience two levels of Heat and take damage more quickly. Link can avoid taking damage from Heat by taking shelter within shade, submerging himself in a body of Water, equipping certain pieces of Armor, consuming Chilly Cooking, or drinking Chilly Elixirs. Both Gerudo Town and Kara Kara Bazaar provide constant protection from Heat.

The Rito traveler, Guy, can be found in the Kara Kara Bazaar, waiting for the daytime Heat to pass in order to visit Gerudo Town. The Side Quest "An Ice Guy" involves giving a Chilly Elixir to Guy so that he can cross the Desert.
