## Texto de BOTW
Meats are a recurring class of Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
In Breath of the Wild, Meats can be obtained by killing Creatures. The quality and type of Meat is dependent on what kind of Creature has been killed. For example, mammals will drop Raw Meats, Raw Prime Meats, and Raw Gourmet Meats when they have been defeated, while Birds drop Raw Bird Thighs, Raw Bird Drumsticks, and Raw Whole Birds.

All Meats can be turned into both Roasted Food and Frozen Food. In addition to this, several Recipes require Meats as Cooking Ingredients. Creamy Meat Soup, Glazed Meat, a Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin, a Meat and Mushroom Skewer, Meat Pie, a Meat Skewer, and Copious Meat Skewers, Meaty Rice Balls, Monster Stew, Pepper Steak, Steamed Meat can use any type of Meat when Cooking them. Recipes that use a specific type of Meat are a Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl, Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry, Gourmet Meat Curry, Gourmet Meat Stew, Gourmet Poultry Pilaf, a Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer, a Meat and Rice Bowl, Meat and Seafood Fry, Meat Curry, Meat Stew, Poultry Curry, Poultry Pilaf, a Prime Meat and Rice Bowl, Prime Meat and Seafood Fry, Prime Meat Curry, Prime Meat Stew, Prime Poultry Curry, Prime Poultry Pilaf, a Prime Spiced Meat Skewer, Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat, Salt-Grilled Meat, Salt-Grilled Prime Meat, and a Spiced Meat Skewer
