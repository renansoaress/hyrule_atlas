Monster Parts,(BotW, TotK) also known as Monster Materials,(Tears of the Kingdom) are a recurring class of Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Monster Parts are a type of Material dropped by Monsters when they are defeated. Higher-ranking Monster Parts can be dropped by higher-ranking Enemies. For example, Bokoblin Horns can be dropped by all Bokoblins, but Bokoblin Guts will only be dropped by Blue Bokoblins or higher. Materials dropped from Creature that can deal damage to Link, like Boars or Wolves, are not considered Monster Parts.

Monster Parts can be sold to Shop and Merchants all around Hyrule for varying prices. Rarer Monster Parts will sell for higher amounts of Rupees. Kilton, the proprietor of the Fang and Bone, accepts Monster Parts in exchange for his currency, Mon.

All Monster Parts can be Cooked with Lizards and Insects to make Elixirs, and their duration will vary based on the part included. If Cooked by themselves, Monster Part will only create Dubious Food.
