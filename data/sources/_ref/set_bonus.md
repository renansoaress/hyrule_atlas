## Texto de BOTW
Set Bonuses,(BotW, TotK) also known as Special Bonuses,(Tears of the Kingdom) are a recurring mechanic in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
In Breath of the Wild, there are 15 different Set Bonuses, two of which are hidden in the Inventory.

#### Set Bonuses in Breath of the Wild
(colunas: Set Bonus | Found On | Description)
- Ancient Proficiency | Ancient Set (★★), Vah Medoh Divine Helm (★★) + Ancient Cuirass (★★) + Ancient Greaves (★★), Vah Naboris Divine Helm (★★) + Ancient Cuirass (★★) + Ancient Greaves (★★), Vah Rudania Divine Helm (★★) + Ancient Cuirass (★★) + Ancient Greaves (★★), Vah Ruta Divine Helm (★★) + Ancient Cuirass (★★) + Ancient Greaves (★★) | Increases the attack power of Ancient Weapons by 80%. This stacks multiplicatively with the Attack Up effect.
- Bone Atk. Up | Radiant Attire (★★), Phantom Ganon Set | Increases the attack power of bone weapons by 80%.
The following items are considered to be bone weapons:
Bokoblin Arm, Dragonbone Boko Bat, Dragonbone Boko Bow, Dragonbone Boko Club, Dragonbone Boko Spear, Dragonbone Moblin Club, Dragonbone Moblin Spear, Lizal Bow, Lizalfos Arm, Moblin Arm, Spiked Boko Bow, Spiked Boko Club, Spiked Boko Spear, Spiked Moblin Club, Spiked Moblin Spear
- Charge Atk. Stamina Up | Barbarian Set (★★), Royal Guard Set, Fierce Deity Set (★★) | Decreases the Stamina consumption of charge attacks by 50%.
- Climb Jump Stamina Up | Climbing Set (★★) | Decreases the Stamina used when jumping while climbing by 50%.
- Fireproof | Flame-Resistant Equipment (★★) | Prevents Link from taking any damage from Fire or Fire-based attacks. Does not protect against damage from Extremely Hot Zones, Scorching Climates, or Lava.
- Heat-Resistant | Gerudo Outfit | Mimics a level one Heat Resistance effect. Stacks with a level one Heat Resistance effect, for a total of two levels.
- Master Sword Beam Up | Hero Set (★★), Hero of Time Set (★★), Hero of Twilight Set (★★), Hero of Winds Set (★★), Hero of the Sky Set (★★), Hero of the Wild Set (★★) | Increases the attack power of the Master Sword's Sword Beam attack by 50%, stacking with the attack power increase granted when the Master Sword is awakened.
- Night Speed Up | Dark Series, Stealth Attire (★★), Tingle's Outfit | Between the in-game hours of 9:00pm and 4:00am, mimics the running speed increase provided by a level two Speed Up effect, increasing Link's running speed by 20%. Can be stacked with a level one Speed Up effect.
- Shock Damage Resist | Desert Voe Set (★★) | Mimics a level two Shock Resistance effect, reducing the damage Link takes from Electricity by 66%. Does not affect the explosion damage from lightning strikes. Stacks with a level one Shock Resistance effect.
- Stal Disguise | Radiant Attire (★★), Phantom Ganon Set | Prevents Stalkoblins, Stalmoblins, and Stalizalfos from attacking Link unless he attacks them first. Instead, they will follow Link around and study him. Stalnoxes are temporarily fooled by the disguise. The effect will end early if an enemy that is not affected by the disguise begins to attack Link.
- Swim Dash Stamina Up | Zora Set (★★), Salvager Armor Set | Decreases the Stamina used when dashing or using a spin attack while swimming by 75%.
- Unfreezable | Snowquill Set (★★) | Prevents Link from taking any damage from Ice-based attacks, as well as preventing him from being frozen. Does not protect against damage from Freezing Climates or freezing water.
- Unshockable | Rubber Set (★★) | Prevents Link from taking any damage from Electricity or Lightning strikes, and prevents any negative effects from either, such as being knocked back or disarmed.
