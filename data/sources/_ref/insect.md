## Texto de BOTW
Insects,(Breath of the Wild) also known as Bugs,(OoT, MM, SS, TotK) and Bug Parts,(Breath of the Wild) are recurring Enemies, Creatures, and Items in série The Legend of Zelda. With the exception of Shadow Insects, Bugs do not have plot-relevance; their role is usually limited to Side Quests.

### Breath of the Wild
Many Insects can be found throughout Hyrule in Breath of the Wild. They can be Cooked into Elixirs.
