## Texto de BOTW
Frogs are a recurring species in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Two species of Frog exist in Breath of the Wild. The first is the Hot-Footed Frog, which is known for its speed and can be Cooked to create a Hasty Elixir. The second is the Tireless Frog, which can only be found during Rain. It can also be Cooked to create an Enduring Elixir. Both types of Frogs are most common on Hyrule Ridge and in the Lanayru Great Spring, and they will run away if they spot Link. To catch them, Link can chase after them, use Stealth to get close without being seen, or stun them with an Arrow or a Remote Bomb.
