## Texto de BOTW
Temperatures is a recurring gameplay mechanic in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Unlike in the previous games, Temperature is now a gameplay mechanic featured everywhere around Hyrule Kingdom, and Link can check the Temperature with a dial on the bottom-right screen or opening his Sheikah Slate and viewing the Map section with actual measurements. When exposed to extreme Temperatures, Link will begin to take damage, but can prevent this by going to a more temperate area, entering bodies of Water in Scorching Climates, equipping an elemental Weapon, wearing certain Armor, or eating Food with status effects.

When the Temperature drops below 32°F (0°C), Link will experience a basic level of cold Temperature that will slowly damage him. When the Temperature drops below 14°F (-10°C), Link will experience an advanced level of cold Temperature and will take damage more quickly. Certain bodies of water in Freezing Climates will cause Link to quickly take damage; no armor, elixir nor food can offer protection against this. Similarly, when the Temperature rises above 104°F (40°C), Link will experience a basic level of Heat, he will also slowly sustain damage. When the Temperature rises above 122°F (50°C), Link will experience an advanced level of Heat and take damage more quickly. Places that contain lava flows raise Temperatures significantly beyond normal Heat, the Sheikah Slate will malfunction and read "ERROR". Additionally, Link and any wooden equipment exposed to the environment will begin to catch fire.
