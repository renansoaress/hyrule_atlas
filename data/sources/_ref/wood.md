## Texto de BOTW
Wood is a recurring Item in série The Legend of Zelda.(BotW, TotK, AoI)

### Breath of the Wild
In Breath of the Wild, Wood can be obtained from Woodcutting Trees throughout Hyrule or found at various settlements like Hateno Village or Stables. They can be chopped using any bladed Weapon or knocked down with Remote Bombs. Once a Tree is felled, Link can strike or blow up the fallen Log to create a bundle of Wood.

Wood can be used to light Campfires, along with a flammable Weapon or a piece of Flint and a metallic Weapon. In addition, Wood is used during the "Hylian Homeowner" Side Quest to buy Link's House, as well as during the "From the Ground Up" Side Quest to construct Houses for Tarrey Town.

To remake the Great Eagle Bow, Link must bring Harth a Swallow Bow, a Diamond, and five bundles of Wood.

## Trivia
* In the E3 2016 demo of Breath of the Wild, Wood was called "Firewood" and had a differently-worded item description.
