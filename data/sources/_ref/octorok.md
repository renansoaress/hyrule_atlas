### Breath of the Wild
Octoroks appear in many different varieties in Breath of the Wild, including Water Octoroks, Forest Octoroks, Rock Octoroks, Snow Octoroks, Treasure Octoroks, and in Master Mode, Sky Octoroks. All of them except for  Sky and Treasure Octoroks are characterized by their ability to shoot rocks (or snowballs) at Link, though Rock Octoroks will inhale a massive load of air and then shoot an explosive rock at Link. Link can use a Shield to reflect rocks back at them, though explosive rocks will explode even if Link performs a Perfect Guard. Each Octorok except Sky Octoroks have a means of camouflage, staying hidden under a plant or a rock, or appearing as Buried Treasure (though Rock Octoroks will remain above ground until Link gets too close). For all but Rock Octoroks, Link can shoot their various coverings to force them to reveal themselves. In Master Mode, Sky Octoroks can be found holding plank platforms that hold Treasure Chests and enemies, and can be popped with a single hit. Octorok Tentacles, Octo Balloons, and Octorok Eyeballs can be recovered from any variant (except for Sky Octoroks, as they do not drop any Monster Parts). Rupees are also dropped by Treasure Octoroks whenever hit or defeated.
