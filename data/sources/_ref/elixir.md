## Texto de BOTW
Elixirs are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.(BotW, TotK)

### Breath of the Wild
BotW
Elixirs can be created by Cooking at least one Critter with at least one Monster Part in a Cooking Pot. Other Materials can also be added when Cooking to create Elixirs that can restore Hearts. All Critters and some of the other Materials are divided into Categories that determine the Elixir's effect. If the Materials used share the same Category, the Elixir produced match that Category and the strength of its effect is determined by the Potency of the Materials used. Cooking Materials from multiple Categories will produce Dubious Food instead of an Elixir. The duration of the Elixir depends on the Materials used when Cooking with each Material increasing the duration by a fixed amount. A critical success when Cooking also increases the Elixir's duration by 5:00. Elixirs can have a duration of up to 30 minutes.
