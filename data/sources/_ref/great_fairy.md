## Texto de BOTW
Great Fairies are recurring characters in série The Legend of Zelda. They are large humanoid Fairies. Their main purpose is to heal Link whenever he is wounded. They will also recover his Magic Meter in games that incorporate one. In other titles, such as Ocarina of Time and The Wind Waker, they also bless Link with new items, powers, or upgrades of his existing equipment. Most Great Fairies can be found living within a Great Fairy Fountain.

### Breath of the Wild
In Breath of the Wild only four Great Fairies appear, named Cotera, Kaysa, Mija and Tera. At first they are found in closed Great Fairy Fountains resembling a large flower bud. To restore them, Link must offer them an increasing amount of Rupees. Once restored they will upgrade Link's Armor when provided with the proper Materials, though the max level depends on the total number of restored Fairies. Additionally, a fifth Fairy Fountain can be found, but it contains the Horse God Malanya instead, though his hands resemble those of a Great Fairy.
