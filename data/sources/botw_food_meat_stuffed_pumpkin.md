### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Meat-Stuffed Pumpkins are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Meat-Stuffed Pumpkins can be Cooked by combining a Fortified Pumpkin with any type of Meat in a Cooking Pot. Their default version can be made by adding a third ingredient with an effect other than tough. By eating them, some of Links's Hearts will be restored. These Meals are a specialty of Kakariko Village.

After completing Koko's first two quests, Link can speak to her again to find that she wants to make her father's favorite meal, Tough Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin. Unfortunately, while going over the recipe, Koko realizes that she doesn't have any Raw Meat. This begins the Side Quest "Koko Cuisine". Link can bring her Raw Meat, which Koko will be thankful for After making it, Koko will be pleased with the result, saying it is just like her mother made and giving some to Link as a reward.

Only one variation of Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin exists:

* Tough Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin: Made by using the default recipe. Eating these pumpkins will increase Link's Defense.
