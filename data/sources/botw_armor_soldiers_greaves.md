### Breath of the Wild
Soldier's Greaves can be purchased at the Ventest Clothing Boutique in Hateno Village. They are part of the Soldier's Set, along with the Soldier's Helm and Soldier's Armor. Wearing them provides no bonus aside from an increased Defense. Soldier's Greaves can be both enhanced by a Great Fairy and Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.
