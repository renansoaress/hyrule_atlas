### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Royal Guard's Shields are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.(BotW, TotK) Royal Guard's Shields go unnamed in Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity.

### Breath of the Wild
Royal Guard's Shield
Royal Guard's Shields are found in Hyrule Castle, either abandoned or being used by the Enemies inside. They were created using the Ancient Technology of the Sheikah for use of the Royal Guards of Hyrule Kingdom. Though they have an incredibly high defense, a structural weakness led to their low Durability, making them impractical for combat. This is a common feature of the Royal Guard's Series, the equipment archetype to which Royal Guard's Shields belong.

One Royal Guard's Shield can be found in the King's Study, which is within Hyrule Castle's Library. If Link wears a Royal Guard's Shield while talking to Selmie, she will be unimpressed to the point of yawning and calls them flimsy pieces of garbage that are worthless to serious Shield Surfers, due to their poor Durability making them unsuitable for Shield Surfing.

As part of "The Royal Guard's Gear" Side Quest, Parcy wishes to see a piece of the Royal Guard's Series. If Link speaks to her while carrying a Royal Guard's Shield, she will recognize it and ask him to show it to her. Should Link agree, she will be grateful and give him a Gold Rupee as a reward. Afterwards, Link can trade Royal Guard's Shields with Parcy in exchange for a Topaz, Ruby, or Sapphire.

Being made of metal, Royal Guard's Shields conduct Electricity and will attract Lightning if equipped during Thunderstorms. However, they will not burn like wooden Shields do when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. They will also sink if they are dropped in water, but they can be picked up and manipulated with the Magnesis Rune.
