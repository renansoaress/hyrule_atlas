### Breath of the Wild
The Extravagant Bridle is a type of Bridle that can be obtained from Blynne by completing the Obstacle Course Mini-Game in less than one minute and thirty seconds. These Bridles are often utilized in festivals. They are also decorated with feathers, making them great for adding a splash of color to Horses.

The Extravagant Bridle is a piece of Extravagant Gear, which is a set of Horse Gear. Link can have the Extravagant Bridle equipped onto his Horses by one of the workers at Highland Stable, Outskirt Stable, South Akkala Stable, or Woodland Stable. However, as is the case with other Bridles, they are unable be worn by Epona or the Giant Horse.
