## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Spiked Boko Spear
Spiked Boko Spears are upgraded Boko Spears that have been enhanced using Animal bones. They are used most often by Blue Bokoblins and Blue Moblins in the Faron Grasslands and East Necluda. They belong to the Bokoblin Gear equipent, which includes all Weapons made by Bokoblins. Known to be light and easy to use, they deal a decent amount of damage. When scanned with the amiibo Rune, the Breath of the Wild Series Bokoblin amiibo has a chance of spawning a Treasure Chest containing a Spiked Boko Spear.

Being made of wood, Spiked Boko Spears will float in Water if they are dropped. They will also burn if exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate, and they do not attract Lightning during Thunderstorm or conduct Electricity.
