### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Wildberries are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Wildberry

Wildberries are found growing on bushes in the Gerudo Highlands and Hebra Mountains, or they can be dropped by Hinoxes when defeated. They can be eaten raw to restore half of a Heart. If a Wildberry is roasted over a Campfire or dropped in a hot location like Death Mountain, it becomes a Roasted Wildberry, which doubles its healing properties to heal a full Heart of the Life Gauge. At the Kochi Dye Shop, Wildberries are one of the ingredients that can make Peach Dye. Wildberries are not required to upgrade any pieces of Armor at a Great Fairy Fountain.

As part of the Side Quest "The Mystery Polluter," Link must give Calyban 10 Wildberries in order to get her to stop polluting the Water trough in Dalia's garden. Once Link delivers the Wildberries, Calyban will help Dalia start a Wildberry garden in Gerudo Town, which completes the Side Quest.

Wildberries are necessary to make a Wildberry Crepe in a Cooking Pot. Either a Wildberry or an Apple is needed to make Fruitcake.

- Fruitcake | Apple or Wildberry, Any Fruit, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar
- Wildberry Crepe | Wildberry, Fresh Milk, Cane Sugar, Tabantha Wheat, Bird Egg

They can be used in other Fruit-based Meals:

- Fruit and Mushroom Mix | Any Fruit, Any Mushroom
- Fruit Pie | Any Fruit (besides Apple), Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter
- Fruitcake | Apple or Wildberry, Any Fruit, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar
- Honeyed Fruits | Courser Bee Honey, Any Fruit (besides Apple)
- Simmered Fruit | Any Fruit
- Copious Simmered Fruit | Any Fruit (4 different kinds)
- Steamed Fruit | Any Fruit, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
