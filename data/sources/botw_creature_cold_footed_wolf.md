Cold-Footed Wolves are recurring Creatures in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Cold-Footed Wolf
Cold-Footed Wolves are a breed of Maraudo Wolf that have adapted to Freezing Climates in Hyrule Kingdom. They are most commonly found in packs on the Hebra Mountains and Gerudo Highlands, but they are also present on the Tabantha Tundra and Mount Lanayru. They can be distinguished from other Wolves through their unique white fur, which serves as camouflage in their alpine habitat.

Cold-Footed Wolves are highly social animals that are never encountered alone, averaging at three individuals per pack. When idle, Cold-Footed Wolves can be seen sniffing the ground and stretching their bodies. However, if a Wolf spots Link, it will howl to alert its pack members and give chase. Once Link has been cornered, the Wolves will encircle him and take turns charging at him with snapping jaws, inflicting damage on contact. However, they will disband and retreat if one is injured or killed, and they will not try to attack Link if he is riding a Horse. Killing a Cold-Footed Wolf produces either Raw Prime Meat or Raw Gourmet Meat.
