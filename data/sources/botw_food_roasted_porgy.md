### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Roasted Porgies are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Porgies are a type of Roasted Food, and they are made when Armored Porgies and Mighty Porgies are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. They also have a chance of being dropped by Blue Hinoxes when felled by Link. They have a delicious, crisp skin and a soft and fluffy texture. Eating one restores one and a half of Link's Hearts.
