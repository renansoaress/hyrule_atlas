## Location and Uses
Thunderspear
Thunderspears are magical Weapons that can be found on Hyrule Ridge and in Hyrule Field, particularly within Hyrule Castle. Their spearheads contain thunder from the Thundra Plateau in their tips. This elemental nature makes them a part of the Magic Gear equipment archetype. When their blades glow, they are able to unleash an attack that will electrocute Enemies.

Being made of metal, Thunderspears attract Lightning during Thunderstorms and conduct Electricity. They also sink in Water, but can be manipulated and picked back up using the Magnesis Rune. When exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate, Thunderspears do not burn like wooden Weapons do.
