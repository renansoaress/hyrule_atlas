Moblin Guts are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Moblin Guts are dropped by Blue Moblins, Black Moblins, Silver Moblins, and, in Master Mode, Golden Moblins. They have a pungent odor, but can be used while Cooking with critters to make Elixirs. They can also be exchanged for Mon at the Fang and Bone or used at the Kochi Dye Shop to make Light Blue Dye.

Moblin Guts can be used at a Great Fairy Fountain to upgrade several pieces of Armor. Fireproof Armor requires three Moblin Guts to upgrade each piece to their third tier, while the Radiant Set and Soldier's Set require them for their second tiers.
