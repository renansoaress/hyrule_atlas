Nutcakes are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Nutcakes are a kind of Cake that can be made by Cooking either an Acorn or a Chickaloo Tree Nut together with Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, and Goat Butter. Eating them will restore some of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge. These Cakes have a pleasant texture and a simple sweetness, both of which come from the use of nuts.

At The Slippery Falcon in Rito Village, Link can find Misa's Recipe Book, which contains a Recipe for Nutcake. Misa first instructs the reader to purchase Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, and Cane Sugar from her General Store. She then says to throw them into a Cooking Pot with nuts of one's choice, though she prefers Acorns.

Link can add a fifth Ingredient when making Nutcake, which can result in the following variations:

* Chilly Nutcake: Made by adding a chilly Ingredient. When eaten, Link will gain one level of Heat Resistance.

* Electro Nutcake: Made by adding an electric Ingredient. If eaten, Link will gain one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Nutcake: Made by adding an enduring Ingredient. Consuming them will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Nutcake: Made by adding an energizing Ingredient. These Cakes can be eaten to refill part of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Nutcake: Made by adding a hasty Ingredient. Link can eat them to have his movement speed increased.

* Hearty Nutcake: Made by adding a hearty Ingredient. When eaten, all of Link's Hearts will be healed, and he will gain Extra Hearts.

* Mighty Nutcake: Made by adding a mighty Ingredient. When consumed, Link will gain a boost to his attack power.

* Sneaky Nutcake: Made by adding a sneaky Ingredient. Eating these Cakes will provide a boost to Link's Stealth.

* Spicy Nutcake: Made by adding a spicy Ingredient. Eating them will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.

* Tough Nutcake: Made by adding a tough Ingredient. They can be eaten to increase Link's defensive abilities.
