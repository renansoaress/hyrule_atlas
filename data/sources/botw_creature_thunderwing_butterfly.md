### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Thunderwing Butterflies are recurring Creatures and Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Thunderwing Butterfly
Thunderwing Butterflies can be found flying on Hyrule Ridge and in the Gerudo Highlands and can be caught be sneaking up on them. Beedle also sells Thunderwing Butterflies at Lakeside Stable, Riverside Stable, and Tabantha Bridge Stable. They can be combined with Monster Parts to make an Electro Elixir, which grants Shock Resistance when used. The Kochi Dye Shop can also use Thunderwing Butterflies to create Yellow Dye.
