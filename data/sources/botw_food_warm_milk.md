### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Milk,(OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, TP, TPHD, ALBW, TotK) also known as Lon Lon Milk,(OoT, TWW, TWWHD, TMC, HWL) Milk Bottles,(Majora's Mask) Ordon Goat Milk,(Twilight Princess) Milk in a Bottle,(Twilight Princess) and Warm Milk,(Breath of the Wild) is a recurring Item in série The Legend of Zelda. It can be stored in Empty Bottles and usually has two helpings. Each helping recovers up to five Hearts, with the exception of Twilight Princess where it only recovers three.

### Breath of the Wild
Warm Milk are a kind of Food that Link can make by Cooking Fresh Milk in a Cooking Pot. Drinking them will heal Link's Life Gauge of some of his Hearts. Having one before sleeping will ensure a good night's rest.

Link can add extra ingredients to Warm Milk, which can result in the following variations:

* Chilly Milk: Made by adding chilly ingredients. By drinking them, Link will receive one level of Cold Resistance.

* Electro Milk: Made by adding electric ingredients. If drunk, they will grant Link one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Milk: Made by adding enduring ingredients. They can be drunk to completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Milk: Made by adding energizing ingredients. When drunk, Link's Stamina Wheel will be partly refilled.

* Hasty Milk: Made by adding hasty ingredients. These drinks will provide a boost to Link's movement speed.

* Hearty Milk: Made by adding hearty ingredients. If consumed, all of Link's Hearts will be healed, and he will gain temporary Hearts.

* Mighty Milk: Made by adding mighty ingredients. Drinking them will grant Link a boost to his attack power.

* Sneaky Milk: Made by adding sneaky ingredients. When drunk, they will provide a boost to Link's Stealth.

* Spicy Milk: Made by adding spicy ingredients. One level of Cold Resistance will be given to Link if he drinks them.

* Tough Milk: Made by adding tough ingredients. Link's defense will be increased when he drinks them.
