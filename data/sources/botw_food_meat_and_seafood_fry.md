### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Meat and Seafood Fries are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Meat and Seafood Fries can be made by Cooking any Meat and Seafood together in a Cooking Pot. Their default version can be made by using Seafood with contradicting effects. Alternatively, Seafood without any effects can be used, such as Hyrule Basses and Sanke Carp. When eaten, these Meals will restore some of Link's Hearts. These dishes are noted to be filling and have fresh Ingredients.

In his diary, the Old Man writes about making Spicy Meat and Seafood Fry, claiming it restores health and keeps him warm in the cold. Using this dish, he claims he will no longer need his Warm Doublet. However, on the next page, he laments the fact that he did not write down the recipe, only remembering that he used Raw Meat and Spicy Peppers. Though it seems unlikely to him, if anyone were to know the final Ingredient, he would give them his Warm Doublet. Link can make Spicy Meat and Seafood Fry for the Old Man, and he will be rewarded with a Warm Doublet if he shares the recipe. He will later write in his diary about how Link is a fine young man who he sees potential in, as well as transcribing the recipe.

Based on the type of Seafood used and whether additional Ingredients are added, the following variations of Meat and Seafood Fry can be made:

* Chilly Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by adding Chillfin Trouts or other cold Ingredients. These meals grant Link one level of Heat Resistance when consumed.

* Electro Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by using Voltfin Trout or other electric Ingredients. Link will gain one level of Shock Resistance when he eats these meals.

* Enduring Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by adding enduring Ingredients. When eaten, they will completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by using Staminoka Basses, Bright-Eyed Crabs, or other energizing Ingredients. Eating them will fill a part of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by adding hasty Ingredients. If eaten, they will increase Link's speed for a short period of time.

* Hearty Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by using Hearty Bass, Hearty Salmons, Hearty Blueshell Snails, or other hearty Ingredients. These meals will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.

* Mighty Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by using Mighty Carp, Mighty Porgies, Razorclaw Crabs, or other mighty Ingredients. When eaten, they will increase Link's attack power.

* Sneaky Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by using Stealthfin Trouts or other sneaky Ingredients. When eaten, they boost Link's Stealth.

* Spicy Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by using Sizzlefin Trouts or other spicy Ingredients. They can be eaten to grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.

* Tough Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by using Armored Carp, Armored Porgies, Ironshell Crabs, or other tough Ingredients. They increase Link's Defense when he eats them.
