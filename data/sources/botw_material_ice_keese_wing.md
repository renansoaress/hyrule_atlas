### Breath of the Wild
Ice Keese Wings are dropped by Ice Keese, which can be found in Freezing Climates such as the Hebra Mountains and Mount Lanayru. They are Monster Parts and can be used in a Cooking Pot to create Elixirs. They can also be exchanged for Mon at the Fang and Bone or used to make Blue Dye at the Kochi Dye Shop.
