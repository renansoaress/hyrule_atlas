### Breath of the Wild
The Soldier's Armor, along with the Soldier's Helm and Soldier's Greaves, comprise the Soldier's Set. The set can be purchased at Hateno Village. It appears to be composed of a pair of pauldrons, a pair of gauntlets, and a full breastplate, all of which are made of steel. It has no effect aside from increasing defense, but it can be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop and enhanced by a Great Fairy.
