### Breath of the Wild
Rock Salt can be found by destroying Ore Deposits across Hyrule Kingdom. It can also be purchased from the Goron Gusto Shop, the Coral Reef, and the travelling merchant Mezer. Though it cannot be eaten, it can be used to make Meals in a Cooking Pot. It can also be an ingredient to make Peach Dye at the Kochi Dye Shop.

Rock Salt is required in order to Cook the following Meals:
