Toasty Hylian Shrooms are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Toasty Hylian Shrooms are a variety of Roasted Food, being the result of Hylian Shrooms that have been burnt by Fire or a Scorching Climate. They can also purchased from the Protein Palace in Goron City for 12 Rupees. Toasty Hylian Shrooms have an amplified taste and fragrance compared to regular Hylian Shrooms. When eaten, they restore three-quarters of a Heart to Link's Life Gauge.
