Traveler's Bridles are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
A Traveler's Bridle is a Bridle and a piece of Horse Gear. It has a chance to appear in a pair of Treasure Chest that spawn when the Link (Rider) amiibo is scanned with the amiibo Rune. One Treasure Chest will contain the Traveler's Bridle, while the other Chest will contain the Traveler's Saddle.

Once obtained, the Traveler's Bridle will appear in Link's Key Item Inventory. Link can have it equipped on his Horses by speaking to specific workers at Highland Stable, Outskirt Stable, South Akkala Stable, and Woodland Stable.  However, they are unable to be equipped onto Epona or the Giant Horse. Though its design does not stand out, it is favored among peddlers and travelers for suiting any type of Horse.

In his Recovered Memories, Link is shown to have a Traveler's Bridle and Traveler's Saddle equipped to his Horse before the second Great Calamity.
