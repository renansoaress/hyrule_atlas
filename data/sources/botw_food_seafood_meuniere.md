### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Seafood Meunières are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Link can make Seafood Meunières by Cooking Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, and any Seafood together in a Cooking Pot. The only exceptions to this are Hearty Salmons, Armored Porgies, and Mighty Porgies, as including these Fish will result in different Meals. The default version of Seafood Meunière can be made either by using Hyrule Basses or Sanke Carps, which offer no status effects, or by using multiple types of Seafood with differing effects. Eating these Meals will heal some of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge. The secret Ingredient to Seafood Meunière is a lot of love.

When making Seafood Meunière, Link can make the following variations, based on both the type of seafood used as well as additional Ingredients:

* Hearty Seafood Meunière: Made by using Hearty Bass, Hearty Blueshell Snails, or other hearty Ingredients. When eaten, these will heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.

* Energizing Seafood Meunière: Made by using Staminoka Basses, Bright-Eyed Crabs, or other energizing Ingredients. By eating them, Link can refill part of his Stamina Wheel.

* Enduring Seafood Meunière: Made by using enduring Ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely refreshed and overfilled if he eats them.

* Hasty Seafood Meunière: Made by using hasty Ingredients. They can be eaten to grant Link a boost to his movement speed.

* Spicy Seafood Meunière: Made by using Sizzlefin Trouts or other Ingredients. If Link consumes these meals, he will gain one level of Cold Resistance.

* Electro Seafood Meunière: Made by using Voltfin Trout or other spicy Ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one level of Shock Resistance.

* Mighty Seafood Meunière: Made by using Mighty Carp, Razorclaw Crabs, or other mighty Ingredients. Eating them will provide Link with a boost to his attack power.

* Tough Seafood Meunière: Made by using Armored Carp, Ironshell Crabs, or other tough Ingredients. Link's defensive abilities will be increased if he eats these.

* Sneaky Seafood Meunière: Made by using Stealthfin Trouts, Sneaky River Snails, or other sneaky Ingredients. These meals will provide a boost to Link's Stealth.

* Chilly Seafood Meunière: Made by using Chillfin Trouts or other chilly Ingredients. Consuming them will grant Link one level of Heat Resistance.
