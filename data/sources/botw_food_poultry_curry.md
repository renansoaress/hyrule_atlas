### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Poultry Curries are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Link can make Poultry Curries by Cooking a Raw Bird Drumstick, Hylian Rice, and Goron Spice together in a Cooking Pot. When eaten, they will heal some of Link's Hearts. The savory Meat in this common dish pairs well with the aroma of Goron Spice.

By adding other Ingredients to Poultry Curry, several different variations can be made:

* Chilly Poultry Curry: Made by adding chilly Ingredients. They grant Link one level of Heat Resistance when eaten.

* Electro Poultry Curry: Made by adding electric Ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Poultry Curry: Made by adding enduring Ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely restored and overfilled by eating them.

* Energizing Poultry Curry: Made by adding energizing Ingredients. If eaten, they will refill part of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Poultry Curry: Made by adding hasty Ingredients. Consuming them will increase Link's movement speed.

* Hearty Poultry Curry: Made by adding hearty Ingredients. Eating these curries will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.

* Mighty Poultry Curry: Made by adding mighty Ingredients. Eating them will grant Link a boost to the power of his attacks.

* Sneaky Poultry Curry: Made by adding sneaky Ingredients. They can be eaten to increase Link's Stealth abilities.

* Spicy Poultry Curry: Made by adding spicy Ingredients. These curries can be eaten to grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.

* Tough Poultry Curry: Made by adding tough Ingredients. Link will gain a boost to his Defense when he eats these.
