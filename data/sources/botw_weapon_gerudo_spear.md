### Breath of the Wild
Gerudo Spear
Gerudo Spears are Weapons found in the Gerudo Highlands and Gerudo Desert, particularly around Gerudo Town. They belong to the Gerudo Gear equipment archetype, a series of Weapons created and used by the Gerudo. Due to this, Gerudo Soldiers commonly wield these Spears to keep out intruders. Their center of gravity is in their tips, making them difficult for anyone other than skilled Gerudo warriors to use.

The following Gerudo wield Gerudo Spears:
* Babi
* Barta
* Bertri
* Dorrah
* Katta
* Kotta
* Laine
* Lashley
* Leena
* Marta
* Merina
* Ploka
* Reeza
* Ripp
* Smaude

Being metallic Weapons, Gerudo Spears will attract Lightning if they are equipped during Thunderstorms or conduct Electricity. They are also incapable of burning if they are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. If they are dropped in the Water, Gerudo Spears will sink, but they can be picked up using the Magnesis Rune.
