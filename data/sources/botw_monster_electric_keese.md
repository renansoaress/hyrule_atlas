### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Electric Keese, also known as Thunder Keese, are recurring Enemies in série The Legend of Zelda. They are an electrified variant of Keese.

### Breath of the Wild
Electric Keese
Electric Keese appear in Breath of the Wild, most commonly is Regions such as East Necluda, the Lanayru Great Spring, and the Gerudo Desert. They can be found hanging from the ceilings of caves at all hours of the day, but they also fly around at night. In addition to this, Electric Wizzrobes on Thunder Wizzrobes are capable of summoning Electric Keese.

The Electricity in the bodies of Electric Keese are known to make them more dangerous than regular Keese. When attacking Link, they have the ability to shock him, which will stun him and cause him to drop any metal Weapons he may be holding. Upon their defeat, Electric Keese can drop Keese Eyeballs or Electric Keese Wings.
