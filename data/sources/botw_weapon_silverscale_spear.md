## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Silverscale Spear
Silverscale Spears are spears used by the Zora with a fish-tail design. They belong to the Zora Gear equipment archetype, which consists of Weapons created and used by the Zora. Their strength is known to be impressive, being able to pierce the toughest scales. The male guards of Zora's Domain, such as Bazz, use the Silverscale Spear. However, Link cannot pick them up, and attacking the guards will result in retaliation.

An unoccupied Silverscale Spear can be found under the flowing Water of Zora's Domain. They are also used by Lizalfos in the surrounding Lanayru Great Spring, as well as in the Akkala Highlands. A Silverscale Spear is wielded by a Black Moblin in the Dining Hall of Hyrule Castle. More Silverscale Spears can be obtained in Treasure Chests dropped when the Breath of the Wild Series Mipha amiibo is scanned with the amiibo Rune.

Silverscale Spears are made of metal, which means they will conduct Electricity and attract Lightning if wielded during Thunderstorms. They can also be picked up using the Magnesis Rune, but they will sink if they are dropped in Water. These Spears are also unable to burn when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate.
