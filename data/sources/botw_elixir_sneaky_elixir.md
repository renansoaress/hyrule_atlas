### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Sneaky Elixirs are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
By drinking Sneaky Elixirs, Link can receive a boost to his Stealth. They are capable of calming his nerves and silencing his footfalls, which allows him to be able to move undetected amongst Monsters and Creatures.

Sneaky Elixirs come in three varying levels of efficiency. They can be made by Cooking any of the following critters with Monster Parts in a Cooking Pot:

* Sunset Firefly

By adding any of these sneaky ingredients, Link can make a Sneaky Elixir that will also replenish his Hearts:

* Blue Nightshade

* Silent Princess

* Silent Shroom

* Sneaky River Snail

* Stealthfin Trout
