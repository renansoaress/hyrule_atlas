### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Meat Skewers,(BotW, TotK) also known as Zesty Meat Skewers,(Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity) are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Meat Skewers can be made by Cooking one to three different pieces of Meat together in a Cooking Pot. When eaten, they will restore some of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge. These skewers are juicy and filling snacks that are made with grilled Meat.

By adding more ingredients, different variations of Meat Skewers can be made:

* Chilly Meat Skewer: Made by adding chilly ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one level of Heat Resistance.

* Electro Meat Skewer: Made by adding electric ingredients. They grant Link one level of Shock Resistance if they're consumed.

* Enduring Meat Skewer: Made by adding enduring ingredients. These skewers will both refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel when they are eaten.

* Energizing Meat Skewer: Made by adding energizing ingredients. If eaten, they'll refill part of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Meat Skewer: Made by adding hasty ingredients. Link gains an increase to his speed when he eats these.

* Hearty Meat Skewer: Made by adding hearty ingredients. They heal all of Link's Hearts when eaten and will temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.

* Mighty Meat Skewer: Made by adding mighty ingredients. If eaten, the power of Link's attacks will be boosted.

* Sneaky Meat Skewer: Made by adding sneaky ingredients. Eating these skewers will boost Link's Stealth for a short period.

* Spicy Meat Skewer: Made by adding spicy ingredients. When eaten, they'll grant Link one level of Cold Resistance temporarily.

* Tough Meat Skewer: Made by adding tough ingredients. They'll increase Link's defense when he eats them.
