## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Savage Lynel Crusher
Savage Lynel Crushers are Weapons used by high-tier Lynels, notably White-Maned Lynels and Silver Lynels, as well as Golden Lynels in Master Mode. They were forged by Lynels using metal from Death Mountain and are known to have a powerful downward swing. Due to being made by Lynels, they are part of the Lynel Gear equipment archetype. They are commonly used in the Eldin Mountains and Hebra Mountains.

The Lynels who use Savage Lynel Crushers often swing the Weapon down to the ground, creating a hemisphere of Fire, or use it to spin and knock out Link. They are wielded with two hands by Link, as they are incredibly long and heavy. It is among one of the most powerful Weapons in the game, having a base attack damage of 78.

Savage Lynel Crushers are made of metal, and therefore will conduct Electricity and attract Lightning during Thunderstorms, but they can be picked up with the Magnesis Rune. They will not be burned by Fire or a Scorching Climate, and they will sink if they are dropped in Water.

## Trivia
* The Savage Lynel Crusher is one of a few Weapons stronger than the upgraded Master Sword, the others being the Royal Guard's Claymore and the Ancient Battle Axe++.
