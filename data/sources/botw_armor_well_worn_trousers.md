## Location and Uses
The Well-Worn Trousers are a heavily-worn piece of Armor that were made long before Link's awakening from the Slumber of Restoration. Due to their weathered condition and short legs, they appear to not have been made for Link at his age. The Well-Worn Trousers provide no additional benefits from wearing them other than marginally boosting Link's defense. They can be obtained either from a stone Treasure Chest inside the Shrine of Resurrection or from Granté in Tarrey Town after the "From the Ground Up" Side Quest is completed.
