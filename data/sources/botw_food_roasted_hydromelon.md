Roasted Hydromelons are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Hydromelons are a type of Roasted Food, resulting from Hydromelons that have made contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. Roasted Hydromelons have soft, flaky flesh and an intriguing flavor. Link can restore three quarters of a Heart to his Life Gauge by eating a Roasted Hydromelon.
