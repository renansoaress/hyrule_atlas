### Breath of the Wild
Bright-Chested Duck
Bright-Chested Ducks are common Birds that can be found in small flocks in bodies of Water throughout Hyrule Kingdom. They spend most of their time in the Water, paddling with their webbed feet, and occasionally dive the fronts of their bodies underwater to feed. When startled, the flock will fly away. Their meat is very fatty, and is considered to be high-quality food. When killed, Bright-Chested Ducks drop a Raw Bird Drumstick.
