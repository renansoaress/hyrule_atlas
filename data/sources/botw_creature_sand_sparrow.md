### Breath of the Wild
Sand Sparrow
Sand Sparrows are a breed of Sparrow native to the Gerudo Desert. They have adapted to the intense Heat and aridity of their home, and they bury themselves in the Sand and remain immobile in order to avoid predators. They gather in small flocks on the ground, but they will fly away if they are disturbed. Sand Sparrows drop Chickaloo Tree Nuts when they are killed.
