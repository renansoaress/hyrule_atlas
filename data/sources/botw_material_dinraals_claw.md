### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Dinraal's Claws are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Dinraal's Claws can be collected from the Dragon Dinraal, who typically flies around the Eldin Mountains and the Tabantha Frontier. Link must shoot its hands with an Arrow. The Claws in question will fall onto the land in the form of light.

Dinraal's Claws that are used in a Cooking Pot will make Dubious Food. However, when used with critters, the result will be Elixirs. At the Kochi Dye Shop, Dinraal's Claws are one of the ingredients that can be used to make Crimson Dye. They also count as Monster Parts, which means they can be exchanged for Mon at the Fang and Bone.
