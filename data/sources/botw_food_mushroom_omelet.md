### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Mushroom Omelets are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Mushroom Omelets are Meals made by Cooking any Mushroom, a Bird Egg, Goat Butter, and Rock Salt together in a Cooking Pot. The regular version can be made either by using Hylian Shrooms, which offer no status effects, or by using multiple mushrooms with differing effects. Eating a Mushroom Omelet will heal some of Link's Hearts. The fluffy texture is considered to be a highlight of Mushroom Omelets.

Either by using certain mushrooms or through the additional of more Ingredients, Link can create several variations of Mushroom Omelet:

* Chilly Mushroom Omelet: Made by using Chillshrooms or other chilly Ingredients. Link will gain one level of Heat Resistance if he eats these omelets.

* Electro Mushroom Omelet: Made by using Zapshrooms or other electric Ingredients. When eaten, Link will be granted one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Mushroom Omelet: Made by using Endura Shrooms or other enduring Ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely restored and overfilled when he eats these.

* Energizing Mushroom Omelet: Made by using Stamella Shrooms or other energizing Ingredients. Eating these omelets will refill part of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Mushroom Omelet: Made by using Rushrooms or other hasty Ingredients. Consuming these will increase Link's movement speed.

* Hearty Mushroom Omelet: Made by using Hearty Truffles, Big Hearty Truffles, or other hearty Ingredients. When eaten, all of Link's Hearts will be healed and he will gain Extra Hearts.

* Mighty Mushroom Omelet: Made by using Razorshrooms or other mighty Ingredients. By eating these omelets, Link's attack power will be increased.

* Sneaky Mushroom Omelet: Made by using Silent Shrooms or other sneaky Ingredients. Eating them will boost Link's Stealth.

* Spicy Mushroom Omelet: Made by using Sunshrooms or other spicy Ingredients. Link will gain one level of Cold Resistance when he eats these omelets.

* Tough Mushroom Omelet: Made by using Ironshrooms or other tough Ingredients. When eaten, they will boost Link's Defense.
