### Breath of the Wild
Sunshroom
Sunshrooms are a type of Mushroom that can found growing near Trees in Eldin Canyon and the Gerudo Highlands. They can be purchased from The Slippery Falcon and Ardin's Stall in Gerudo Town. They can be eaten as is to restore half a Heart to Link's Life Gauge, but they can also be Cooked into Food to restore greater health and provide Cold Resistance. When Sunshrooms are burned by Fire, they become Toasty Sunshrooms. In addition, Sunshrooms can be used as an ingredient at the Kochi Dye Shop to create Red Dye.

After Link upgrades his Sheikah Sensor at the Hateno Ancient Tech Lab, Symin will ask to see it. When Link speaks to Symin next, he decides to demonstrate how he can use Sheikah Sensor to find Items that are depicted in the Hyrule Compendium. He tasks Link with taking a Picture of a Sunshroom, pointing out that one can be found behind the Lab. This begins the "Sunshroom Sensing" Side Quest.

Once Link returns with a Picture of a Sunshroom, Symin will ask to see it to confirm. He is pleased to see the Picture, and he then tasks Link to tune his Sheikah Sensor to his Hyrule Compendium Picture of the Sunshroom. He then asks Link to bring him three Sunshrooms using the Sheikah Sensor. After gathering three Sunshrooms, Link can return to Symin and hand them over. When Link does so, Symin thanks Link and gives him three Hearty Truffles as a reward.

Another Side Quest, "Riddles of Hyrule," involves Walton telling Link riddles and having him bring the corresponding Item. For his third riddle, Walton will ask Link to find a Mushroom that warms one's bones and can be found in warmer areas. If Link places a Sunshroom on the leaf in front of Walton, he will cheer that Link figured out the riddle.

Sunshrooms, among other Mushrooms, can be used in a Cooking Pot to make the following Meals:

- Cream of Mushroom Soup | Any Mushroom, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Fish and Mushroom Skewer | Any Fish, Any Mushroom
- Fragrant Mushroom Sauté | Goron Spice, Any Mushroom
- Fruit and Mushroom Mix | Any Fruit, Any Mushroom
- Glazed Mushrooms | Courser Bee Honey, Any Mushroom
- Meat and Mushroom Skewer | Any Meat, Any Mushroom
- Mushroom Omelet | Any Mushroom, Bird Egg, Goat Butter, Rock Salt
- Mushroom Rice Balls | Hylian Rice, Any Mushroom
- Mushroom Risotto | Hylian Rice, Goat Butter, Rock Salt, Any Mushroom
- Mushroom Skewer | Any Mushroom
- Copious Mushroom Skewers | Any Mushrooms (4 different kinds)
- Salt-Grilled Mushrooms | Any Mushroom, Rock Salt
- Steamed Mushrooms | Any Mushroom, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower

## Trivia
* Due to a Picture of a Sunshroom being required as part of the "Sunshroom Sensing" Side Quest, it will automatically be registered to the Hyrule Compendium in Breath of the Wild. This also means that Link will not be able to purchase its Compendium entry from Symin, as he only sells them after the Side Quest has been completed. The default image still exists in the game's files, however.
