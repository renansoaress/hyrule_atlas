### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Wooden Mops are recurring Items and Objects in série The Legend of Zelda.(BotW, TotK, AoC) Wooden Mops go unnamed in Skyward Sword.

### Breath of the Wild
Wooden Mop
Wooden Mops are commonly used to clean up around households in Hyrule. Therefore, they can be found in villages such as Hateno Village and ruins within East Necluda and Hyrule Field. Despite their intended purpose, Wooden Mops have a sturdy construction, giving them some merit in combat.

Being made of Wood, Wooden Mops will float when dropped in Water. They are also unable to conduct Electricity or attract Lightning when equipped during Thunderstorms, but they will burn when exposed to a Fire or a Scorching Climate.

Wooden Mops belong to the Daily Use Goods equipment archetype, which consists of tools used for the upkeep of settlements throughout Hyrule Kingdom.
