## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Mighty Lynel Spear
Mighty Lynel Spears are Weapons used by Blue-Maned Lynels found throughout Hyrule Kingdom. In Master Mode, though, they are used by White-Maned Lynels. Both the weight and cutting edge of these Spears have been enhanced by Lynels. They belong to the Lynel Gear equipment archetype, which includes all Weapons created by Lynels. Though they are heavy for a Hylian, Lynels are strong enough to cleave through rocks with them.

Due to their metallic nature, Mighty Lynel Spears will attract Lightning if they are equipped during Thunderstorms and conduct Electricity. They will also sink if they are dropped in Water, but the Magnesis Rune is able to pick them up. They are unable to burn if they are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate.

## Trivia
* Mighty Lynel Spears are one of several Permanently Missable Items in Breath of the Wild. They stop appearing once Blue-Maned Lynels scale up to White-Maned Lynels, and in Master Mode, the same thing happens when White-Maned Lynels scale up to Silver Lynels.
