### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Curry Rices are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Curry Rices are made by Cooking Hylian Rice and Goron Spice together in a Cooking Pot. They can be eaten to restore two Hearts to Link, and they also sell for 20 Rupees. They are a favorite all over Hyrule, noted to be a simple dish with a flavor people do not get tired of.

Curry Rice is mentioned as part of the "Curry for What Ails You" Side Quest. Lester, a Stable Worker at Rito Stable, enjoys spice, specifically Curry Rice. While he has Hylian Rice, he does not have any Goron Spice. He will request Link bring him Goron Spice, which begins the "Curry for What Ails You" Side Quest. As a reward for completing the quest, Lester will give Link 50 Rupees.

Only fruits (and Courser Bee Honey) can be added to Curry Rice. Otherwise, the resulting dishes will be different Meals. Using additional Ingredients will create the following variations:

* Hearty Curry Rice: Made by using Hearty Durians. They heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum number of Hearts when eaten. They are worth 50 Rupees when sold.

* Energizing Curry Rice: Made by adding Courser Bee Honey. When eaten, they restore a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Curry Rice: Made by using Fleet-Lotus Seeds. When eaten, Link's speed will be temporarily boosted. They heal three of Link's Hearts and sell for 30 Rupees.

* Spicy Curry Rice: Made by using Spicy Pepper. Eating one will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance. They restore three Hearts and sell for 30 Rupees.

* Electro Curry Rice: Made by using Voltfruit. If they are eaten, Link will gain one level of Shock Resistance. They heal six of Link's Hearts and can also be sold for 30 Rupees.

* Mighty Curry Rice: Made by using Mighty Bananas. Eating these will temporarily boost the power of Link's attacks. They will also restore three of Link's Hearts, and they sell for 30 Rupees.

* Chilly Curry Rice: Made by using Hydromelons. When they are eaten, Link will gain one level of Heat Resistance for a short period of time. They heal three Hearts and sell for 30 Rupees.
