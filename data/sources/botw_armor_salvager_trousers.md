## Location and Uses
The Salvager Trousers are a piece of Armor in Breath of the Wild, part of the Salvager Armor Set. They are obtained from a Treasure Chest as part of the "[Xenoblade Chronicles 2]" Side Quest. The Chest crashes on a plateau on the southern side of the Hebra East Summit after Link looks to the southeastern sky from the Hebra Peak during the night. After obtaining the Salvager Trousers, Granté will begin selling them for 50 Rupees. When worn, the Salvager Trousers provide one level of Swim Speed Up. The Salvager Trousers cannot be Enhanced by a Great Fairy, nor can they be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.
