### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Salt-Grilled Mushrooms are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Salt-Grilled Mushrooms are made by Cooking any Mushroom with Rock Salt in a Cooking Pot. The default can be made either by using Hylian Shrooms, which offer no status effects, or by using two types of Mushrooms with differing effects. Eating them will restore some of Link's Hearts. These Meals are noted to be simple, their preparation only requiring one to lightly salt Mushrooms before grilling them.

Based on both the type of Mushroom added and the inclusion of other Ingredients, the following variations of Salt-Grilled Mushrooms can be made:

* Hearty Salt-Grilled Mushrooms: Made by using Hearty Truffles, Big Hearty Truffles, or other hearty Ingredients. When eaten, they will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him Extra Hearts.

* Energizing Salt-Grilled Mushrooms: Made by using Stamella Shrooms or other energizing Ingredients. They can be eaten to refill part of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Enduring Salt-Grilled Mushrooms: Made by using Endura Shrooms or other enduring Ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel can be completely restored and overfilled by eaten these meals.

* Hasty Salt-Grilled Mushrooms: Made by using Rushrooms or other hasty Ingredients. If they're eaten, Link will receive a boost to his movement speed.

* Spicy Salt-Grilled Mushrooms: Made by using Sunshrooms or other spicy Ingredients. They grant Link one level of Cold Resistance when they're eaten.

* Electro Salt-Grilled Mushrooms: Made by using Zapshrooms or other electric Ingredients. Link can eat them to receive one level of Shock Resistance.

* Mighty Salt-Grilled Mushrooms: Made by using Razorshrooms or other mighty Ingredients. By eating them, Link will receive a boost to the power of his attacks.

* Tough Salt-Grilled Mushrooms: Made by using Ironshrooms or other tough Ingredients. When Link eats them, he will gain a boost to his defensive abilities.

* Sneaky Salt-Grilled Mushrooms: Made by using Silent Shrooms or other sneaky Ingredients. Link's Stealth will be boosted if he eats these meals.

* Chilly Salt-Grilled Mushrooms: Made by using Chillshrooms or other chilly Ingredients. They can be eaten to grant Link one level of Heat Resistance.
