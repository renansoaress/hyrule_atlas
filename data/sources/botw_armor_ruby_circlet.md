### Breath of the Wild
Ruby Circlets are a type of Circlet Accessory that is worn on Link's head. They can be purchased at the Starlight Memories Jewelry Shop at Gerudo Town for 500 Rupees and three Rubies. Alternatively, at the end of the "Tools of the Trade" Side Quest, a Ruby Circlet is one of the Accessories that Isha offers to make for Link as a reward. When worn, Ruby Circlets provide one level of Cold Resistance to Link. They also provide the hidden Added Effect Ancient Pow Up, increasing damage done by Ancient Soldier Gear and Guardian Gear by 20%.

Ruby Circlets can be enhanced by a Great Fairy at a Great Fairy Fountain. However, they cannot be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.
