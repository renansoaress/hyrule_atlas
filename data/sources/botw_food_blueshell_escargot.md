## Location and Uses
Blueshell Escargots are a type of Roasted Food, resulting from Hearty Blueshell Snails that have made contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. Blueshell Escargots are known for having a trademark bitter taste. Link can restore four and a half Hearts to his Life Gauge by eating Blueshell Escargot.
