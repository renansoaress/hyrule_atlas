### Breath of the Wild
Seared Steaks are a variety of Roasted Food. They are the result of Raw Meat that has made contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. They can also be found around Monster Strongholds being cooked over a Campfire, and they have a chance to be dropped by Black Hinoxes upon their death. The Protein Palace, Estan's Stall in Gerudo Town, and Maike in Kara Kara Bazaar all sell Seared Steaks. After being Cooked, their flavor increases. Link can eat one to restore one and a half Hearts to his Life Gauge.
