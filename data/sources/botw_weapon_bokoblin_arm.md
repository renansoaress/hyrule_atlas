Bokoblin Arms are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Bokoblin Arm
Bokoblin Arms are the arms of Stalkoblins that they drop when they are defeated. Even though they have been removed from their bodies, they continue to move on their own. Due to their old age, they break quickly.

Being made from bone, Bokoblin Arms will float if they are dropped in Water. They do not conduct Electricity or attract Lightning, making them a good choice to use during Thunderstorms. If they are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate, they will not burn like a wooden Weapon would.
