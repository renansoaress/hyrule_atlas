### Breath of the Wild
Tireless Frog
Tireless Frogs are a type of Frog that can be found near ponds in the Lanayru Great Spring and Hyrule Ridge, but they only appear during the Rain. When Link approaches a Tireless Frog, it will run away. To circumvent this, Link can sneak up to them, stun them, or chase after them. Tireless Frogs can be combined with Monster Parts in a Cooking Pot to make an Enduring Elixir, which will grant Extra Stamina when consumed. At the Kochi Dye Shop, Tireless Frogs can be used to make Crimson Dye.
