Perfect Dodging is a recurring ability in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Perfect Dodging is accomplished by jumping out of the way of an Enemy's attack at the last moment. Doing so will temporarily slow down time, allowing Link to quickly strike Enemies with a Flurry Rush. A Flurry Rush is a rapid string of attacks, the quantity depending on what type of Weapon he has equipped at the start of the attack string. One-Handed Weapons have a seven-hit string, Two-Handed Weapons have a four-hit string, and Spears have a nine-hit combo. After performing a Flurry Rush with a Two-Handed Weapon, assuming Link does not attack the fourth time, he will require a short period of time to recover.
