### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Toasty Stamella Shrooms are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Toasty Stamella Shrooms are a type of Roasted Food, and they are made by burning Stamella Shrooms with Fire or a Scorching Climate. Having lost the special properties of Stamella Shrooms, Toasty Stamella Shrooms are only capable of healing. They restore three-quarters of a Heart to Link when eaten.
