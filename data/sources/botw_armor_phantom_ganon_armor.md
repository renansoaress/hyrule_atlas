### Breath of the Wild
The Phantom Ganon Armor is a piece of Armor exclusive to  Downloadable Content pack. Obtaining the Phantom Ganon Armor is one part of the objective of the "EX Treasure: Dark Armor" Side Quest. The Armor was stolen from Hyrule Castle by Misko and a clue to its location can be found in Misko's EX Journal 2 at the Deya Village Ruins. The metallic Treasure Chest that hides the Phantom Ganon Armor can be found submerged in Floria River underneath Sarjon Bridge. Link must use the Magnesis Rune to pull the Chest from the River to retrieve it.

When worn, the Armor provides one level of Stealth Up. The Phantom Ganon Armor cannot be enhanced by a Great Fairy nor can it be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Phantom Ganon Armor, alongside the Phantom Ganon Skull and Phantom Ganon Greaves, compose the Phantom Ganon Set. When the Phantom Ganon Armor is worn with the Bokoblin Mask, Flamebreaker Helm, Salvager Headwear, Zant's Helmet, Hylian Hood, or Ravio's Hood, its cape will be removed.

According to its description, it is believed that the Phantom Ganon Armor is infused with a dark entity that resembles the Great King of Evil. After obtaining the Phantom Ganon Armor, Granté in Tarrey Town will begin selling it for 50 Rupees.
