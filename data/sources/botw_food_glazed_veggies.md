### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Glazed Veggies are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Glazed Veggies are made by Cooking any Vegetables, herb, or Flower in a Cooking Pot with Courser Bee Honey. Regular Glazed Veggies can be made by using Ingredients with a different effect than the Courser Bee Honey. When eaten, they will restore some of Link's Hearts. Glazed Veggies are Veggies sautéed with Honey for a salty-sweet flavor, which makes them ideal for those who dislike the taste of vegetables.

Due to the limitations of their Recipe, only one variation of Glazed Veggies exists:

* Energizing Glazed Veggies: Made using Hyrule Herbs, which doesn't have any effects. Consuming these Veggies will refill a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.
