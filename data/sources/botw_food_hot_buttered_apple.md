### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Hot Buttered Apples are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Hot Buttered Apples are made by Cooking an Apple and Goat Butter together in a Cooking Pot. When eaten, they will heal several of Link's Hearts. The sweetness of the Apple has been enhanced by smothering it with Goat Butter and baking it.

During the Side Quest "Cooking with Koko", Koko decides to make Hot Buttered Apples, since her mother used to make them and her sister Cottla loves them. However, she realizes that she has run out of Goat Butter, and therefore cannot make the Meal. When Link brings her Goat Butter, Koko will be excited, thanking him and making sure he means it. She then makes a Hot Buttered Apple, which she gives to Link as a reward.

Additional Ingredients can be added when making Hot Buttered Apples, which can result in one of the following variations:

* Chilly Hot Buttered Apple: Made by adding cold Ingredients. If eaten, Link will gain one level of Heat Resistance.

* Electro Hot Buttered Apple: Made by adding electric Ingredients. They grant Link one level of Shock Resistance when eaten.

* Enduring Hot Buttered Apple: Made by adding enduring Ingredients. They completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel when consumed.

* Energizing Hot Buttered Apple: Made by adding energizing Ingredients. If eaten, they will replenish a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Hot Buttered Apple: Made by adding hasty Ingredients. When eaten, they temporarily increase Link's speed.

* Hearty Hot Buttered Apple: Made by adding hearty Ingredients. Eating them will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.

* Mighty Hot Buttered Apple: Made by adding mighty Ingredients. Link gains a boost to the power of his attacks when he eats these apples.

* Sneaky Hot Buttered Apple: Made by adding sneaky Ingredients. When eaten, they grant Link a boost to his Stealth temporarily.

* Spicy Hot Buttered Apple: Made by adding spicy Ingredients. Eating these apples will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.

* Tough Hot Buttered Apple: Made by adding tough Ingredients. They can be eaten to increase Link's Defense for a short period of time.
