### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Energizing Elixirs are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
When drunk, Energizing Elixirs will partially refill Link's Stamina Wheel. They're useful for performing physical actions, such as climbing walls or swimming.

Energizing Elixirs can be made by Cooking any of the following critters with Monster Parts in a Cooking Pot:

* Energetic Rhino Beetle

* Restless Cricket

By adding any of these energizing ingredients, Link can make an Energizing Elixir that will also heal him:

* Stamella Shroom

* Courser Bee Honey

* Staminoka Bass

* Bright-Eyed Crab
