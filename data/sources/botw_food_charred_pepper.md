### Breath of the Wild
Charred Peppers are a type of Roasted Food, being the result of Spicy Peppers that have made contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. The roasting process is said to remove some of the spiciness of the Pepper. Link can heal three quarters of a Heart by eating a Charred Pepper.
