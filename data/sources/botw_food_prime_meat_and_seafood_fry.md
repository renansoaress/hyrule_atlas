### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Prime Meat and Seafood Fries are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Prime Meat and Seafood Fries are Meals that can be made by Cooking either Raw Prime Meat or a Raw Bird Thigh with any Seafood in a Cooking Pot. They can be eaten to restore some of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge. These Meals are considered comfort Foods, containing choice cuts of both Meat and Seafood.

Based on both the type of seafood used and whether any additional Ingredients are added, Link can make several variations of Prime Meat and Seafood Fry:

* Chilly Prime Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by using Chillfin Trouts or other chilly Ingredients. Eating these fries will grant Link one level of Heat Resistance.

* Electro Prime Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by using Voltfin Trout or other electric Ingredients. Link can eat these fries to gain one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Prime Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by using enduring Ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely replenished and overfilled by eating these.

* Energizing Prime Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by using Staminoka Basses, Bright-Eyed Crabs, or other energizing Ingredients. Eating these fries will refill part of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Prime Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by using hasty Ingredients. By eating them, Link's movement speed will be increased.

* Hearty Prime Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by using Hearty Salmons, Hearty Bass, Hearty Blueshell Snails, or other hearty Ingredients. When eaten, they heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.

* Mighty Prime Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by using Mighty Carps, Mighty Porgies, Razorclaw Crabs, or other mighty Ingredients. Eating them will increase Link's attack power.

* Sneaky Prime Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by using Stealthfin Trouts, Sneaky River Snails, or other sneaky Ingredients. If eaten, Link will have his Stealth increased.

* Spicy Prime Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by using Sizzlefin Trouts or other spicy Ingredients. If eaten, Link will gain one level of Cold Resistance.

* Tough Prime Meat and Seafood Fry: Made by using Armored Carp, Armored Porgies, Ironshell Crabs, or other tough Ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain a boost to his Defense.
