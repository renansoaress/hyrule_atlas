## Location and Uses
Rudania's Emblems are collected as part of the Main Quest "EX Champion Daruk's Song", which is exclusive to  DLC Pack. They're given to Link by the Monks of Kamia Omuna Shrine, Sharo Lun Shrine, and Rinu Honika Shrine after he completes them. After completing the third Shrine, a Monk will direct Link to Divine Beast Vah Rudania.

Once he arrives, Link will hear the voice of a Monk offer him a trial in the Illusory Realm in exchange for all of his Emblems. He'll be transported inside Rudania, where he will once again fight Fireblight Ganon. After defeating it, however, Link may challenge it again at any time.
