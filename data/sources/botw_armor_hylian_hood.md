### Breath of the Wild
The Hylian Hood is a piece of Armor that can be purchased at the Ventest Clothing Boutique in Hateno Village or Enchanted in Kakariko Village. According to its description, it is made from a sturdy material to protect travelers from the harsh sun and weather. It can be enhanced by a Great Fairy and can be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Hylian Hood, alongside the Hylian Tunic and Hylian Trousers, compose the Hylian Armor Series.
