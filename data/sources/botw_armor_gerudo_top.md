## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
The Gerudo Top can be acquired from Vilia on top of the Kara Kara Bazaar, who sells the top along with the rest of the set for 600 Rupees as part of the Forbidden City Entry Main Quest. It can also be purchased by itself at Fashion Passion in Gerudo Town for 180 Rupees. It provides Link with 3 defense, and if the entire Gerudo Outfit is worn, Link will have level 1 Heat Resistance, even though the pieces of the set cannot be upgraded at a Great Fairy Fountain.
