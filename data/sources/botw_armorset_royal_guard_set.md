### Breath of the Wild
The Royal Guard Set is exclusive to  Downloadable Content Pack. It is a set of Armor originally worn by members of the Royal Guard that can be found as part of the "EX Royal Guard Rumors" Side Quest. It consists of the Royal Guard Cap, the Royal Guard Uniform and the Royal Guard Boots. Wearing the full set confers the effects of Charge Atk. Stamina Up, Swim Dash Stamina Up, and Climb Jump Stamina Up, with the Inventory only displaying Charge Atk. Stamina Up. These reduce the Stamina cost of charge attacks and jumping while climbing by 50%, and the cost of dashing or spin attacking while swimming by 75%. The Armor Set does not apply any other effects. It cannot be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop, nor can it be enhanced by a Great Fairy.
