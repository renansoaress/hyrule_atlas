Extravagant Saddles are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
The Extravagant Saddle is one of several Saddles that Link can obtain. They can be won by beating Blynne's record of one minute and fifteen seconds at the Obstacle Course. This type of Saddle is commonly used in festivals. Its delicate embroidery and Gem arrangements leaves a lasting impression, making it perfect for standing out.

The Extravagant Saddle is a piece of Extravagant Gear, which is a set of Horse Gear. Link can equip the Extravagant Saddle to his Horses by speaking to workers at Highland Stable, Outskirt Stable, South Akkala Stable, or Woodland Stable. Similar to other Saddles, they are unable to be worn by Epona or the Giant Horse.
