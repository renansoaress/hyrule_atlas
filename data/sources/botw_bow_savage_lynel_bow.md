### Breath of the Wild
Savage Lynel Bow
Savage Lynel Bows are Bows found in the Hebra Mountains and Hyrule Field. White-Maned Lynels, Silver Lynels, and Golden Lynels all use them when fighting. These Bows are part of the Lynel Gear equipment archetype, which is a group of Weapons created by Lynels. They are forged with a special steel found at the peak of Death Mountain, and they have the strength to pierce thick Armor.

Being made of metal, Savage Lynel Bows will conduct Electricity and attract Lightning if they are equipped during a Thunderstorm. They also sink if they are dropped in Water, but they can be picked up with the Magnesis Rune. Finally, Savage Lynel Bows will not burn if they are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate.
