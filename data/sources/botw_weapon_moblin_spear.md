### Breath of the Wild
Moblin Spear
Moblin Spears are wooden Weapons carried by regular Moblins, particularly in the Faron Grasslands and Gerudo Highlands. They are roughly fashioned and created from whittling Trees, and they therefore have both low Durability and little strength. They belong to the Moblin Gear equipment archetype, which includes all Weapons created by Moblins.

Being made of wood, Moblin Spears will float if they are dropped in the Water. However, they will also burn if they are exposed to Fire or equipped in a Scorching Climate. They will not conduct Electricity or attract Lightning during a Thunderstorm, and they are unable to be picked up with the Magnesis Rune.
