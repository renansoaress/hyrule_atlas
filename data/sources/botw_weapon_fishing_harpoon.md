### Breath of the Wild
Fishing Harpoon
Fishing Harpoons are tools used by fisherman to catch large Fish. They are commonly found around Lurelin Village in East Necluda, though they are also used by Lizalfos at Lake Hylia. Because of their sharp spearheads, Fishing Harpoons can be used as Weapons by Link.

Because of their metal spearheads, Fishing Harpoons will conduct Electricity and attract Lightning if they are equipped during a Thunderstorm. They also sink if they are dropped in Water, but they can be picked up and manipulated with the Magnesis Rune. If they are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate, Fishing Harpoons will not burn.
