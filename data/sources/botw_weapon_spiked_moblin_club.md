## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Spiked Moblin Club
Spiked Moblin Clubs are upgraded Moblin Clubs that have had Animal bones attached to them to increase their damage. They belong to the Moblin Gear equipment archetype, a series of Weapons created by Moblins. They are wielded most commonly by Blue Moblins, particularly in Hyrule Field and the Akkala Highlands.

Since they are made of wood, Spiked Moblin Clubs will float when dropped in Water. They will also burn when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. They do not conduct Lightning if they are equipped during a Thunderstorm or conduct Electricity, and the Magnesis Rune is unable to pick them up.
