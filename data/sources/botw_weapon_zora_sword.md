### Breath of the Wild
Zora Sword
Zora Swords are one-handed Weapons fashioned by the Zora. These Swords belong to the Zora Gear equipment archetype. They are forged from a type of durable and rust-resistant metal and ornamented with traditional Zora designs. The metal they are constructed from is known to be durable and rust-proof. They are commonly found within East Necluda and the Lanayru Great Spring, particularly in and around Zora's Domain.

Zora Swords are made of metal, and therefore they do not burn when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. They do, however, conduct Lightning if they are equipped during Thunderstorms, and they also conduct Electricity. If they are dropped in the Water, they will sink, but they can be picked up using the Magnesis Rune.
