### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Yellow Chuchu Jellies, also known as Yellow Chu Jellies, are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Yellow Chuchu Jelly is dropped by Electric Chuchus when killed. It is also dropped by other Chuchus if these are killed with electric weapons, and can be created when regular Chuchu Jelly is exposed to electricity. When struck by physical attacks, it releases a spherical electrical pulse. Like other Monster Parts, they can be combined with critters in a Cooking Pot to make Elixirs. They can also be used at the Kochi Dye Shop to make Yellow Dye or sold to Kilton at the Fang and Bone for Mon.

The Rubber Armor set requires Yellow Chuchu Jellies to be upgraded to its first and second tiers at a Great Fairy Fountain.
