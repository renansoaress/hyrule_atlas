### Breath of the Wild
Boulder Breaker
Boulder Breakers are Weapons that are part of the Goron Gear equipment archetype. They are the strongest variant of the Cobble Crusher. Just like the other Goron crushers, they are Two-Handed Weapons that can be used for both mining and combat. They also have a hidden property which causes their damage output to be doubled when used on the Ore Deposit atop a Stone Talus. They are capable of immediately destroying Stone Pebblits. Despite Bludo describing Boulder Breakers as sharp Weapons, they do not act as such and cannot cut Grass or Trees.

Prior to the Great Calamity, a Boulder Breaker was wielded by the Goron Champion, Daruk It was made for him by a blacksmith based out of Goron City. Daruk supposedly made swinging this Weapon around look easy, while others who used it would need a large amount of strength. According to Bludo, Daruk used his Boulder Breaker to carve the valley at Gortram Cliff in one night to make it easier for the Gorons to reach their Rock Roast. In addition, there is a rumor that Daruk used to use it to fan himself. During the EX Recovered Memory "Champion Daruk's Song", Daruk can be seen using his Boulder Breaker to fight off a group of Bokoblins harassing a Hylian Retriever.

After appeasing the Divine Beast Vah Rudania, Bludo will allow Link to take Daruk's Boulder Breaker from a Treasure Chest in his House as a reward. If Link ever misplaces or breaks the Boulder Breaker, Rohan will be able to craft him a new one in exchange for a Cobble Crusher, five pieces of Flint, and a Diamond. However, he will not make if the Boulder Breaker is still recoverable.

Boulder Breakers are made of metal, and they will therefore conduct Electricity and attract Lightning if they are equipped during a Thunderstorm. However, they are unable to burn when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. They sink if dropped in Water, but they can be picked up and manipulated using the Magnesis Rune.
