### Breath of the Wild
The Soldier's Helm is a part of the Soldier's Set, in combination with the Soldier's Armor and Soldier's Greaves. It is sold at Ventest Clothing Boutique in Hateno Village. It can be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop and upgraded at a Great Fairy Fountain. It offers no effect aside from defense.
