## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
The Gerudo Veil can be acquired from Vilia on top of the Kara Kara Bazaar Inn, who sells the Veil along with the rest of the Gerudo Outfit for 600 Rupees as part of the "Forbidden City Entry" Main Quest. It can also be purchased by itself from Saula at Fashion Passion in Gerudo Town for 180 Rupees. It provides Link with three Defense, and if the entire Armor Set is worn, Link will receive one level of Heat Resistance. However, the pieces of the set cannot be upgraded at a Great Fairy Fountain.
