Lizalfos Talons are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Lizalfos Talons are Monster Parts that are dropped by all types of Lizalfos and Stalizalfos upon their defeat. They can be combined with critters in a Cooking Pot to make an Elixir. They can also be sold to Kilton at the Fang and Bone for Mon or given to the Kochi Dye Shop to make Gray Dye.

The Zora Set requires five Lizalfos Talons, as well as five Hyrule Bass, to upgrade each piece to their second tiers at a Great Fairy Fountain.
