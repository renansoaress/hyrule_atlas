### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Armoranths are a type of Roasted Food. They are the result of Armoranths that have made contact with Fire or Scorching Climates. The plant's roasted fibers are said to be hard and chewy, but still easy to eat. Link can heal half a Heart by eating a Roasted Armoranth.
