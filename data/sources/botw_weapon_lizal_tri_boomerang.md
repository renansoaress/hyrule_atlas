## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Lizal Tri-Boomerang
Lizal Tri-Boomerangs are Boomerangs with three blades that are typically carried by Black Lizalfos. They are also used by Silver Lizalfos, Golden Lizalfos, Fire-Breath Lizalfos, Ice-Breath Lizalfos, and Electric Lizalfos. They can be thrown to attack Enemies at a range, but they must be caught before returning to Link or else he will take damage. Alternatively, they can be used as melee Weapons. These Boomerangs are part of the Lizalfos Gear equipment archetype. They are commonly found in the Hebra Mountains and the Akkala Highlands.

As metallic Weapons, Lizal Tri-Boomerangs will conduct Electricity and attract Lightning when equipped during Thunderstorms. They can also be picked up using the Magnesis Rune on the Sheikah Slate, but they will sink if they are dropped in Water.
