Glazed Mushrooms are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Glazed Mushrooms are meals made by Cooking any Mushroom together with Courser Bee Honey in a Cooking Pot. Their default version can only be made by using a Mushroom that has a differing effect to the Courser Bee Honey. Eating this meal will heal a few Hearts to Link's Life Gauge. The Courser Bee Honey gives the Mushrooms a sweet and complex taste with a savory finish.

Only one variation of Glazed Mushrooms exists:

* Energizing Glazed Mushrooms: Made using either Hylian Shrooms or Stamella Shrooms. If eaten, they will replenish a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.
