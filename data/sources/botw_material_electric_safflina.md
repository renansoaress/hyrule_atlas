Electric Safflinas are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Electric Safflina
Electric Safflinas can be located on Hyrule Ridge and in the Gerudo Desert. They can be found growing near the Great Fairy Fountain under the Gerudo Great Skeleton. If Link uses Electric Safflinas in a Meal made in a Cooking Pot, the resulting dish will grant him Shock Resistance when eaten. In addition, Electric Safflinas can be used to create Light Yellow Dye at the Kochi Dye Shop.

Like other Flowers, Electric Safflinas can be used in a Cooking Pot to make the following Meals:

- Cream of Mushroom Soup | Any Mushroom, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Cream of Vegetable Soup | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Creamy Meat Soup | Any Meat, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Creamy Seafood Soup | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Fried Wild Greens | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Copious Fried Wild Greens | Any Vegetables, Herbs, or Flowers(4 different kinds)
- Glazed Veggies | Courser Bee Honey, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Herb Sauté | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Goron Spice
- Salt-Grilled Greens | Rock Salt, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Steamed Fish | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable,, Herb, or Flower
- Steamed Fruit | Any Fruit, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Steamed Meat | Any Meat, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Pumpkins)
- Steamed Mushrooms | Any Mushroom, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Vegetable Omelet | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Bird Egg, Goat Butter, Rock Salt
- Veggie Rice Balls | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Hylian Rice
