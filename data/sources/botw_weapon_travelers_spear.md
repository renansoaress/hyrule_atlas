Traveler's Spears are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Traveler's Spear
Traveler's Spears are Weapons utilized by travelers to defend themselves from wild Animals. They are noted to be easy to hold and simple to use. They are commonly found in Hyrule Field and West Necluda.

When scanned with the amiibo Rune, the following amiibo have a chance to spawn a Treasure Chest containing a Traveler's Spear:
* série The Legend of Zelda Series Link (Link's Awakening) amiibo
* Breath of the Wild Series Mipha amiibo

Being made of metal, Traveler's Spears will conduct Electricity and attract Lightning when they are equipped during a Thunderstorm, though they will not burn when exposed to a Fire or a Scorching Climate. They can also be picked up using the Magnesis Rune on the Sheikah Slate. Finally, they will sink if they are dropped in Water.

Traveler's Spears belong to the equipment archetype known as Traveler's Gear, which is utilized for self-defense by travelers.
