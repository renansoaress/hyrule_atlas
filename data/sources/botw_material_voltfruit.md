Voltfruits are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.(BotW, TotK, AoC, AoI)

### Breath of the Wild
Voltfruit
Voltfruits can be found growing from Cacti within the Gerudo Desert, and they can be be bought at Lorn's Fruit Stand in Gerudo Town. Red Hinoxes can also drop Voltfruits upon their deaths. When eaten, Voltfruits provide Link with half a Heart. Voltfruits exposed to Fire become Roasted Voltfruit. Meals made using Voltfruits give Link Shock Resistance. The Kochi Dye Shop accepts them as an ingredient for Orange Dye.

A Voltfruit is needed in order to make Creamy Heart Soup.

- Creamy Heart Soup | Hydromelon, Voltfruit, Any Radish, Fresh Milk

They can also be used in Fruit-based Meals:

- Fruit and Mushroom Mix | Any Fruit, Any Mushroom
- Fruit Pie | Any Fruit (besides Apple), Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter
- Fruitcake | Apple or Wildberry, Any Fruit, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar
- Honeyed Fruits | Courser Bee Honey, Any Fruit (besides Apple)
- Simmered Fruit | Any Fruit
- Copious Simmered Fruit | Any Fruit (4 different kinds)
- Steamed Fruit | Any Fruit, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
