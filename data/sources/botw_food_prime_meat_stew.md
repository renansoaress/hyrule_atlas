### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Prime Meat Stews are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Link can make Prime Meat Stew by Cooking either Raw Prime Meat or a Raw Bird Thigh with Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, or Fresh Milk together in a Cooking Pot. Eating them will restore some of Link's Hearts. The savoriness of these Meals comes from letting the Meat simmer.

By adding a fifth Ingredient, several different variations of Prime Meat Stew can be made:

* Chilly Prime Meat Stew: Made by adding a chilly Ingredient. They grant Link one level of Heat Resistance when eaten.

* Electro Prime Meat Stew: Made by adding an electric Ingredient. If eaten, they grant Link one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Prime Meat Stew: Made by adding an enduring Ingredient. These stews will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Prime Meat Stew: Made by adding an energizing Ingredient. Link's Stamina Wheel can be refilled partially by eating these stews.

* Hasty Prime Meat Stew: Made by adding a hasty Ingredient. Consuming them will increase Link's movement speed.

* Hearty Prime Meat Stew: Made by adding a hearty Ingredient. When eaten, all of Link's Hearts will be healed and his maximum Hearts will be temporarily increased.

* Mighty Prime Meat Stew: Made by adding a mighty Ingredient. Eating these will provide a boost to the power of Link's attacks.

* Sneaky Prime Meat Stew: Made by adding a sneaky Ingredient. When these stews are eaten, they increase Link's Stealth abilities.

* Spicy Prime Meat Stew: Made by adding a spicy Ingredient. By eating them, Link will gain one level of Cold Resistance.

* Tough Prime Meat Stew: Made by adding a tough Ingredient. Link will receive a boost to his Defense by eating these.
