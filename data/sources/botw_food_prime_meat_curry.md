### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Prime Meat Curries are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Prime Meat Curries can be made by Cooking Raw Prime Meat, Hylian Rice, and Goron Spice together in a Cooking Pot. Link can eat these curries to have some of his Hearts restored. Since high-quality Meat is used when making these Meals, they have a deeper taste than other curries.

Link can add additional Ingredients when making Prime Meat Curry, which can result in the following variations:

* Chilly Prime Meat Curry: Made by adding chilly Ingredients. If eaten, Link will gain one level of Heat Resistance.

* Electro Prime Meat Curry: Made by adding electric Ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Prime Meat Curry: Made by adding enduring Ingredients. Consuming these will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Prime Meat Curry: Made by adding energizing Ingredients. These curries will refill part of Link's Stamina Wheel if eaten.

* Hasty Prime Meat Curry: Made by adding hasty Ingredients. Link will receive a boost to his movement speed by eating these.

* Hearty Prime Meat Curry: Made by adding hearty Ingredients. When eaten, all of Link's Hearts will be healed, and he will gain temporary Hearts.

* Mighty Prime Meat Curry: Made by adding mighty Ingredients. These curries will increase Link's attack power when they're consumed.

* Sneaky Prime Meat Curry: Made by adding sneaky Ingredients. When eaten, Link will receive a boost to his Stealth.

* Spicy Prime Meat Curry: Made by adding spicy Ingredients. If these curries are eaten, Link will gain one level of Cold Resistance.

* Tough Prime Meat Curry: Made by adding tough Ingredients. Link's Defense will be boosted if he eats these.
