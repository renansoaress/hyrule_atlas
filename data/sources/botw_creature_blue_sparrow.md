### Breath of the Wild
Blue Sparrow
Blue Sparrows are a type of Sparrow. They chiefly inhabit wetlands and watersides within the Lanayru, such as the Lanayru Wetlands, the Zora River, and Zora's Domain. They are also found in and around the Great Hyrule Forest. Blue Sparrows feed on waterside plants and bathe in shallow Water when safe. They can commonly be found in groups, but they will fly away if they are disturbed. When killed, Blue Sparrows drop a single Chickaloo Tree Nut.
