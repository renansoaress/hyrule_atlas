## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
A Warm Doublet can be acquired from the Old Man by either gifting him Spicy Meat and Seafood Fry at the Old Man's Cabin or the Forest of Spirits, or meeting him at the King's Tomb at the top of Mount Hylia. If Link leaves the Great Plateau without receiving the Warm Doublet from the Old Man, a Stone Chest will appear in the Old Man's Cabin containing the Warm Doublet. The Ventest Clothing Boutique in Hateno Village also sells the Warm Doublet. When worn, it provides Link with one level of Cold Resistance. While the Warm Doublet can not be upgraded, it can be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.
