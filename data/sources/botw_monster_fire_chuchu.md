### Breath of the Wild
Fire Chuchu
Fire Chuchus are flaming Chuchus that appear in hot places, such as the Eldin Region. Fire Wizzrobes and Meteo Wizzrobes are also capable of summoning them. They leave trails of Fire behind them as they move, igniting flammable materials such as Grass. Fire Chuchus come in three different sizes, with their strength correlating to how large they are.

Fire Chuchus attack by dashing forward and emitting a blast of Fire around them. If hit by their attack, Link will be temporarily set on Fire, but this can be avoided by wearing the full Fireproof Armor for the Fireproof Set Bonus. After attacking, Fire Chuchus temporarily lose their fiery coating and become a darker color, leaving them vulnerable. If they are defeated while on Fire, they will explode with a fiery blast. As a result, the Hyrule Compendium suggests fighting them at a distance by utilizing Spears or shooting Arrows. Fire Chuchus drop Red Chuchu Jelly after being defeated.
