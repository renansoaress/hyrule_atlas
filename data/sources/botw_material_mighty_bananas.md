### Breath of the Wild
Mighty Bananas
Mighty Bananas can be found growing in the tropical forests of West Necluda and on Eventide Island. They are also sold by Emri in Kara Kara Bazaar and dropped by regular Hinox. Eating one raw heals half of a Heart. When Cooked, the resulting Meal provides Link with the Attack Up Added Effect. This is due to the high amount of protein and calories present that they contain. Mighty Bananas exposed to Fire become Roasted Mighty Bananas. They can be used at the Kochi Dye Shop to make Yellow Dye.

Mighty Bananas are one of three foodstuffs favored by the Sheikah, with the others being Swift Carrots and Fortified Pumpkins. They are the clear obsession of the Yiga Clan, as the Yiga Clan Hideout contains many Mighty Bananas. In addition, all Yiga Footsoldiers and Yiga Blademasters drop them upon their defeat. The Yiga Blademasters patrolling the Yiga Clan Hideout will also deviate from their path to eat Mighty Bananas that are left on the ground. Monk Maz Koshia will also stop fighting and eat them should Link drop some during their fight.

Mighty Bananas are required to make Fried Bananas in a Cooking Pot.

- Fried Bananas | Mighty Bananas, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar

They can also be used in Fruit-based Meals:

- Fruit and Mushroom Mix | Any Fruit, Any Mushroom
- Fruit Pie | Any Fruit (besides Apple), Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter
- Fruitcake | Apple or Wildberry, Any Fruit, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar
- Honeyed Fruits | Courser Bee Honey, Any Fruit (besides Apple)
- Simmered Fruit | Any Fruit
- Copious Simmered Fruit | Any Fruit (4 different kinds)
- Steamed Fruit | Any Fruit, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
