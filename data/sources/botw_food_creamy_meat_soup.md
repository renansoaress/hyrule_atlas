### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Creamy Meat Soups are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Creamy Meat Soups are made by Cooking any Meat, Fresh Milk, Rock Salt, and any Vegetable, herb, or Flower. Regular Creamy Meat Soups are made by using Ingredients without effects or with contradicting effects. However, Endura Carrots, Swift Carrots, and Fortified Pumpkins cannot be used, as they will result in Veggie Cream Soup. They heal six of Link's Hearts, and they sell for 60 Rupees. These Soups are noted to be nutritious, containing a good amount of lightly-braised Meat and Vegetables.

Several different variations of Creamy Meat Soup can be made by using different Ingredients.

* Hearty Creamy Meat Soup: Made using Hearty Radishes, Big Hearty Radishes, or other hearty Ingredients. When consumed, they fully heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him Extra Hearts. They are worth 70 Rupees when sold.

* Energizing Creamy Meat Soup: Made by adding Courser Bee Honey. When consumed, they refill a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Creamy Meat Soup: Made using Swift Violets or other speed-boosting Ingredients. Eating one will temporarily boost Link's movement speed. They heal four of his Hearts and sell for 80 Rupees.

* Spicy Creamy Meat Soup: Made using Warm Safflinas or other spicy Ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance. They restore four Hearts to Link, and they sell for 60 Rupees.

* Electro Creamy Meat Soup: Made using Electric Safflinas or other electricity-related Ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one level of Shock Resistance for a short period of time. They heal four of his Hearts, but they can also be sold for 60 Rupees.

* Mighty Creamy Meat Soup: Made using Mighty Thistles or other attack-boosting Ingredients. Eating one will temporarily increase Link's attack power. They also heal four Hearts, and they sell for 60 Rupees.

* Tough Creamy Meat Soup: Made using Armoranths or other defensive Ingredients. When consumed, these soups increase Link's defense for a time. They heal four Hearts and sell for 60 Rupees.

* Sneaky Creamy Meat Soup: Made using Blue Nightshades, Silent Princesses, or other sneaky Ingredients. When eaten, Link's Stealth will be boosted for a short while. They heal four of Link's Hearts, but they also can be sold for 60 Rupees.

* Chilly Creamy Meat Soup: Made using Cool Safflinas or other chilly Ingredients. Eating these soups grants Link one level of Heat Resistance. They heal four Hearts, and they sell for 60 Rupees.
