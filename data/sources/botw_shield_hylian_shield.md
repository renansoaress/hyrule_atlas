### Breath of the Wild
Hylian Shield
The Hylian Shield is located in a Treasure Chest in the Lockup Back Room within Hyrule Castle, guarded by a Stalnox. It is said to have been passed down through the Royal Family of Hyrule along with legends of the hero. The defensive capability and Durability of this Shield are known to outshine all others. It is the strongest and most durable Shield in the game, having a Durability of 800 (up to 1160 with Durability Up +) and Defense rating of 90 (up to 144 with Guard Up +). Unlike Skyward Sword, the Hylian Shield is capable of breaking like other Shields. Once obtained within Hyrule Castle, it can be purchased from Granté in Tarrey Town for 3,000 Rupees, provided Link does not have one already. However, when purchased here, it does not come with a chance of being granted modifiers.

When spoken to at Trilby Plain, Russ of the Shield Breakers will explain that all Shield Surfers have heard of and covet the Hylian Shield. He believes it to have been commissioned by the Royal Family, and its Durability makes it perfect for Shield Surfing. The traveler Baumar has also heard about the Hylian Shield's durability.

The Hylian Shield, being made of metal, will attract Lightning if it is equipped during a Thunderstorm and conduct Electricity. It will also sink if it is dropped in Water, but it can be picked up and manipulated with the Magnesis Rune. Finally, it will not burn if it is exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate.
