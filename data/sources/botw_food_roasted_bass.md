### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Bass are a type of Roasted Food, resulting from Hyrule Bass or Staminoka Bass that have made contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. Blue Hinoxes also have the potential to drop them after death, and they are sold at the Protein Palace General Store in Goron City. Roasted Bass is said to have flaky flesh and smell savory. Link can restore one and a half Hearts to his Life Gauge by eating a Roasted Bass.
