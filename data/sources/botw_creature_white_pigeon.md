White Pigeons are recurring Creatures in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
White Pigeon
White Pigeons are a variety of Pigeon with white feathers. It is believed that they evolved from Wood Pigeons, turning white to blend in with the snow. They are commonly found in the Hebra Mountains, though they also live in the Gerudo Highlands and other snowy Regions. Their extra layer of fat protects them from Freezing Climates, and as a result, they yield high quality Meat. When they are killed, White Pigeons drop either a Raw Bird Thigh or a Raw Whole Bird.
