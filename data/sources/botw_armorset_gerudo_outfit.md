### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
The Gerudo Outfits, also known as the Gerudo Clothes, is a recurring Armor Set in série The Legend of Zelda. The Gerudo Outfit goes unnamed in Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity.

## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
The Gerudo Outfit is an Armor Set consisting of the Gerudo Veil, Gerudo Top, and Gerudo Sirwal. The set can be purchased from Vilia on top of the Kara Kara Bazaar for 600 Rupees as part of the "Forbidden City Entry" Main Quest. Afterwards, all three pieces can be bought in Gerudo Town for 540 Rupees total. Wearing the set provides Link with level 1 heat resistance and a total of 3 defense. In addition, wearing the set will disguise Link as a woman and allow him entry into Gerudo Town. It cannot be Enhanced.
