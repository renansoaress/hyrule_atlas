### Breath of the Wild
Wood Pigeon
Wood Pigeons are a common variety of Pigeon. Their bodies are gray in color, though their head has purple feathers. They can be found all throughout Hyrule, typically inhabiting forests, grasslands, and villages. Due to their lack of awareness, Wood Pigeons are mostly hunted by amateur hunters. When killed, Wood Pigeons drop Raw Bird Drumsticks.
