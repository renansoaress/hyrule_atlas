### Breath of the Wild
Fleet-Lotus Seeds
Fleet-Lotus Seeds can be found growing in wetlands across Hyrule, such as near the Ne'ez Yohma Shrine and throughout the Lanayru Wetlands. Stamm, a Wandering Merchant, sells them for 20 Rupees. They restore half of a Heart for Link when eaten. They can also be Cooked into Meals to grant Link Speed Up. When they are burned by Fire, they turn into Roasted Lotus Seeds. At the Kochi Dye Shop, they can be used to make Green Dye.

Fleet-Lotus Seeds can be used in a Cooking Pot to make the following Meals:

- Fruit and Mushroom Mix | Any Fruit, Any Mushroom
- Fruit Pie | Any Fruit (besides Apple), Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter
- Fruitcake | Apple or Wildberry, Any Fruit, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar
- Honeyed Fruits | Courser Bee Honey, Any Fruit (besides Apple)
- Simmered Fruit | Any Fruit
- Copious Simmered Fruit | Any Fruit (4 different kinds)
- Steamed Fruit | Any Fruit, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
