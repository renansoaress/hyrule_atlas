### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Swift Carrots are a variety of Roasted Food. Link are a can make Roasted Swift Carrots by burning a Swift Carrot in a Fire or Scorching Climate. Though they are fragrant, the roasting process removes the special effects of Swift Carrots. Roasted Swift Carrots restore three-quarters of a Heart to Link when eaten.
