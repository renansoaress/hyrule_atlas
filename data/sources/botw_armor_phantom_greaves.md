### Breath of the Wild
Phantom Greaves are a piece of Armor exclusive to  Downloadable Content pack. Obtaining them is one part of the objective of the "EX Treasure: Phantasma" Side Quest. The Greaves were stolen from Hyrule Castle by Misko and a clue to their location can be found in Misko's EX Journal at the Outpost Ruins. The Treasure Chest that hides the Phantom Greaves can be found buried in the southeast of the Hyrule Garrison Ruins near a ruined Guardian Stalker. After obtaining the Phantom Greaves, Granté will begin selling them for 50 Rupees.Prior to the Version 1.3.3 Update, Granté would not sell any Armor that is exclusive to the Expansion Pass or  Downloadable Content Pack. This meant that the Phantom Greaves could not be replaced if sold, which was noted in the description of the Greaves.

According to its description, the Phantom Greaves look just like the Greaves supposedly worn by Phantoms that tormented heroes. When worn, the Phantom Greaves provide one level of Attack Up. The Phantom Greaves cannot be enhanced by a Great Fairy nor can they be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Phantom Greaves, alongside the Phantom Helmet and Phantom Armor, compose the Phantom Equipment.
