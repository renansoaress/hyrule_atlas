### Breath of the Wild
Black Moblin
Black Moblins are tall and heavy Monsters that can be found all over Hyrule. They are stronger, more resilient variants of Moblins, and they are known to carry the stronger Moblin Gear or other Weapons. When defeated, they drop Moblin Horns, Moblin Fangs, and Moblin Guts.
