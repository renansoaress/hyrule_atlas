### Breath of the Wild
The Sheikah Sensor is used to locate Ancient Shrines in Breath of the Wild. It can be unlocked either by activating any Sheikah Tower after leaving the Great Plateau or in the Hateno Ancient Tech Lab by repairing the Sheikah Slate. When Link is near an Ancient Shrine, the Sheikah Sensor will beep louder. It can be upgraded to the Sheikah Sensor + in order to detect objects other than Ancient Shrines.
