### Breath of the Wild
The Royal Bridle is a Bridle that can be obtained from Toffa by completing "The Royal White Stallion" Side Quest. Prior to the second Great Calamity, Zelda equipped her White Horse with the custom-made Royal Bridle and its counterpart, the Royal Saddle, as part of an attempt to bond with her Horse. Following the Great Calamity, Toffa's grandfather inherited the Royal Bridle and passed it down to Toffa. By registering the White Horse at a Stable and showing it to Toffa, the elderly man will gift Link the Royal Bridle and Royal Saddle and equip them onto the Horse.

The Royal Bridle is a piece of Royal Horse Gear, which is a set of Horse Gear. It can be equipped to any Horse by speaking to specific workers at Highland Stable, Outskirt Stable, South Akkala Stable, or Woodland Stable. However, it is unable to be worn by Epona or the Giant Horse. The Royal Bridle is well-known for its craftsmanship and how much the Royal Family of Hyrule favored it.

The Royal Bridle can be seen equipped to Zelda's White Horse during the special cutscene that plays after the credits for Breath of the Wild, but only if the "Captured Memories" Main Quest has been completed.
