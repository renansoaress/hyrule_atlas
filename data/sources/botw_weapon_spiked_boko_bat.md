## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Spiked Boko Bat
Spiked Boko Bats are upgraded Boko Bats that Bokoblins decided to attach sharp spikes to. Despite their simplicity, skilled fighters can cause a good amount of damage using these Weapons. They are commonly used by Blue Moblins and Blue Bokoblins in East Necluda and the Faron Grasslands. They also have a chance of appearing inside Treasure Chests that spawn after scanning the Breath of the Wild Series Bokoblin amiibo with the amiibo Rune. These Bats are part considered Bokoblin Gear, an equipment archetype that includes all Weapons created by Bokoblins.

Due to being made of wood, Spiked Boko Bats will float when they are dropped in Water. However, will burn when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. The do not attract Lightning during Thunderstorms or conduct Electricity, but they can not be picked up using the Magnesis Rune.
