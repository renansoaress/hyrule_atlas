### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Rainbow Pigeons are recurring Creatures in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Rainbow Pigeon
Rainbow Pigeons are a variety of Pigeon known for their colorful feathers. They can most often be found on Hyrule Ridge and in the Great Hyrule Forest, though particularly large flocks can be found circling in the sky above the Seres Scablands. Though they prefer nuts and grains, their omnivorous nature allows them to consume Insects and worms as well. When a Rainbow Pigeon is killed, a they drop Raw Bird Thighs. Their Meat is noted to be of higher quality than that of a Wood Pigeon.
