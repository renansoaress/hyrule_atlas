## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Boko Bat
Boko Bats are clubs found across Hyrule Kingdom, particularly in the hands of Bokoblins and Moblins in Hyrule Field and West Necluda. They also have a chance of appearing in Treasure Chests that appear when the Breath of the Wild Series Bokoblin amiibo is scanned using the amiibo Rune. Bokoblins create these Weapons, making them part of the Bokoblin Gear equipment archetype. They are clunky and able to knock the Shields out of an Enemy's hands.

Since they are made of wood, Boko Bats burn when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. They also float if they dropped in Water. While this means they do not conduct Electricity or attract Lightning during Thunderstorms, they also are not able to be picked up with the Magnesis Rune.
