### Breath of the Wild
Ravio's Hood is a piece of Armor introduced in the "EX Treasure: Merchant Hood" Side Quest from  Downloadable Content pack in Breath of the Wild. It is a priceless relic that was once kept safely within the walls of Hyrule Castle, but was later stolen by Misko. Misko hints at the whereabouts of Ravio's Hood in Misko's EX Journal 2 in the Deya Village Ruins, claiming that it rests in the right hand of a dragon near Dracozu Lake. The Treasure Chest housing Ravio's Hood can be found embedded in the ground beneath the right hand of the dragon statue at the Spring of Courage. In order to retrieve it, Link must use the Magnesis Rune to pull it from the ground.

Ravio's Hood is the same Hood worn by Ravio. When worn, it grants Link the Sideways Climbing Speed Increased Added Effect, which increases his sideways Climbing speed. If Link sells Ravio's Hood, he can later purchase it from Granté at Tarrey Town for 50 Rupees. Ravio's Hood cannot be enhanced by a Great Fairy, nor can it be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.

## Trivia
* The effect given by Ravio's Hood in Breath of the Wild is a nod to the power given by Ravio's Bracelet in A Link Between Worlds, as horizontal movement is the only means of navigating while Wall Merging.
