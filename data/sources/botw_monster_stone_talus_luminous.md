### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Stone Taluses (Luminous),(BotW, TotK) also known as Luminous Stone Taluses,(Tears of the Kingdom) and simply as Stone Taluses,(Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity) are recurring Bosses and Sub-Bosses in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Stone Talus (Luminous)
Stone Taluses (Luminous) are variations of Stone Taluses, with the notable difference that the Ore Deposits on their bodies are Luminous Stone Deposits instead. They hide their massive size by posing as natural rock formations. Like all Taluses, the only way to damage Stone Taluses (Luminous) is by striking its Luminous Stone Deposit. They are particularly vulnerable to Iron Sledgehammers and other mining tools, as well as Bomb Arrows.

After Link has defeated Calamity Ganon for the first time, he will be able to ask Kilton about Taluses. Kilton is unable to approach them due to their giant size, so he asks Link to defeat all of the Talus in Hyrule so he can analyze their scent. This includes all of the Stone Taluses (Luminous). When Link returns to Kilton after defeating all of the Taluses, he will be rewarded with the Medal of Honor: Talus. As a result of this research, Kilton is able to learn about the musculoskeletal structure and diet of Taluses.
