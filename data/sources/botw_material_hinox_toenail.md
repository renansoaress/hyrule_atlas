### Breath of the Wild
Hinox Toenails are Monster Parts that are dropped by all types of Hinoxes when they're killed. They're as thick as a plate of armor and can be cooked with critters in a Cooking Pot to make Elixirs. Alternatively, they can be sold to Kilton at the Fang and Bone for Mon or used as an ingredient for Brown Dye at the Kochi Dye Shop.

Each piece of the Fierce Deity Set requires five Hinox Toenails, as well as a dragon scale, to be upgraded to their first tier by a Great Fairy.
