### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Gourmet Meat Curries are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Gourmet Meat Curries are Meals made by Cooking Raw Gourmet Meat, Goron Spice, and Hylian Rice together in a Cooking Pot. They heal several of Link's Hearts when eaten. The Meat used in these Curries is of high quality, which satisfies lovers of both Meat and Curry.

Additional Ingredients can be added to the recipe of Gourmet Meat Curry, which will result in the follow variations:

* Chilly Gourmet Meat Curry: Made by adding cold Ingredients. These Meals grant Link one level of Heat Resistance when consumed.

* Electro Gourmet Meat Curry: Made by adding electric Ingredients. When eaten, they will give Link one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Gourmet Meat Curry: Made by adding enduring Ingredients. These Curries completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Gourmet Meat Curry: Made by adding energizing Ingredients. If eaten, they restore a portion of Link's expended Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Gourmet Meat Curry: Made by adding hasty Ingredients. When they are eaten, Link's movement speed will be temporarily boosted.

* Hearty Gourmet Meat Curry: Made by adding hearty Ingredients. Eating them will completely heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him Extra Hearts.

* Mighty Gourmet Meat Curry: Made by adding mighty Ingredients. Link's attack power will by increased by eating this Curry.

* Sneaky Gourmet Meat Curry: Made by adding sneaky Ingredients. Eating these will increase Link's Stealth for a short period of time.

* Spicy Gourmet Meat Curry: Made by adding spicy Ingredients. Eating these will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.

* Tough Gourmet Meat Curry: Made by adding tough Ingredients. If eaten, Link's Defense will increase.
