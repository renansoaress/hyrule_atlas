### Breath of the Wild
Rushroom
Rushrooms are purple Mushrooms that can be commonly found growing on mountains and cliffs, mainly in the Gerudo Highlands and the Hyrule Ridge. They can also be purchased at the Spore Store in the Great Hyrule Forest. When Rushrooms are eaten raw, they will provide Link with half of a Heart of healing. Dishes made in a Cooking Pot using a Rushroom will grant Link the Speed Up Added Effect temporarily. Rushrooms that are burned using Fire turn into Toasty Rushrooms. They can also be used at the Kochi Dye Shop to create Purple Dye.

Pirou at the Gerudo Canyon Stable has eaten Rushrooms everyday since he was five and now cannot live without them. The Side Quest "Rushroom Rush!" requires Link to gather fifty-five Rushrooms for Pirou in exchange for a Diamond. Afterwards, Link can trade fifty-five Rushrooms with Pirou and receive a payment of twice their regular value.

Rushrooms, among other Mushrooms, can be used in a Cooking Pot to make the following Meals:

- Cream of Mushroom Soup | Any Mushroom, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Fish and Mushroom Skewer | Any Fish, Any Mushroom
- Fragrant Mushroom Sauté | Goron Spice, Any Mushroom
- Fruit and Mushroom Mix | Any Fruit, Any Mushroom
- Glazed Mushrooms | Courser Bee Honey, Any Mushroom
- Meat and Mushroom Skewer | Any Meat, Any Mushroom
- Mushroom Omelet | Any Mushroom, Bird Egg, Goat Butter, Rock Salt
- Mushroom Rice Balls | Hylian Rice, Any Mushroom
- Mushroom Risotto | Hylian Rice, Goat Butter, Rock Salt, Any Mushroom
- Mushroom Skewer | Any Mushroom
- Copious Mushroom Skewers | Any Mushrooms (4 different kinds)
- Salt-Grilled Mushrooms | Any Mushroom, Rock Salt
- Steamed Mushrooms | Any Mushroom, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
