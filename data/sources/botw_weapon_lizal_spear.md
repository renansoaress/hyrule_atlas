## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Lizal Spear
Lizal Spears are Weapons, bearing the ripping edge characteristic of Lizalfos design. These designs are common among other pieces of Lizalfos Gear, which includes all Weapons created by Lizalfos. They are wielded by low-tier Lizalfos, most commonly in the Lanayru Wetlands and East Necluda. Fire-Breath Lizalfos, Ice-Breath Lizalfos, and Electric Lizalfos also use them on occasion. Because of their low durability, Lizal Spears do not last very long in combat.

Due to their metallic spearheads, Lizal Spears will attract Lightning if they are equipped during a Thunderstorm and conduct Electricity. They will also sink if they are dropped in Water, but they can be picked back up using the Magnesis Rune. They cannot burn if they are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate.
