### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Island Lobster Shirts,(BotW, TotK) also known as Link's Pajamas,(The Wind Waker) Blue Shirts,(Tears of the Kingdom) Island Outfits,(HWL, HWDE) and Outset Shirts,(HWL, HWDE) are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda. The Island Lobster Shirt goes unnamed in The Wind Waker HD.

### Breath of the Wild
The Island Lobster Shirt is a piece of Armor introduced in The Champions' Ballad DLC pack as part of the "EX Treasure: Garb of Winds" Side Quest. It is a priceless relic that was once kept safely within the walls of Hyrule Castle, but was later stolen by Misko. Misko hints at the whereabouts of the Island Lobster Shirt in Misko's EX Journal 2 in the Deya Village Ruins, claiming that it rests at the mouth of the Menoat River. The Treasure Chest housing the Island Lobster Shirt can be found in the water beneath the alcove at Cora Lake. In order to retrieve it, Link must use the Magnesis Rune to pull it from the water.

The Island Lobster Shirt is the same Shirt that Link wore at the beginning of his journey in The Wind Waker. When worn, it grants Link with one level of Heat Resistance. It also grants Link the Wake Wind Added Effect, allowing him to move a raft with a Korok Leaf quicker while equipped. If Link sells the Island Lobster Shirt, he can later purchase it from Granté at Tarrey Town for 50 Rupees.
