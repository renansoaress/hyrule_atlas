### Breath of the Wild
Fierce Deity Boots are pieces of Armor in Breath of the Wild. They are obtained by scanning the Majora's Mask-themed The Legend of Zelda Series Link amiibo with the amiibo Rune. According to their description, the Fierce Deity Boots are the same boots as the ones worn by the Fierce Deity in the Era of the Hero of Time.

When worn, the Fierce Deity Boots provide one level of Attack Up. The Fierce Deity Boots can be enhanced by a Great Fairy, though they cannot be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Fierce Deity Boots, alongside the Fierce Deity Armor and Fierce Deity Mask, compose the Fierce Deity Set.
