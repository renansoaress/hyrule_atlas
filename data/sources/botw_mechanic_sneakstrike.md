Sneakstrikes is a recurring ability in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Sneakstrikes can be performed by sneaking behind an enemy and attacking it while it is unaware of Link's presence. Successfully executing a Sneakstrike will deal 8 times the damage by Link's equipped melee weapon.
