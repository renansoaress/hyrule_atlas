## Location and Uses
Roasted Hearty Durians are a type of Roasted Food, resulting from Hearty Durians that have made contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. The roasting process does nothing to diminish the harsh odor of Hearty Durians, as it still retains its signature smell. Link can restore four and a half Hearts to his Life Gauge by eating a Roasted Hearty Durian.
