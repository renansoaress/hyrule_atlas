### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Cold Darners are recurring Creatures and Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Cold Darner
Cold Darners can be found in cold regions of Hyrule, such as Hyrule Ridge and the Tabantha Frontier. They can also be purchased from traveling merchants, such as Maypin and Beedle at the Kara Kara Bazaar and Woodland Stable. When Cooked, Cold Darners can be made into Elixirs that provide Heat Resistance. At the Kochi Dye Shop, Cold Darners can be used to make Blue Dye.

As part of the Side Quest "Little Sister's Big Request", Link must ask Gleema what the Insect she wants to see is. She will reveal that she once saw Dragonflies in a Book, and when she grows up, she wants to travel and see Dragonflies in real life. Gleema was unwilling to tell her sister Jana that she wanted to see them because of Jana's distaste for them. However, because it is Gleema's birthday wish, Jana will ask Link to bring her the Dragonflies. Upon delivery, Jana grows disgusted by them asks Link to give them to Gleema instead. As a reward for completing this Quest, Jana will give Link a Silver Rupee.
