Icy Meats are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Icy Meats are a type of Frozen Food, being the result of Raw Meat that has been frozen by a Freezing Climate or Cold Water. They can also be found in Wooden Boxes in the Hebra Mountains. Eating one will restore one Heart to Link and grant him one temporary level of Heat Resistance.
