Hot-Footed Frogs are recurring Creatures and Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Hot-Footed Frog
Hot-Footed Frogs are a type of Frog that can be located near bodies of Water, either Swimming or hopping along the bank. Link can Crouch to sneak up and catch them, or alternatively, he can quickly Spring to capture them before they startle and disappear. Otherwise, Hot-Footed Frogs will run away once they spot him. They can be Cooked alongside Monster Parts to create Hasty Elixirs that grant Speed Up. The Kochi Dye Shop also accepts them as an ingredient for Green Dye.

A Hot-Footed Frog appears in the ninth Recovered Memory, "Silent Princess", wherein Zelda captures one to show Link. She excitedly shares that it is a delicacy known to have potent effects. She then states that Hyrule Castle's research shows that eating one can enhance certain abilities, going on to add that while they are not in a controlled environment, Link's high level of physical fitness would make him perfect for the study. She presses him to taste it, to which Link reacts poorly.
