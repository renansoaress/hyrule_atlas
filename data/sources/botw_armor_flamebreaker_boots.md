### Breath of the Wild
Flamebreaker Boots are pieces of Armor that can be purchased in the Ripped and Shredded shop at Goron City. When worn, they provide one level of Flame Guard. Flamebreaker Boots can be enhanced by a Great Fairy and can be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Flamebreaker Boots, alongside the Flamebreaker Helm and Flamebreaker Armor, compose the Flamebreaker Set. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides the Fireproof Set Bonus.
