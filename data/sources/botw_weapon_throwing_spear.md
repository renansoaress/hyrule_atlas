### Breath of the Wild
Throwing Spear
Throwing Spears are Weapons balanced to be thrown farther than other Spears. They are capable of striking Enemies at a great distance. Commonly, they are found in Treasure Chests in West Necluda and Hyrule Field.

Being made of metal, Throwing Spears will conduct Electricity and attract Lightning if they are equipped during a Thunderstorm. They also sink if they are dropped in Water, but they can be picked up with the Magnesis Rune. Throwing Spears will not burn if they are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate.
