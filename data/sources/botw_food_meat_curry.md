### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Meat Curries are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Meat Curries are Meals made by Cooking Raw Meat, Hylian Rice, and Goron Spice together in a Cooking Pot. They can be eaten to restore some of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge. The heat from the Goron Spice allows one to enjoy the savoriness of the Meat.

By adding more Ingredients to Meat Curry, several different variations can be made:

* Chilly Meat Curry: Made by adding cold Ingredients. Link will be granted one level of Heat Resistance if he eats these curries.

* Electro Meat Curry: Made by adding electric Ingredients. Link gains one level of Shock Resistance when he eats these curries.

* Enduring Meat Curry: Made by adding enduring Ingredients. Eating these curries will completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Meat Curry: Made by adding energizing Ingredients. They replenish a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel when consumed.

* Hasty Meat Curry: Made by adding hasty Ingredients. If eaten, they'll increase Link's movement speed.

* Hearty Meat Curry: Made by adding hearty Ingredients. When eaten, they'll heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him Extra Hearts.

* Mighty Meat Curry: Made by adding mighty Ingredients. They offer Link a boost to his attack power when consumed.

* Sneaky Meat Curry: Made by adding sneaky Ingredients. When eaten, they increase Link's Stealth for a short period of time.

* Spicy Meat Curry: Made by adding spicy Ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one temporary level of Cold Resistance.

* Tough Meat Curry: Made by adding tough Ingredients. They can be eaten to temporarily increase Link's Defense.
