## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Dragonbone Boko Club
Dragonbone Boko Clubs are often found being used by Black and Silver Bokoblins. In Master Mode, they are also used by Golden Bokoblins. They belong to the Bokoblin Gear equipment archetype, a series of Weapons created by Bokoblins. Being reinforced with fossilized bones has increased the amount of damage that Dragonbone Boko Clubs can deal. They are commonly found in Bokoblin Monster Strongholds on Hyrule Ridge and near the Necluda Sea. They also have a chance to appear in Treasure Chests that spawn when the Breath of the Wild Series Bokoblin amiibo is scanned with the amiibo Rune.

Being made of wood, Dragonbone Boko Clubs will burn and eventually disintegrate if exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. This tactic is often used by enemy Bokoblins, who will set their Clubs on Fire to deal additional damage to Link. They also do not attract Lightning during Thunderstorms or conduct Electricity, unlike metal Weapons, and they will float if dropped in Water. Finally, they can not be picked up with the Magnesis Rune.
