Electric Keese Wings are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Electric Keese Wings are dropped by Electric Keese upon their defeat. Like other Monster Parts, they can be Cooked with critters in a Cooking Pot to make Elixirs. They can also be sold to Kilton at the Fang and Bone for Mon or used at the Kochi Dye Shop to make Yellow Dye.
