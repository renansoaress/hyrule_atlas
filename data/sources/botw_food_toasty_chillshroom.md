Toasty Chillshrooms are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Toasty Chillshrooms are a variety of Roasted Food. Link can make Toasty Chillshrooms by burning Chillshrooms with Fire or a Scorching Climate. They are noted to taste better after being toasted. Eating a Toasty Chillshroom will restore three-quarters of a Heart to Link.
