### Breath of the Wild
Hylian Tunics are pieces of Armor that can be purchased at Enchanted in Kakariko Village and at the Ventest Clothing Boutique in Hateno Village. They can be enhanced by a Great Fairy and can be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Hylian Tunic, alongside the Hylian Hood and Hylian Trousers, compose the Hylian Armor Series. The Tunic itself is composed of a cuirass, a spaulder, and a pair of gloves and bracers, all made from leather.
