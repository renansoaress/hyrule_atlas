### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Voltfruits are a type of Roasted Food, and they are made when Voltfruits are burned by Fire or a Scorching Climate. The roasting of the Voltfruits brings out their sweet and sour flavors. Roasted Voltfruits restore three-quarters of a Heart to Link's Life Gauge when they are eaten.
