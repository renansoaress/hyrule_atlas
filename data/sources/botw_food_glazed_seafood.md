Glazed Seafoods are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Glazed Seafood is a dish that can be made by Cooking any Seafood together with Courser Bee Honey. The default variation can be made by using seafood that has a different effect than the Courser Bee Honey. When eaten, they will some of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge. Glazed Seafood is known to be a meal that can be eaten in one sitting.

Only one variation of Glazed Seafood is available:

* Energizing Glazed Seafood: Made using Hyrule Basses, Sanke Carp, Staminoka Basses, or Bright-Eyed Crabs. Eating these Meals will refill a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.
