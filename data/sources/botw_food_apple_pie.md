### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Apple Pies are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Apple Pies can be made in Cooking Pots by combining Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter, and an Apple. Their flaky pie crusts and sweet Apples are thought by many people throughout Hyrule to be a match made in heaven.

Misa's Recipe Book at the Slippery Falcon in Rito Village contains a recipe for Apple Pie. Misa first suggests buying Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, and Cane Sugar from the Slippery Falcon. She then tells the reader to cook them with an Apple until the crust is a flaky golden brown and the insides are gooey. Finally, she tells the reader they can substitute the Apple with another Ingredient for an added kick.

Apple Pies made with the base recipe sell for 30 Rupees. However, they can sell for up to 70 Rupees depending on the additional Ingredient added. Eating a basic Apple Pie will restore three of Link's Hearts. They will restore four Hearts if an additional Apple is added, or five Hearts if another Tabantha Wheat is added.

A fifth Ingredient can be added to Apple Pie, which will result in it having different effects:

* Chilly Apple Pie: They can be eaten to temporarily increase Link's Heat Resistance by one level. They also restore four of Link's Hearts and can be sold for 50 Rupees.

* Electro Apple Pie: Eating one will grant Link Shock Resistance for a short period of time. They also restore four of Link's Hearts and can be sold for 50 Rupees.

* Enduring Apple Pie: Eating one will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel. They also restore eight of Link's Hearts and can be sold for 50 Rupees.

* Energizing Apple Pie: Eating one will refill a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel. They also restore seven of Link's Hearts and can be sold for 50 Rupees.

* Hasty Apple Pie: They will grant Link a speed boost when they are eaten. They also restore four of Link's Hearts and can be sold for 70 Rupees.

* Hearty Apple Pie: They can be eaten to temporarily increase Link's maximum Hearts. They also fully restore all of Link's Hearts and sell for 60 Rupees.

* Mighty Apple Pie: When eaten, they will grant Link a small boost to his attack-power. They also restore five of Link's Hearts and can be sold for 50 Rupees.

* Spicy Apple Pie: Eating one will increase Link's Cold Resistance by one level. They also restore three of Link's Hearts and can be sold for 50 Rupees.

* Sneaky Apple Pie: Eating one will increase Link's Stealth. They also restore four of Link's Hearts and can be sold for 50 Rupees.

* Tough Apple Pie: If they are eaten, they will temporarily boost Link's Defense. They also restore four of Link's Hearts and can be sold for 50 Rupees.
