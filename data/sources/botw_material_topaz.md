### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Topazes are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Topazes can be found by destroying Ore Deposits and Rare Ore Deposits, as well as by defeating Stone Taluses (Rare). The Traveling Merchant Kairo and the store Ore and More both sell Topazes for differing amounts. They are precious Gems that are said to have the power of Electricity.

The jewelry shop Starlight Memories requires five Topazes as payment for the Topaz Earrings. In addition, the merchant Ramella in Goron City will occasionally ask for Topazes, paying more than the regular price. The Kochi Dye Shop also accepts Topazes as an ingredient for Yellow Dye.
