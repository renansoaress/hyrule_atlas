### Breath of the Wild
Diamond Circlets are a type of Circlet Accessory that serve as head Armor. One can be found inside a Treasure Chest located in a chamber under the Lomei Labyrinth Island. Once obtained, all of the Decayed Guardians inside the chamber will activate. Link can also purchase Diamond Circlets in the Starlight Memories Jewelry Shop at Gerudo Town for 1,500 Rupees and three Diamonds. When worn, Diamond Circlets provide one level of Guardian Resist Up. They also provide the hidden Added Effect Ancient Pow Up, increasing damage done by Ancient Soldier Gear and Guardian Gear by 20%.

Diamond Circlets can be enhanced by a Great Fairy at a Great Fairy Fountain. However, they cannot be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.
