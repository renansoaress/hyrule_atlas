### Breath of the Wild
In Breath of the Wild, Link can scale up most landscapes while climbing. While Climbing, in most scenarios, Link will have his Stamina meter to be used up continuously if he climbs, at a linear rate. Link does not use up his stamina while climbing ladders, however, jumping still uses up his stamina, and unlike climbing other surfaces, Link can still climb slowly when his stamina is drained completely.

Link cannot climb on Sheikah technology such as Ancient Shrines and Divine Beasts.
