### Breath of the Wild
Knight's Halberd
Knight's Halberds are Weapons that have been modeled after Axes. They were used by Knights of Hyrule who were skilled in mounted combat before the Great Calamity. In the present, they are commonly used by low-tier Enemies on the Tabantha Frontier and in the Gerudo Desert.

Scanning the following amiibo with the amiibo Rune has a chance to drop a Treasure Chest containing a Knight's Halberd:
* Super Smash Bros. Series Young Link amiibo
* série The Legend of Zelda Series Link (Link's Awakening) amiibo

Being made of metal, Knight's Halberds will attract Lightning when equipped during Thunderstorms and conduct Electricity. They are also unable to be burned by a Fire or Scorching Climate, and they can be picked up using the Magnesis Rune. Finally, Knight's Halberds will sink if they are dropped in Water.

Knight's Halberds belong to the Knight's Gear equipment archetype, a series of Weapons used by the Knights of Hyrule.
