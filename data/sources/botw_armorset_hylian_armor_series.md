The ,(Tears of the Kingdom) also known as the Hylian Series,(Tears of the Kingdom) is a recurring Armor Set in série The Legend of Zelda. The Hylian Armor Series goes unnamed in Breath of the Wild.

### Breath of the Wild
The Hylian Set is an Armor Set consisting of the Hylian Hood, Hylian Tunic, and Hylian Trousers. All three pieces are sold at both the Enchanted Armor Shop in Kakariko Village and Ventest Clothing Boutique in Hateno Village. It does not feature any set bonuses.
