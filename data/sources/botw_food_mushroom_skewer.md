### Breath of the Wild
Mushroom Skewers are Meals that can be made by Cooking any Mushrooms in a Cooking Pot. In order to cook the regular version of this dish, Link must use either only Hylian Shrooms or multiple, different Mushrooms with varying effects. If Link consumes a Mushroom Skewer it will restore at least one of his Hearts. The skewers are known for their colorful presentation.

Using certain Mushrooms when making Mushroom Skewers can result in the following variations:

* Chilly Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Chillshrooms. They can be eaten to grant Link one level of Heat Resistance.

* Electro Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Zapshrooms. Eating them will give Link one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Endura Shrooms. Link can eat them to completely refill and overfill his Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Stamella Shrooms. By eating them, a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel will be refilled.

* Hasty Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Rushrooms. When eaten, they will increase Link's movement speed.

* Hearty Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Hearty Truffles or Big Hearty Truffles. Eating these skewers will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him Extra Hearts.

* Mighty Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Razorshrooms. If these skewers are eaten, Link will gain a boost to his attack power.

* Sneaky Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Silent Shrooms. Link's Stealth abilities will be increased by eating them.

* Spicy Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Sunshrooms. Consuming these skewers will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.

* Tough Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Ironshrooms. Link gains a boost to his Defense when he eats them.
