Roasted Acorns are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Acorns are a type of Roasted Food, resulting from Acorns that have made contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. A Roasted Acorn is said to smell even more nutty than a normal Acorn. Link can heal half a Heart by eating a Roasted Acorn.
