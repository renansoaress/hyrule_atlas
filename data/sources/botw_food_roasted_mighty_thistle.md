### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Roasted Mighty Thistles are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Mighty Thistles are a type of Roasted Food, and they are the result of Mighty Thistles that have made contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. Despite their pointy ends, Roasted Mighty Thistles can easily be eaten. Link can restore half a Heart to his Life Gauge by eating a Roasted Mighty Thistle.
