### Breath of the Wild
The Tunic of the Hero are a piece of Armor which can be obtained using the amiibo Rune while scanning the 30th Anniversary 8-Bit Link amiibo. Each time the figure is scanned, a Treasure Chest will appear that has a small chance to contain the Tunic of the Hero. They can be enhanced by a Great Fairy but cannot be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.

The Tunic of the Hero, alongside the Cap of the Hero and Trousers of the Hero, comprise the Hero Set which resembles Link's outfit from the original The Legend of Zelda. When it has been enhanced by two levels, it provides the Master Sword Beam Up Set Bonus when worn with the full set.
