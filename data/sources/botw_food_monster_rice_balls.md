### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Monster Rice Balls are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Monster Rice Balls are a Meal that can be made by Cooking Hylian Rice, Rock Salt, and Monster Extract together in a Cooking Pot. Due to Monster Extract being used in their making, the amount of Hearts restored to Link, as well as the strength and duration of any status effects, will be random. The unique aroma of Monster Rice Balls is disliked by many people.

By incorporating additional Ingredients to the recipe, several different variations of Monster Rice Balls can be made:

* Chilly Monster Rice Balls: Made by adding chilly Ingredients. They can be eaten to grant Link one level of Heat Resistance.

* Electro Monster Rice Balls: Made by adding electric Ingredients. If eaten, Link will be granted one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Monster Rice Balls: Made by adding enduring Ingredients. When eaten, they will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Monster Rice Balls: Made by adding energizing Ingredients. They will refill a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel when eaten.

* Hasty Monster Rice Balls: Made by adding hasty Ingredients. Link can eat them to gain boost to his movement speed.

* Hearty Monster Rice Balls: Made by adding hearty Ingredients. These rice balls will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him Extra Hearts.

* Mighty Monster Rice Balls: Made by adding mighty Ingredients. When these rice balls are eaten, Link will receive a boost to his attack power.

* Sneaky Monster Rice Balls: Made by adding sneaky Ingredients. Eating one will increase Link's Stealth.

* Spicy Monster Rice Balls: Made by adding spicy Ingredients. Eating them will increase Link's Cold Resistance by one level.

* Tough Monster Rice Balls: Made by adding tough Ingredients. Link will gain a boost to his Defense when he eats these.
