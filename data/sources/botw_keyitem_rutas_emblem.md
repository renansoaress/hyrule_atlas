Ruta's Emblems are Items in Breath of the Wild. They are exclusive to  Downloadable Content Pack.

## Location and Uses
Link can only obtain Ruta's Emblems as part of the Main Quest "EX Champion Mipha's Song". The Monks of Kee Dafunia Shrine, Sato Koda Shrine, and Mah Eliya Shrine grant Link these Emblem once he completes their trials. They are commemorative symbols of the completion of a water trial.

Once Link has collected all three of Ruta's Emblems, the last Monk will instruct him to go to Divine Beast Vah Ruta. There, an unknown Monk will take the Emblems so Link can face a challenge in the Illusory Realm composed of his own memories. Link will find himself inside Divine Beast Vah Ruta, where he will have to face Waterblight Ganon once more. After doing so, Link will be allowed to challenge it again at any time.
