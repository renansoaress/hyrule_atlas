Farosh's Scales are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Farosh's Scales can be collected from the Dragon Farosh, who typically flies around Lake Hylia, Lake Floria, and the Gerudo Highlands. The first time that Link strikes Farosh, it will yield a Scale regardless of where it was struck, whereas after that he must strike the main portion of its body to receive the Scale. It will subsequently fall somewhere on the land in the form of a light.

Cooking with Farosh's Scales will always result in Dubious Food. However, when critters are used in the Cooking Pot, the result will be an Elixir. At the Kochi Dye Shop, Farosh's Scales can be used as an ingredient to create Light Yellow Dye.

During "The Serpent's Jaws" Shrine Quest, Link will come upon the Spring of Courage. When he prays to the Goddess Statue there, Hylia will instruct him to offer one of Farosh's Scales to the Spring. Doing this will open the way to Shae Katha Shrine.

## Trivia
* In Breath of the Wild, Farosh's Scale is the only scale that cannot be collected without hitting its respective Dragon. Both a Naydra's and a Dinraal's Scale can be found in Treasure Chests in Hyrule Castle.
