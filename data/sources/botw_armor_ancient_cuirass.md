## Location and Uses
BotW
Ancient Cuirasses are pieces of Armor that can be purchased from Ancient Oven at the Akkala Ancient Tech Lab. When worn, it provides one level of Guardian Resist Up. Ancient Cuirasses can be enhanced by a Great Fairy and can be Hateno Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Ancient Cuirass joins the Ancient Helm and Ancient Greaves to compose the Ancient Set. When each piece of the Ancient Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Ancient Set is worn, it provides the "Ancient Proficiency" Set Bonus.
