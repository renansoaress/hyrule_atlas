### Breath of the Wild
The Tunic of the Sky can be obtained using the amiibo Rune while scanning the Skyward Sword Link amiibo. Each time the figure is scanned, a Treasure Chest will appear that has a small chance to contain the Tunic of the Sky. It can be enhanced by a Great Fairy, but it cannot be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.

The Tunic of the Sky, the Cap of the Sky, and the Trousers of the Sky comprise the Hero of the Sky Set, which resembles the Knight's Uniform from Skyward Sword. When the Armor Set has been enhanced by two levels, it provides the Master Sword Beam Up Set Bonus when worn.
