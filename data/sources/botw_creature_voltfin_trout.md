Voltfin Trouts are recurring Creatures and Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Voltfin Trout
Voltfin Trouts can be found swimming in the Waters of Hyrule Ridge and the Tabantha Frontier. Like other Fish, they can also be dropped by Octoroks, specifically Forest Octoroks near Thundra Plateau. Link can eat one raw to restore one Heart. Voltfin Trouts exposed to Ice become Frozen Trout, while ones exposed to Fire turn into Roasted Trout. Cooking with Voltfin Trouts result in Meals that grant Link temporary Shock Resistance.

During the Side Quest "Riddles of Hyrule", Walton will ask Link to bring him several Items that correspond to his riddles. The fourth riddle he tells asks for something with scales and a tail that will zap people, and refers to it as a shocking swimmer. When Link places a Voltfin Trout on the leaf, Walton will cheer that he figured it out, and then move on to the next riddle.

In a Cooking Pot, Voltfin Trouts and other Fish can be used to make the following Meals:

- Fish Skewer | Any Fish
- Fish and Mushroom Skewer | Any Fish, Any Mushroom
- Salt-Grilled Fish | Rock Salt, Any Fish or Snail

Voltfin Trouts are also among the Seafood that can make the following Meals:

- Creamy Seafood Soup | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Fish Pie | Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, Rock Salt, Any Seafood
- Glazed Seafood | Courser Bee Honey, Any Seafood
- Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Gourmet Meat, Any Seafood
- Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Meat, Any Seafood
- Monster Stew | Any Meat, Any Seafood, Monster Extract
- Pepper Seafood | Spicy Pepper, Any Seafood
- Prime Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Prime Meat, Any Seafood
- Seafood Meunière | Any Seafood (except Porgy or Salmon), Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter
- Seafood Rice Balls | Hylian Rice, Any Seafood
- Copious Seafood Skewers | Any Seafood (4 different kinds)
- Steamed Fish | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable,, Herb, or Flower
