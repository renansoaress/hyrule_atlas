## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Iron Sledgehammer
Iron Sledgehammers are Goron-made Hammers that can be found in all the major populated settlements of Hyrule Kingdom. They are reasonably durable, and good for breaking open Ore Deposits. Despite being crafted by Goron blacksmiths, they appear to be favored most by Hylians, such as Bolson, Karson, and Hudson. Iron Sledgehammers are two-handed Weapons, meaning they can not be used in conjunction with a Shield. They were originally used for mining, but the Gorons of the Southern Mine have found more use in Pickaxes.

Befitting their original purpose as a mining tool, the Iron Sledgehammer has a hidden property that increases its damage dealt by four times when used against the Ore Deposit atop a Stone Talus. It is also capable of immediately destroying Stone Pebblits.

Iron Sledgehammers have a metal head and a wooden pole, but they are considered metal Weapons. This means they will not burn when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate, the Magnesis Rune can move them, and they will attract Lightning during Thunderstorms and conduct Electricity. They will also sink if they are dropped in Water.
