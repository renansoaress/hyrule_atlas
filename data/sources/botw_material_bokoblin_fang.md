### Breath of the Wild
Bokoblin Fangs are Monster Parts that are dropped by Bokoblins and Stalkoblins upon defeat. They are worn down and dull, but the material is hard. Bokoblin Fangs can be used with other ingredients to Cook Elixirs. They can also be sold to Kilton at Fang and Bone for Mon. The Kochi Dye Shop can use Bokoblin Fangs as an ingredient for Light Yellow Dye.

Bokoblin Fangs are required to upgrade the following pieces of Armor at a Great Fairy Fountain.
