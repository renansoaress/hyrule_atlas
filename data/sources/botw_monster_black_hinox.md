### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Black Hinoxes are recurring Bosses and Sub-Bosses in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Black Hinox
Black Hinox are the strongest versions of Hinox found in Breath of the Wild. They wear greaves on their legs, similar to Blue Hinox, but their metal composition makes Black Hinox vulnerable to Electricity-based attacks. Most of the time, they will be asleep, but they will wake up and begin to attack Link if he disturbs them. Like all other Hinox, their eyes are vulnerable to attacks. When defeated, they drop Hinox Toenails, Hinox Teeth, Hinox Guts, and a range of Roasted Meats.

As part of the Shrine Quest "The Three Giant Brothers", Link must retrieve an Ancient Orb from each of the three Hinox on Mount Taran. The Hinox (Oldest Kin) is a Black Hinox, and it is located in the Rabella Wetlands.

After Link defeats Calamity Ganon for the first time, he will able able to ask Kilton about Hinox. If Link kills all of the Hinox in Hyrule, including all Black Hinox, Kilton will reward him with the Medal of Honor: Hinox. From his research, Kilton determines that each type of Hinox has different dietary preferences.
