### Breath of the Wild
Apple
In Breath of the Wild, Apples are Materials that can be used in Cooking Food. This red fruit can be found growing on various trees throughout Hyrule. They're also sold by the General Shoppe and the travelling Merchant Gartan near Gerudo Town. Regular Hinox also drop Apples when defeated.

Apples can be eaten fresh to heal half of a Heart. They can also be made into Baked Apples by exposing them to Fire, which heal three-quarters of a Heart. At the Kochi Dye Shop, Apples can be used to create Red Dye.

During the Side Quest "Riddles of Hyrule", Walton will ask Link to bring him something small, red, round, and sweet. If Link drops an Apple on the leaf in front of Walton, he will cheer before moving on to the next riddle.

Apples are required for the following Meals:

- Apple Pie | Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter, Apple
- Fruit Pie | Any Fruit (besides Apple), Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter
- Fruitcake | Apple or Wildberry, Any Fruit, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar
- Honeyed Apple | Apple, Courser Bee Honey
- Honeyed Fruits | Courser Bee Honey, Any Fruit (besides Apple)
- Hot Buttered Apple | Apple, Goat Butter

They can also be used to make the following Meals:

- Fruit and Mushroom Mix | Any Fruit, Any Mushroom
- Fruit Pie | Any Fruit (besides Apple), Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter
- Fruitcake | Apple or Wildberry, Any Fruit, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar
- Honeyed Fruits | Courser Bee Honey, Any Fruit (besides Apple)
- Simmered Fruit | Any Fruit
- Copious Simmered Fruit | Any Fruit (4 different kinds)
- Steamed Fruit | Any Fruit, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
