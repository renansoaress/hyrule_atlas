Frozen Bird Drumsticks are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Frozen Bird Drumsticks are a type of Frozen Food, being the result of Raw Bird Drumsticks that have been exposed to Freezing Climates or Cold Water. They are hard to eat, but they restore one Heart and grant Link Heat Resistance.
