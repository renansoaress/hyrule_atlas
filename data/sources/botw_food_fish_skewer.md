### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Fish Skewers,(BotW, TotK) also known as Chilly Fish Skewers,(Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity) are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Fish Skewers are made by Cooking one to three Fish together in a Cooking Pot. Regular versions of these Skewers can only be made by either using Hyrule Basses or Sanke Carps or using Fish with contradictory effects. They heal Link when eaten. Fish Skewers are simple dishes made by cooking fresh chunks of Fish on a skewer.

Based on the type of Fish used, several different variations of Fish Skewers exist:

* Hearty Fish Skewer: Made using Hearty Bass, Hearty Salmons, or other hearty Ingredients. When eaten, they will replenish all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.

* Energizing Fish Skewer: Made using Staminoka Basses or other energizing Ingredients. Eating one of these Skewers will refill a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Fish Skewer: Made by adding speed-boosting Ingredients. If they are eaten, Link's movement speed will be boosted temporarily.

* Spicy Fish Skewer: Made using Sizzlefin Trouts or other spicy Ingredients. They grant Link one level of Cold Resistance when eaten.

* Electro Fish Skewer: Made using Voltfin Trout or other electricity-related Ingredients. Eating one grants Link one level of Shock Resistance.

* Mighty Fish Skewer: Made using Mighty Carps, Mighty Porgies, or other attack-boosting Ingredients. Consuming one of these will increase Link's attack power for a short period of time.

* Tough Fish Skewer: Made using Armored Carps, Armored Porgies, or other defensive Ingredients. When eaten, Link's Defense will be temporarily increased.

* Sneaky Fish Skewer: Made using Stealthfin Trouts or other Stealth-boosting Ingredients. Eating one increases Link's Stealth abilities.

* Chilly Fish Skewer: Made using Chillfin Trouts or other cold Ingredients. If they are eaten, Link will gain one level of Heat Resistance.
