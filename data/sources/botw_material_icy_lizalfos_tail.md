Ice-Breath Lizalfos Tails, also known as Icy Lizalfos Tails, are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Icy Lizalfos Tails are Monster Parts that are dropped by Ice-Breath Lizalfos, who can be found in mountainous regions of Hyrule, upon their deaths. Though they have hard scales and flesh, which makes them unsuitable for cooking, they can be put in a Cooking Pot with critters to make Elixirs. They can be used at the Kochi Dye Shop to make Blue Dye or sold to Kilton at the Fang and Bone in exchange for Mon.

The Desert Voe Set requires Icy Lizalfos Tails to be upgraded to their third and fourth tiers at a Great Fairy Fountain.
