Creamy Heart Soups are Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Creamy Heart Soups are made by Cooking a Hydromelon, a Voltfruit, Fresh Milk, and either a Hearty Radish or Big Hearty Radish together in a Cooking Pot. Soups made with a regular Hearty Radish heal eight Hearts to Link's Life Gauge and sell for 50 Rupees. Soups made with Big Hearty Radishes heal eleven Hearts and sell for 70 Rupees. Unlike many other Foods, there are no variations of Creamy Heart Soup that grant additional affects. These soups are known to be sweet, and sharing them is believed to bring two people closer together.

During her Cooking Class in Gerudo Town, Ashai will mention that she was planning on making Creamy Heart Soup. However, when reciting the recipe to Link, she will name every ingredient but the Hearty Radishes, claiming she cannot remember them.
