### Breath of the Wild
Forest Octorok
Forest Octoroks are a type of Octorok that have adapted to live in forests. They hide underground and disguise themselves as shrubbery, jumping out if a person wanders close enough. If a Forest Octorok spots Link, it will spit rocks at him from afar. Link can deflect these back at them by Blocking with any Shield. If Link is close enough, the Rock will hit the Forest Octorok after being deflected and instantly defeat it. A Perfect Guard will send the Rock flying back faster, letting Link kill them from farther away. If their shrubbery is cut, Forest Octoroks will pop out of the ground in confusion, leaving themselves open for an attack.
