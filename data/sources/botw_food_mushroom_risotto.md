### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Mushroom Risottos are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Mushroom Risotto can be made by Cooking any Mushroom, Hylian Rice, Goat Butter, and Rock Salt together in a Cooking Pot. The default version of Mushroom Risotto can only be made either by using Hylian Shrooms, which offer no status effects, or by using multiple Mushrooms with differing effects. When eaten, some Hearts will be restored to Link. The smell of the Mushrooms and butter in this Meal draws people to eat.

Ivee's Diary, which can be found in her home in Hateno Village, details a recipe for Mushroom Risotto. Ivee lists the Ingredients as one bundle of Hylian Rice, one serving of Goat Butter, a few Hylian Shrooms, and a little bit of Rock Salt. She also notes that the Hylian Shrooms can be substituted for a Hearty Salmon, which would result in Salmon Risotto.

Several different kinds of Mushrooms can be used when making Mushroom Risotto, which can result in the following variations:

* Chilly Mushroom Risotto: Made by using Chillshrooms or other chilly Ingredients. If eaten, Link will gain one level of Heat Resistance.

* Electro Mushroom Risotto: Made by using Zapshrooms or other electric Ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Mushroom Risotto: Made by using Endura Shrooms or other enduring Ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely replenished and overfilled by eating these.

* Energizing Mushroom Risotto: Made by using Stamella Shrooms or other energizing Ingredients. Eating them will replenish part of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Mushroom Risotto: Made by using Rushrooms or other hasty Ingredients. If eaten, Link's movement speed will be increased.

* Hearty Mushroom Risotto: Made by using Hearty Truffles, Big Hearty Truffles, or other hearty Ingredients. These meals will heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.

* Mighty Mushroom Risotto: Made by using Razorshrooms or other mighty Ingredients. Consuming these meals will boost Link's attack power.

* Sneaky Mushroom Risotto: Made by using Silent Shrooms or other sneaky Ingredients. Link's Stealth can be increased by eating these.

* Spicy Mushroom Risotto: Made by using Sunshrooms or other spicy Ingredients. They grant Link one level of Cold Resistance when consumed.

* Tough Mushroom Risotto: Made by using Ironshrooms or other tough Ingredients. Eating them will grant Link a boost to his Defense.
