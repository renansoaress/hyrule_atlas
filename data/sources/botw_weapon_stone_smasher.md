## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Stone Smasher
Stone Smashers are two-handed Weapons made by the Gorons in Goron City. They belong to the Goron Gear equipment archetype, a series of Weapons created and used by the Gorons. Their center of gravity is at their tip, so they use centrifugal force to deal damage. A Stone Smasher has the attack damage of 42. Like other hammer-type Weapons, the Smashers can break Ore Deposits with one hit. Mining ores in this manner was the Gorons' intended purpose when crafting them, as the name suggests. Because Gorons are much stronger and larger than Hylian, they can wield these Weapons one-handed. Link, however, needs both hands to use one.

Stone Smashers have a hidden property which causes their damage output to be doubled when used on the Ore Deposit atop a Stone Talus. Similarly, they are capable of immediately destroying Stone Pebblits.

Though they are commonly found around Goron City in Eldin Canyon, two Stone Smashers can be found in Hyrule Castle. One of them is wielded by a Black Moblin in the Dining Hall, and the other is in a hidden chamber connected to the Library. A Stone Smasher can also be found when a Breath of the Wild Series Daruk amiibo is scanned with the amiibo Rune.

Stone Smashers are made from metal, and they will therefore conduct Electricity and attract Lightning if they are equipped during Thunderstorms. They will also sink if dropped in the Water, but they can be picked up again using the Magnesis Rune. Finally, they will not burn if exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate.
