### Breath of the Wild
Snowquill Headdresses can be purchased at the Brazen Beak in Rito Village, alongside Snowquill Trousers and Snowquill Tunics. All three of these clothing items are part of the Snowquill Set, which makes Link unfreezable when worn together. Wearing a Snowquill Headdress on its own will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.

The Headdress can be both Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop and Enhanced at a Great Fairy Fountain.
