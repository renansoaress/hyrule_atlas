### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Big Hearty Truffles,(BotW, TotK, AoC) also known as White Hearty Truffles,(Tears of the Kingdom) are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Big Hearty Truffle
Big Hearty Truffles are a more rare yet more effective version of the regular Hearty Truffle. Though they were once regular Hearty Truffles, being left unpicked for years and has caused them to grow larger. They can be found growing at the base of Trees in the Hebra Mountains and the Great Hyrule Forest.

Like all hearty-type foods, Big Hearty Truffles can be Cooked in a Cooking Pot to restore Link's Life Gauge and provide Extra Hearts at a higher potency than regular Hearty Truffles. They can be eaten for three Hearts, which is more than the normal Hearty Truffle. Big Hearty Truffles exposed to Fire become Toasted Big Hearty Truffles. They can be used at the Kochi Dye Shop to create Black Dye.

Big Hearty Truffles, among other Mushrooms, can be used in a Cooking Pot to make the following Meals:

- Cream of Mushroom Soup | Any Mushroom, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Fish and Mushroom Skewer | Any Fish, Any Mushroom
- Fragrant Mushroom Sauté | Goron Spice, Any Mushroom
- Fruit and Mushroom Mix | Any Fruit, Any Mushroom
- Glazed Mushrooms | Courser Bee Honey, Any Mushroom
- Meat and Mushroom Skewer | Any Meat, Any Mushroom
- Mushroom Omelet | Any Mushroom, Bird Egg, Goat Butter, Rock Salt
- Mushroom Rice Balls | Hylian Rice, Any Mushroom
- Mushroom Risotto | Hylian Rice, Goat Butter, Rock Salt, Any Mushroom
- Mushroom Skewer | Any Mushroom
- Copious Mushroom Skewers | Any Mushrooms (4 different kinds)
- Salt-Grilled Mushrooms | Any Mushroom, Rock Salt
- Steamed Mushrooms | Any Mushroom, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
