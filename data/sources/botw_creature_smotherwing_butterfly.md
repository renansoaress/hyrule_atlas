### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Smotherwing Butterflies, also known as Papilio vulcensis, are recurring Creatures and Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Smotherwing Butterfly
Smotherwing Butterflies can be found in areas of Death Mountain and Eldin Canyon with Scorching Climates, often on the edges of lava lakes. Beedle also sells them at East Akkala Stable and Wetland Stable. Using one in a Cooking Pot with Monster Parts will result in a Fireproof Elixir, which will grant Link Flame Guard. They can also be used at the Kochi Dye Shop as an ingredient to make Gray Dye.
