Medoh's Emblems are Items in Breath of the Wild. They are exclusive to  Downloadable Content pack.

## Location and Uses
Medoh's Emblems are obtained as part of the Main Quest "EX Champion Revali's Song" from  DLC Pack. They are gifted to Link by the Monks of Shira Gomar Shrine, Kiah Toza Shrine, and Noe Rajee Shrine once he completes their trials. These Emblems are commemorative symbols of the completion of a wind trial.

Once Link has collected all three of Medoh's Emblems, the final Monk will instruct him to head to Divine Beast Vah Medoh. Once there, the Emblems will be taken from him, and he will fight Windblight Ganon again in the Illusory Realm. Afterwards, Link will be able to challenge Windblight Ganon at any time.
