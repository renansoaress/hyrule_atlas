### Breath of the Wild
Desert Voe Spaulders are pieces of Armor that can be purchased in the Gerudo Secret Club at Gerudo Town once "The Secret Club's Secret" Side Quest has been completed. They can also be bought in the Rhondson Armor Boutique at Tarrey Town once the "From the Ground Up" Side Quest has been completed. When worn, they provide one level of Heat Resistance. Desert Voe Spaulders can be enhanced by a Great Fairy and can be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Desert Voe Spaulder, alongside the Desert Voe Headband and Desert Voe Trousers, compose the Desert Voe Set. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides the Shock Damage Resist Set Bonus.
