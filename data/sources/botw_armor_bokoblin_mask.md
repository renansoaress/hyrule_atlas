### Breath of the Wild
The Bokoblin Mask is a piece of Armor that can be purchased from Kilton at the Fang and Bone for 99 Mon. It cannot be enhanced by a Great Fairy, nor can it be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. When worn, it grants Link the Disguise Added Effect, allowing him to blend in with Bokoblins. However, the Bokoblin Mask does not prevent other Enemies in the same area from attacking Link. It also makes quiet Bokoblin sounds as Link moves around. While wearing the Bokoblin Mask, Link adopts a new idle pose to match that of a Bokoblin.
