### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Raw Gourmet Meats are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Raw Gourmet Meats are dropped by Water Buffalo, Snowcoat Foxes, Maraudo Wolves, Wasteland Coyotes, Cold-Footed Wolves, Tabantha Moose, Great-Horned Rhinoceros, Honeyvore Bears, and Grizzlemaw Bears when they are killed. When eaten, Raw Gourmet Meat heals three Hearts to Link's Life Gauge. Raw Gourmet Meat can also be roasted by a Fire to make Seared Gourmet Steak or frozen to make Icy Gourmet Meat.

When Link speaks to Trott at Outskirt Stable, he will express a desire for Raw Gourmet Meat. This will activate the Side Quest "A Rare Find". If Link gives him some, he will be paid 100 Rupees. Afterwards, Link can give Trott Raw Gourmet Meat at any time for 100 Rupees.

When Cooking Meals in a Cooking Pot, Raw Gourmet Meats are required to make the following:

- Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl | Raw Gourmet Meat, Hylian Rice, Rock Salt
- Gourmet Meat Curry | Raw Gourmet Meat, Goron Spice, Hylian Rice
- Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer | Raw Gourmet Meat, Goron Spice

Like other Meats, they can also be used to make the following Meals:

- Creamy Meat Soup | Any Meat, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Glazed Meat | Courser Bee Honey, Any Meat
- Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin | Fortified Pumpkin, Any Meat
- Meat and Mushroom Skewer | Any Meat, Any Mushroom
- Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Meat, Any Seafood
- Meat Pie | Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, Rock Salt, Any Meat
- Copious Meat Skewers | Any Meats (4 different kinds)
- Meaty Rice Balls | Any Meat, Hylian Rice
- Monster Stew | Any Meat, Any Seafood, Monster Extract
- Pepper Steak | Spicy Pepper, Any Meat
- Steamed Meat | Any Meat, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Pumpkins)
