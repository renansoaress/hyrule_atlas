### Breath of the Wild
January 2021

The Desert Voe Set is composed of the Desert Voe Headband, the Desert Voe Spaulder and the Desert Voe Trousers. The full Desert Voe Set can be purchased for a total of 2,400 Rupees from either the Gerudo Secret Club in Gerudo Town or from Rhondson Armor Boutique in Tarrey Town.

Each piece in the Desert Voe Set offers a low-level Heat Resistance which can be combined to provide a maximum mid-level Heat Resistance. The Desert Voe Set yields a Shock Damage Resist Set Bonus when each piece is Enhanced twice by a Great Fairy.

The Desert Voe Set can also be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop in Hateno Village.
