## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Boko Club
Boko Clubs are commonly found and dropped by red Bokoblins throughout Hyrule, especially on the Great Plateau. They are made by Bokoblins to hunt small prey, but due to a lack of refinement, they have low durability. They belong to the Bokoblin Gear equipment archetype, a series of Weapons created and used by Bokoblins. The most common locations to find Boko Clubs is in Hyrule Field and West Necluda. They also have a chance to appear inside Treasure Chests that spawn when the Breath of the Wild Series Bokoblin amiibo is scanned with the amiibo Rune.

Being made of wood, Boko Clubs will burn if exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. Bokoblins often use this fact to deal additional Fire damage to Link, using a Campfire to set their Clubs on Fire before attacking. Unlike metallic Weapons, they will not attract Lightning during Thunderstorms or conduct Electricity, and they will float if they are dropped in Water.
