### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Porgy Meunières are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Porgy Meunière can be made by Cooking either an Armored Porgy or a Mighty Porgy alongside Goat Butter and Tabantha Wheat in a Cooking Pot. The regular version can only be made in different ways. First, Link can utilize both types of Porgy when cooking. Second, he can use another ingredient that has a different effect than the Porgy used. When eaten, these meunières will restore some of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge. These dishes are popular among residents of coastal regions.

Two different variations of Porgy Meunière exist:

* Mighty Porgy Meunière: Made by using Mighty Porgies. Eating these meals will grant Link a boost to his attack power.

* Tough Porgy Meunière: Made by using Tough Porgies. Link can eat them to increase his defensive abilities.
