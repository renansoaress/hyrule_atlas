### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Water Buffalo are recurring Creatures in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Water Buffalo
Water Buffalo are a type of Creature that can be found near sources of Water, where they are known to graze on Grass. When approached from the front, they will charge at Link. When killed, they can drop Raw Meat or Raw Gourmet Meat. While Water Buffalo are wild Animals, some have been domesticated into Hateno Cows.
