Shards of Naydra's Fang are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Shards of Naydra's Fang can be gathered by shooting the dragon Naydra in the lower mouth. The spot it was shot will glow, and the Shard will fall to the ground. Cooking with a Shard of Naydra's Fang will always result in Dubious Food. They can also be used at the Kochi Dye Shop as an ingredient to make Light Blue Dye.
