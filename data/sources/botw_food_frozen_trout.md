Frozen Trouts are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Frozen Trouts are a variety of Frozen Food. Link can make Frozen Trouts by freezing Chillfin Trouts, Sizzlefin Trouts, Voltfin Trout, or Stealthfin Trouts with a Freezing Climate or Cold Water. The flavor of the Trouts has been condensed due to being frozen. Eating one restores one of Link's Hearts and grants one level of temporary Heat Resistance.
