### Breath of the Wild
Bushy-Tailed Squirrel
Bushy-Tailed Squirrels are a brown species of Squirrel with a beige underbelly. They are known for their large tails, which are their namesake. Commonly, they live in forests in Hyrule Field and Hyrule Ridge, but they can also be found in most Regions of Hyrule. When they are disturbed, Bushy-Tailed Squirrels will flee. Their diet mainly consists of nuts, which they carry in their mouths and drop when attacked. If Link kills a Bushy-Tailed Squirrel, it will drop an Acorn.
