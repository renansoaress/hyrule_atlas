### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Ice Chuchus, also stylized as Ice ChuChus, are recurring Bosses and Enemies in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Ice Chuchu
Ice Chuchus appear in more frigid climates, such as Mount Hylia and the Hebra Mountains. They are lightweight and can be pushed about with gusts of wind from the Korok Leaf. If they touch Link, he becomes temporarily frozen due to the frigid air they emit. Ice Chuchus attack by leaping at Link and creating an icy blast around them. After attacking, Ice Chuchus temporarily lose their icy coating and are vulnerable to attack. If they are defeated while they have their icy exteriors, they will explode into a cloud of frost, freezing anything in range. Ice Chuchus drop White Chuchu Jellies upon their defeat.
