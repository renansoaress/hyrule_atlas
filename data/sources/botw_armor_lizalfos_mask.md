### Breath of the Wild
The Lizalfos Mask is a piece of Armor that can be purchased from Kilton at the Fang and Bone for 299 Mon after freeing two Divine Beasts. It cannot be enhanced by a Great Fairy, nor can it be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. When worn, it grants Link the Disguise Added Effect, allowing him to blend in with Lizalfos. However, the Lizalfos Mask does not prevent other Enemies in the same area from attacking Link. It also makes quiet Lizalfos sounds as Link moves around. While wearing the Lizalfos Mask, Link adopts a new idle pose to match that of a Lizalfos.
