Roasted Carps are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Carps are a type of Roasted Food, resulting from Mighty Carp, Armored Carp, or Sanke Carp that have made contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. Blue Hinoxes may also drop them when killed by Link. The roasting process is said to remove some of the stench of raw Carp. Link can restore one and a half of his Hearts by eating a Roasted Carp.
