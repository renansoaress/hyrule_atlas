### Breath of the Wild
Wooden Bow
Wooden Bows are weak Bows that are used by weaker Enemies in Hyrule Field and West Necluda. Though they are not well suited for battle, they are known to be useful for hunting small Animals.

Being made from wood, Wooden Bows will burn if they are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. If they are not put away or extinguished, they will eventually disintegrate. They also float if they are dropped in Water, which is useful for retrieving them. Finally, they do not conduct Electricity or attract Lightning during Thunderstorms, and they cannot be picked up with the Magnesis Rune.
