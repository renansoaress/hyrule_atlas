### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Hearty Lizards are recurring Creatures and Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Hearty Lizard
Hearty Lizards can be found resting on Palm Trees in the Gerudo Desert and on the shores of the Necluda Sea. They can be Cooked with Monster Parts in a Cooking Pot to make a Hearty Elixir, which will grant Extra Hearts when consumed. However, Cooking them with other ingredients will result in Dubious Food. The Kochi Dye Shop also accepts Hearty Lizards as an ingredient to make Blue Dye.
