### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Copious Meat Skewers are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Copious Meat Skewers can be made by Cooking a minimum of four different types of Meat together. If consumed by Link it will restore between ten and twenty of his Hearts, depending on the quantity and quality of the Ingredients that were used to make it. The preparation of Copious Meat Skewers is simple, consisting only of shoving Meat onto a skewer.

A fifth Ingredient can be added to Copious Meat Skewers, which can result in the following variations:

* Chilly Copious Meat Skewers: Made by adding a chilly Ingredient. When these skewers are eaten, Link gains one level of Heat Resistance.

* Electro Copious Meat Skewers: Made by adding an electric Ingredient. Link will gain one level of Shock Resistance when he eats them.

* Enduring Copious Meat Skewers: Made by adding an enduring Ingredient. They completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel when consumed.

* Energizing Copious Meat Skewers: Made by adding an energizing Ingredient. Eating these skewers will refill a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Copious Meat Skewers: Made by adding a hasty Ingredient. Link's movement speed will be increased by eating these skewers.

* Hearty Copious Meat Skewers: Made by adding a hearty Ingredient. When eaten, Link's Hearts will be completely healed and he will gain temporary Hearts.

* Mighty Copious Meat Skewers: Made by adding a mighty Ingredient. When eaten, Link will receive a boost to the power of his attacks.

* Sneaky Copious Meat Skewers: Made by adding a sneaky Ingredient. They can be eaten to temporarily increase Link's Stealth.

* Spicy Copious Meat Skewers: Made by adding a spicy Ingredient. Eating them will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.

* Tough Copious Meat Skewers: Made by adding a tough Ingredient. If consumed, Link's defense will be temporarily boosted.
