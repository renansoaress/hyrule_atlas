### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Creamy Seafood Soups are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Creamy Seafood Soups are made in Cooking Pots by Cooking any seafood, Fresh Milk, Rock Salt, and any Vegetable, herb, or Flower together. However, if Endura Carrots, Swift Carrots, or Fortified Pumpkins are used, the resulting dish will be Veggie Cream Soup. Regular Creamy Seafood Soup can be made by using either a Hyrule Bass or a Sanke Carp in addition to Ingredients lacking any effects. Creamy Seafood Soups heal five of Link's Hearts when eaten and sell for 40 Rupees. The thick-cut chunks of seafood in these soups are known to provide a savory flavor.

Based on the Ingredients used, the final Meal may be one of the following varieties:

* Hearty Creamy Seafood Soup: Made by using Hearty Salmons, Hearty Bass, Hearty Blueshell Snails, or other hearty Ingredients. Eating one of these soups will completely restore Link's Hearts and grant him Extra Hearts. They sell for 50 Rupees.

* Energizing Creamy Seafood Soup: Made using Staminoka Basses, Bright-Eyed Crabs, or other stamina-boosting Ingredients. When eaten, Link's Stamina Wheel will be partially refilled. They heal eight of Link's Hearts and are worth 70 Rupees when sold.

* Hasty Creamy Seafood Soup: Made using speed-boosting Ingredients. Eating these soups will temporarily increase Link's movement speed. They heal three Hearts and sell for 50 Rupees.

* Spicy Creamy Seafood Soup: Made using Sizzlefin Trouts or other spicy Ingredients. When these are eaten, Link will gain one level of Cold Resistance. They also restore five Hearts and sell for 40 Rupees.

* Electro Creamy Seafood Soup: Made using Voltfin Trout or other electricity-related Ingredients. When consumed, Link will gain one level of Shock Resistance for a short period of time. They heal eight of Link's Hearts and can also be sold for 40 Rupees.

* Mighty Creamy Seafood Soup: Made using Mighty Carp, Mighty Porgies, Razorclaw Crabs, or other attack-boosting Ingredients. Eating one will increase the damage of Link's attacks for a time. They replenish five of Link's Hearts and sell for 50 Rupees.

* Tough Creamy Seafood Soup: Made using Armored Carp, Armored Porgies, Ironshell Crabs, or other defensive Ingredients. If eaten, Link's Defense will be temporarily increased. They heal eight of Link's Hearts and sell for 50 Rupees.

* Sneaky Creamy Seafood Soup: Made using Stealthfin Trouts, Sneaky River Snails, or other sneaky Ingredients. Eating one of these soups will increase Link's Stealth abilities. They heal five Hearts and are worth 50 Rupees when sold.

* Chilly Creamy Seafood Soup: Made using Chillfin Trouts or other chilly Ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one level of Heat Resistance. They restore five of Link's Hearts and can be sold for 40 Rupees.
