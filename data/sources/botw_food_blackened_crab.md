Blackened Crabs are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Blackened Crabs are a type of Roasted Food. They are the result of Razorclaw Crabs, Ironshell Crabs, or Bright-Eyed Crabs that have made contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. Blue Hinoxes also sometimes drop them when killed. Blackened Crabs have soft, flaky flesh, and smell faintly of charred shell. They are said to assault all five of your senses in the best ways. Eating one will restore one and a half of Link's Hearts.
