### Breath of the Wild
The Dark Hood is a piece of Armor that can be purchased from Kilton at his Fang and Bone shop after all Divine Beasts have been freed. It cannot be enhanced by a Great Fairy, nor can it be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Dark Hood, alongside the Dark Tunic and Dark Trousers, compose the Dark Series. When the full set is worn, it provides a Night Speed Up bonus. Also, characters get scared when they see Link wearing it.
