## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Mighty Lynel Crusher
Mighty Lynel Crushers are two-handed Weapons wielded by Blue-Maned Lynels in the Gerudo Highlands and Hebra Mountains. In Master Mode, they are utilized by White-Maned Lynels instead. They were made by Lynels and reinforced to increase their durability and damage. Their origin makes them part of the Lynel Gear equipment archetype, a series of Weapons created by Lynels. Their weight is capable of crushing both their opponents and their Shields.

Being made of metal, Mighty Lynel Crushers will attract Lightning when equipped during a Thunderstorm and conduct Electricity, and they can be picked up using the Magnesis Rune. However, they do not burn when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. They will also sink if they are dropped in Water.

## Trivia
* Mighty Lynel Crushers are one of several Permanently Missable Items in Breath of the Wild. Since Lynel Gear scales up with Lynels, once Blue-Maned Lynels are gone, so are Mighty Lynel Crushers.
