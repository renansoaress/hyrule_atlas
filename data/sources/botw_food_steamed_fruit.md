### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Steamed Fruits are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Steamed Fruits are made by Cooking a Fruit with any Vegetable, herb, or Flower together in a Cooking Pot. Their default version can be made in two different ways. The first way requires Link to Cook Hyrule Herbs with either Apples, Palm Fruits, or Wildberries, as none of these Ingredients provide an Added Effect. Link can also use two Ingredients with differing Effects, as these will cancel each other out. Eating these Meals will heal some of his Hearts. They are regional dishes, made by steaming near-ripe Fruits in the leaves of plants.

Both the kind of Fruit and the type of herb, Vegetable, or Flower used will effect the type of Steamed Fruit made:

* Hearty Steamed Fruit: Made by using Hearty Durians, Hearty Radishes, Big Hearty Radishes, or other hearty Ingredients. By eating them, all of Link's Hearts will be healed, and he will be granted Extra Hearts.

* Enduring Steamed Fruit: Made by using Endura Carrots or other enduring Ingredients. They can be eaten to completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Steamed Fruit: Made by using Fleet-Lotus Seeds, Swift Carrots, Swift Violets, or other hasty Ingredients. When eaten, Link's movement speed will be boosted.

* Spicy Steamed Fruit: Made by using Spicy Peppers, Warm Safflinas, or other spicy Ingredients. Link can eat them to gain one level of Cold Resistance.

* Electro Steamed Fruit: Made by using Voltfruits, Electric Safflinas, or other electric Ingredients. If they're eaten, they'll grant Link one level of Shock Resistance.

* Mighty Steamed Fruit: Made by using Mighty Thistles, Mighty Bananas, or other might Ingredients. Eating them will provide Link with a boost to his attack power.

* Tough Steamed Fruit: Made by using Fortified Pumpkins, Armoranths, or other tough Ingredients. Link will receive a boost to his Defense when he eats these fruits.

* Sneaky Steamed Fruit: Made by using Blue Nightshades, Silent Princesses, or other sneaky Ingredients. They will grant Link a boost to his Stealth when he eats them.

* Chilly Steamed Fruit: Made by using Hydromelons, Cool Safflinas, or other chilly Ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one level of Heat Resistance.
