## Location and Uses
The Well-Worn Outfit consists of the Old Shirt and the Well-Worn Trousers, both of which are noted to be too small for Link. Both pieces of Armor can be found in stone Treasure Chests within the Shrine of Resurrection. While the Well-Worn Outfit cannot be upgraded by a Great Fairy, Link is able to Dye its pieces at the Kochi Dye Shop. After completing the "From the Ground Up" Side Quest, Granté will begin to sell the pieces of the Well-Worn Outfit in Tarrey Town.
