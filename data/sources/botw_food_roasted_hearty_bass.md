### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Roasted Hearty Basses are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Hearty Basses are a type of Roasted Food, and they are the result of Hearty Bass that has made contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. Occasionally, Blue Hinoxes will drop Roasted Hearty Basses when they are killed. Roasted Hearty Basses are said to have crispy skin and just the right amount of fat content to create an exceptionally tasty meal. Link can restore three of Hearts to his Life Gauge by eating a Roasted Hearty Bass.
