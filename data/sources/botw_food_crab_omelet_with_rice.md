### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Crab Omelets with Rice are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Crab Omelets with Rice are made by combining Hylian Rice, a Bird Egg, Rock Salt, and any Crab in a Cooking Pot. Regular Crab Omelets with Rice can only be made by using two different types of Crabs in the recipe or by using an ingredient with a differing effect. These types of omelets restore eight of Link's Hearts}. Alternatively, they can be sold for 70 Rupees. The fluffy Crab legs are known to pair perfectly with the rice.

Three different variations of Crab Omelets with Rice exist, correlating with all three different types of Crabs:

* Energizing Crab Omelet with Rice: Made using a Bright-Eyed Crab. Consuming one will partially refill Link's Stamina Wheel. They restore six of his Hearts and sell for 50 Rupees.

* Mighty Crab Omelet with Rice: Made using a Razorclaw Crab. When eaten, they will temporarily boost Link's attack power. They also restore nine of his Hearts and sell for 40 Rupees.

* Tough Crab Omelet with Rice: Made using an Ironshell Crab. If they are consumed, they increase Link's Defense for a short period of time. They heal Link for seven Hearts and sell for 60 Rupees.
