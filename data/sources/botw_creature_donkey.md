### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Donkeys are recurring Creatures in série The Legend of Zelda. Donkeys go unnamed in Majora's Mask.

### Breath of the Wild
Donkey
Donkeys are used as pack animals for traveling Merchants throughout Hyrule Kingdom. They can be found on the road with certain travelers or resting at Stable found throughout Hyrule Kingdom. This is because they are raised as livestock and therefore do not exist in the wild. Donkeys will always follow the Merchant that they are traveling with.

According to the traveling merchant Cambo, the scientific name for Donkeys is equus donkus.
