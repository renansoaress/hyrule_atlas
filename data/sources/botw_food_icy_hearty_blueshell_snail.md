## Location and Uses
Icy Hearty Blueshell Snails are a type of Frozen Food, and they can be made by freezing Hearty Blueshell Snails with a Freezing Climate or Cold Water. Only a skilled chef is able to extract the meat from a Icy Hearty Blueshell Snail. Eating one will grant Link one level of Heat Resistance and restore three Hearts to his Life Gauge.
