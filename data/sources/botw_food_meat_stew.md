Meat Stews are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Meat Stews are Meals that are made by Cooking either Raw Meat or a Raw Bird Drumstick with Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, and Fresh Milk in a Cooking Pot. When eaten, they will heal some of Link's Hearts. These stews are mainstay dishes, containing hearty Meats that leave people satisfied throughout Hyrule.

By adding a fifth Ingredient to Meat Stew, Link can make one of the following variations:

* Chilly Meat Stew: Made by adding chilly Ingredients. Link can eat these stews to gain one level of Heat Resistance.

* Electro Meat Stew: Made by adding electric Ingredients. Link gains one level of Shock Resistance when he eats them.

* Enduring Meat Stew: Made by adding an enduring Ingredient. Consuming them will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Meat Stew: Made by adding an energizing Ingredient. If one of these stews are eaten, Link's Stamina Wheel will be partially refilled.

* Hasty Meat Stew: Made by adding a hasty Ingredient. When eaten, they'll boost Link's movement speed.

* Hearty Meat Stew: Made by adding a hearty Ingredient. Eating them will heal all of Link's Hearts and increase his maximum Hearts.

* Mighty Meat Stew: Made by adding mighty Ingredients. If they are eaten, they will increase the power of Link's attacks.

* Sneaky Meat Stew: Made by adding sneaky Ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain a temporary boost to his Stealth.

* Spicy Meat Stew: Made by adding a spicy Ingredient. Eating these stews will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.

* Tough Meat Stew: Made by adding tough Ingredients. Eating these stews will increase Link's defense.
