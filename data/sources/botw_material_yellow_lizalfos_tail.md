### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Electric Lizalfos Tails, also known as Yellow Lizalfos Tails, are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Yellow Lizalfos Tails are Monster Parts that are dropped by Electric Lizalfos, which can be found in the Gerudo Desert and Hyrule Ridge, when killed. Though their smell makes them unable to be cooked, Yellow Lizalfos Tails can be mixed with critters in a Cooking Pot to make Elixirs. They can also be exchanged with Kilton at the Fang and Bone for Mon or used at the Kochi Dye Shop to make Yellow Dye.

The Rubber Armor set requires Yellow Lizalfos Tails for its third and fourth upgrades at a Great Fairy Fountain.
