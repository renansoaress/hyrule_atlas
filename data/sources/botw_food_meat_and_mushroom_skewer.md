### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Meat and Mushroom Skewers are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Meat and Mushroom Skewers are Meals made by Cooking any piece of Meat with Mushrooms in a Cooking Pot. The regular version can be made either by using Hylian Shrooms, which offer no status effects, or by using multiple Mushrooms with different effects. When consumed, they will heal several of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge.

Several different variations of Meat and Mushroom Skewers can be made by using different Ingredients:

* Chilly Meat and Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Chillshrooms. They grant Link one level of Heat Resistance when eaten.

* Electro Meat and Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Zapshrooms. Consuming these skewers will grant Link one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Meat and Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Endura Shrooms. When eaten, they completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Meat and Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Stamella Shrooms. They can be eaten to restore a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Meat and Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Rushrooms. If eaten, they will temporarily boost Link's movement speed.

* Hearty Meat and Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Hearty Truffles or Big Hearty Truffles. Eating these skewers will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him Extra Hearts.

* Mighty Meat and Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Razorshrooms. Eating them temporarily increases the power of Link's attacks.

* Sneaky Meat and Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Silent Shrooms. If eaten, they increase Link's Stealth for a short period of time.

* Spicy Meat and Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Sunshrooms. They grant Link one level of Cold Resistance when eaten.

* Tough Meat and Mushroom Skewer: Made by using Ironshrooms. Link's Defense will be increased by eating these skewers.
