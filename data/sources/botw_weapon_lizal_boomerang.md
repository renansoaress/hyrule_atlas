### Breath of the Wild
Lizal Boomerang
Lizal Boomerangs are Weapons favored by Lizalfos that can be used for either melee or thrown attacks. They belong to the Lizalfos Gear equipment archetype, which includes all Weapons created by Lizalfos. Though they are most commonly used by regular Lizalfos, Fire-Breath Lizalfos, Ice-Breath Lizalfos, and Electric Lizalfos can also use these Weapons early on. When thrown, Link must catch the Boomerang before it returns to him, or else he will take damage. They can be found around Lake Hylia and in the Lanayru Wetlands.

As metal Weapons, Lizal Boomerangs will spark and attract Lightning when equipped during Thunderstorms, and they are capable of conducting Electricity. They will sink when dropped in Water, however, they can also be picked up using the Magnesis Rune on the Sheikah Slate. Finally, they will not burn when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate.
