Icy Prime Meats are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Icy Prime Meats are a type of Frozen Food, which is made by freezing Raw Prime Meats in a Freezing Climate or Cold Water. When eaten, Icy Prime Meats heal one and a half of Link's Hearts and grants one level of temporary Heat Resistance.
