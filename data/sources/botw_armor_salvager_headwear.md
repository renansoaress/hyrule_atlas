## Location and Uses
The Salvager Headwear is a piece of Armor in Breath of the Wild, part of the Salvager Armor Set. It is obtained from a Treasure Chest as part of the "[Xenoblade Chronicles 2]" Side Quest. The Chest crashes on the southern shore of Lake Hylia after Link looks to the southern sky from the middle of the Bridge of Hylia during the night. After obtaining the Salvager Headwear, Granté will begin selling it for 50 Rupees. When worn, the Salvager Headwear provides one level of Swim Speed Up. The Salvager Headwear cannot be enhanced by a Great Fairy, nor can it be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.
