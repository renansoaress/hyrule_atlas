### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Golden Bokoblins are Enemies in Breath of the Wild. They are exclusive to Master Mode, which is part of  Downloadable Content Pack.

## Characteristics
Golden Bokoblin
In Master Mode, Golden Bokoblins are the strongest variation of Bokoblin, appearing once Enemy Progression reaches its final level. They are said to be Silver Bokoblins that were struck by Lightning, resulting in a mysterious transformation. Due to this, they are resilient to Electric attacks, such as those from Shock Arrows or Thunderblades.

Golden Bokoblins equip strong Weapons, typically pieces of Bokoblin Gear that have various bonuses. When defeated, they may drop Bokoblin Horns, Bokoblin Fangs, Bokoblin Guts, and Gems such as Ambers, Opals, Topazes, Rubies, Sapphires, and Diamonds.
