### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Blue Bokoblins are recurring Enemies in série The Legend of Zelda. Blue-colored Bokoblins appear in The Wind Waker and Twilight Princess, but they are unnamed and known as their default color variants.

### Breath of the Wild
Blue Bokoblin
In Breath of the Wild, Blue Bokoblins are stronger a stronger variation of Bokoblins, as they have more health and wield stronger Bokoblin Gear and Weapons. They are more clever than regular Bokoblins, knowing to kick away Remote Bombs to avoid an explosion. In Master Mode, they are the first type of Bokoblin encountered in Breath of the Wild.

During the Recovered Memory "A Premonition", one Blue Bokoblin can be seen amongst the Monsters that have been defeated by Link.

As part of the "Missing in Action" Side Quest, Blue Bokoblins can be found attacking Canolo, Flaxel, Oliff, and Palme on the Koukot Plateau Path. When Link defeats the Blue Bokoblins, the will leave for Gerudo Canyon Stable to meet with Sesami.
