### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Swimming is a recurring ability in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Link can Swim as soon as he exits the Shrine of Resurrection. Unlike in Skyward Sword, Swimming consumes Stamina. He is also not able to go underwater. When Link Targets an Enemy while Swimming, his animation will change to the sidestroke he uses in Ocarina of Time, Twilight Princess, and Skyward Sword.
