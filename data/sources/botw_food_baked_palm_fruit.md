### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Baked Palm Fruits are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Baked Palm Fruits are a type of Roasted Food, being the result of Palm Fruits that have made contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. Baked Palm Fruits are best served right after the roasting process, once the juices emerge. By eating one, Link can restore one and a half of Hearts to his Life Gauge.
