Great Eagle Bows are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.(BotW, TotK, AoC, AoI)

### Breath of the Wild
Great Eagle Bow
Great Eagle Bows are Bows belonging to the Rito Gear equipment archetype. They are powerful Bows for aerial combat that can fire three Arrows at once, similar to the Lynel Bow and its counterparts.

Prior to the Great Calamity, the Rito Champion Revali wielded a Great Eagle Bow. During the EX Recovered Memory" Champion Revali's Song", the Bow is missing the blue cloth resembling Revali's Champion Garb. In this Memory, Revali can be seen practicing shooting Targets with his Great Eagle Bow at the Flight Range. It is said that Revali could fire Arrows from his Bow with the speed of a gale, making him adept at aerial combat. Teba claims that they too heavy for the average Rito to wield, as they would end up losing all their speed. However, he states that Revali's strength and speed allowed him to counter the detriments of the Weapon.

Kaneli will gift Link Revali's Great Eagle Bow in Rito Village as a reward for calming Divine Beast Vah Medoh. This is done at the suggestion of Teba, who was impressed by how Link handled his own Bow against the Divine Beast. It can be retrieved from inside a Stone Chest in Kaneli's House. If Link's Great Eagle Bow breaks or becomes irreversibly lost, Harth can make a new one for him if he provides a Swallow Bow, five bundles of Wood, and a Diamond. If it was dropped somewhere but can still be re-obtained, Harth will instead recommend trying to find it first, as it is expensive to make.

Being made of wood, Great Eagle Bows will burn if they touch Fire or are equipped in a Scorching Climate. They will also float if dropped in Water, and they will not attract Lightning during Thunderstorms or conduct Electricity. Finally, Great Eagle Bows cannot be picked up with the Magnesis Rune.
