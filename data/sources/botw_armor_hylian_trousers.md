### Breath of the Wild
Hylian Trousers are pieces of Armor that can be obtained from a Treasure Chest found in the ruins of a building in front of the Temple of Time on the Great Plateau. They can also be purchased from Enchanted in Kakariko Village and at the Ventest Clothing Boutique in Hateno Village. Hylian Trousers can be enhanced by a Great Fairy and they can be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Hylian Trousers, alongside the Hylian Hood and Hylian Tunic, compose the Hylian Set. However, the Hylian Trousers consistently appear matched with the Champion's Tunic in promotional media, in each of Link's Recovered Memories, and in the ending cutscene if Link has recovered all of his lost Memories. Similarly, the two aforementioned pieces of Armor are also paired with the Hylian Hood when Link is seen on Horseback in promotional media.
