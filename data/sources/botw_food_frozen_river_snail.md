### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Frozen River Snails are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Frozen River Snails are a type of Frozen Food. Link can make Frozen River Snails by exposing Sneaky River Snails to Freezing Climates or Cold Water. Sneaky River Snails are noted to no longer glow after being frozen. Eating one restores one of Link's Hearts and grants temporary Heat Resistance.
