White Goats are recurring Creatures in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
White Goat
White Goats are domesticated Goats often found grazing at Stables and in Hateno Pasture. The bells they wear are a sign of their domestication, allowing their handlers to easily find them after they have been let out to graze. White Goats are herbivorous, eating any Fruit, nut, Vegetable, Mushroom, or Flower that they are presented with. Alongside Hateno Cows, White Goats are kept in order to produce Fresh Milk. Their Milk can be made into Goat Butter, and their wool is used to make clothes.
