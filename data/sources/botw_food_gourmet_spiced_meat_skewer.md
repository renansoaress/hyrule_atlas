### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewers are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewers are Meals made by Cooking Raw Gourmet Meat with Goron Spice in a Cooking Pot. Eating them will restore some Hearts to Link's Life Gauge. Due to their rich aroma and the quality of their Meats, these Skewers are considered to be in a league of their own.

Additional Ingredients can be added to Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewers, which could result in one of the following versions:

* Chilly Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding cold Ingredients. Eating them will increase Link's Heat Resistance by one level.

* Electro Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding electric Ingredients. If they are eaten, Link will gain one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding enduring Ingredients. These skewers completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding energizing Ingredients. They refill a part of Link's Stamina Wheel when consumed.

* Hasty Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding hasty Ingredients. Link receives a boost to his movement speed when he eats these.

* Hearty Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding hearty Ingredients. When eaten, they will heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.

* Mighty Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding mighty Ingredients. Link can eat them to temporarily boost his attack power.

* Sneaky Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding sneaky Ingredients. They increase Link's Stealth for a short while when eaten.

* Spicy Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding spicy Ingredients. They grant Link one level of Cold Resistance temporarily.

* Tough Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding tough Ingredients. When eaten, Link's Defense will be temporarily increased.
