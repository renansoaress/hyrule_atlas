### Breath of the Wild
Toasted Hearty Truffles are a variety of Roasted Food, and they can be made by exposing Hearty Truffles to Fire or a Scorching Climate. Their outsides are known to be crispy, while their insides are warm and gooey. Eating one restores three Hearts to Link's Life Gauge.
