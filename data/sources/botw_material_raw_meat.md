### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Raw Meats are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Raw Meat is dropped by Woodland Boars, Mountain Goats, and Grassland Foxes when they are killed. It is also sold by two separate Wandering Merchants, those being Cambo and Mezer. Raw Meat can be eaten to heal one Heart. It can be cooked by a Fire to make Seared Steak or frozen with Ice to create Icy Meat.

As part of the Side Quest "Koko Cuisine", Link must gather Raw Meat for Koko so she can make Tough Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin. When Link brings her Raw Meat, Koko will be excited and reward Link with Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin.

When Cooking Meals in a Cooking Pot, Raw Meats are required to make the following:

- Meat and Rice Bowl | Hylian Rice, Raw Meat, Rock Salt
- Meat Curry | Raw Meat, Hylian Rice, Goron Spice
- Meat Stew | Raw Meat or Raw Bird Drumstick, Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, Fresh Milk
- Salt-Grilled Meat | Rock Salt, Raw Meat or Raw Bird Drumstick
- Spiced Meat Skewer | Goron Spice, Raw Meat

Like other Meats, they can also be used to make the following Meals:

- Creamy Meat Soup | Any Meat, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Glazed Meat | Courser Bee Honey, Any Meat
- Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin | Fortified Pumpkin, Any Meat
- Meat and Mushroom Skewer | Any Meat, Any Mushroom
- Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Meat, Any Seafood
- Meat Pie | Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, Rock Salt, Any Meat
- Copious Meat Skewers | Any Meats (4 different kinds)
- Meaty Rice Balls | Any Meat, Hylian Rice
- Monster Stew | Any Meat, Any Seafood, Monster Extract
- Pepper Steak | Spicy Pepper, Any Meat
- Steamed Meat | Any Meat, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Pumpkins)
