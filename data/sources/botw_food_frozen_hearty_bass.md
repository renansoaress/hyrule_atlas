### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Frozen Hearty Basses are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Frozen Hearty Basses are a type of Frozen Food, and they are made by chilling Hearty Bass in a Freezing Climate or Cold Water. Eating one is difficult, but it will restore two Hearts to Link's Life Gauge and grant one minute of Heat Resistance.
