### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Salt-Grilled Fish are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Salt-Grilled Fish can be made by Cooking any Fish together with Rock Salt in a Cooking Pot. The default version can only be made either by using Hyrule Basses or Sanke Carp, both of which offer no status effects, or by using two Fish with contradictory effects. Eating these Fish will heal some of Link's Hearts. To make them, the whole Fish must be rolled in Rock Salt before it is grilled.

Based on both the type of Fish used and whether any additional Ingredients are added, several different variations of Salt-Grilled Fish can be made:

* Chilly Salt-Grilled Fish: Made by using Chillfin Trouts or other chilly Ingredients. Link will gain one level of Heat Resistance if he eats these fish.

* Electro Salt-Grilled Fish: Made by using Voltfin Trout or other electric Ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Salt-Grilled Fish: Made by using enduring Ingredients. They can be eaten to completely replenish and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Salt-Grilled Fish: Made by using Staminoka Basses or other energizing Ingredients. Consuming them will refill a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Salt-Grilled Fish: Made by using hasty Ingredients. Link's movement speed will be increased by eating these meals.

* Hearty Salt-Grilled Fish: Made by using Hearty Salmons, Hearty Bass, or other hearty Ingredients. When eaten, all of Link's Hearts will be healed, and he will gain temporary Hearts.

* Mighty Salt-Grilled Fish: Made by using Mighty Carp, Mighty Porgies, or other mighty Ingredients. By eating this fish, Link will receive a boost to his attack power.

* Sneaky Salt-Grilled Fish: Made by using Stealthfin Trouts or other sneaky Ingredients. If Link eats them, he will receive a boost to his Stealth abilities.

* Spicy Salt-Grilled Fish: Made by using Sizzlefin Trouts or other spicy Ingredients. One level of Heat Resistance will be granted to Link if he eats them.

* Tough Salt-Grilled Fish: Made by using Armored Carp, Armored Porgies, or other tough Ingredients. They will grant Link a boost to his defense if he eats them.
