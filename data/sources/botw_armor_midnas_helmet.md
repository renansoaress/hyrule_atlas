### Breath of the Wild
Midna's Helmet is a piece of Armor that Link can wear on his head. It is exclusive to  Downloadable Content pack. The bandit Misko stole it from Hyrule Castle, along with several other treasures. During the "EX Treasure: Twilight Relic" Side Quest, Link is tasked with reading Misko's EX Journal 2 in order to learn the location of Midna's Helmet. In reading it, he discovers that the Helmet can be found at the ruins of a temple flooded by the Regencia River. At the Sage Temple Ruins, Link will find a Hidden Chest embedded in the ground, which contains Midna's Helmet. Midna's Helmet grants both Guardian Resist Up and Ancient Pow Up.

Once acquired, Granté will begin selling Midna's Helmet for 50 Rupees.Prior to the Version 1.3.3 Update, Granté would not sell any Armor that is exclusive to the Breath of the Wild Expansion Pass. This meant that Midna's Helmet could not be replaced if sold, which was noted in its Inventory description. Furthermore it cannot be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop or enhanced by a Great Fairy.
