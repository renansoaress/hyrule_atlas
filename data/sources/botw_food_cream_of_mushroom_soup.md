### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Cream of Mushroom Soups are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Cream of Mushroom Soups are made by Cooking any Mushroom, Fresh Milk, Rock Salt, and any vegetable, herb, or flower in a Cooking Pot. Regular Cream of Mushroom Soup is made by either using Ingredients without any effects or using two Ingredients with contradictory effects. Eating one restores four Hearts to Link. They are worth 30 Rupees when sold. These soups are known to be so chunky that they're as filling as a full meal.

Several different variations of Cream of Mushroom Soups exist, depending on the type of Ingredients used:

* Hearty Cream of Mushroom Soup: Made by using Hearty Truffles, Big Hearty Truffles, or other hearty Ingredients. When eaten, they completely restore all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum hearts. They also sell for 50 Rupees.

* Energizing Cream of Mushroom Soup: Made using Stamella Shrooms or other stamina-increasing Ingredients. Eating one will refill a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel. They restore seven of his Hearts and they sell for 40 Rupees.

* Enduring Cream of Mushroom Soup: Made using Endura Shrooms or Endura Carrots. Consuming this soup will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel. They heal for five Hearts and sell for 40 Rupees.

* Hasty Cream of Mushroom Soup: Made using Rushrooms or other speed-increasing Ingredients. These soups will temporarily increase Link's speed when eaten. They also restore four Hearts to Link, and they sell for 30 Rupees.

* Spicy Cream of Mushroom Soup: Made using Sunshrooms or other heat-related Ingredients. Eating one will temporarily grant Link one level of Cold Resistance. They also heal him for four Hearts, but are worth 30 Rupees when sold.

* Electro Cream of Mushroom Soup: Made using Zapshrooms or other electricity-related Ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of Shock Resistance for a short time. They also heal four of his Hearts and sell for 30 Rupees.

* Mighty Cream of Mushroom Soup: Made using Razorshrooms or other attack-increasing Ingredients. These soups increase Link's attack power when consumed. They restore four Hearts to Link and sell for 40 Rupees.

* Tough Cream of Mushroom Soup: Made using Ironshrooms or other defensive Ingredients. Eating one will temporarily increase Link's defense. They replenish four Hearts, and they sell for 40 Rupees.

* Sneaky Cream of Mushroom Soup: Made using Silent Shrooms or other sneaky Ingredients. Consuming this soup increases Link's Stealth for a short period. They heal four of Link's Hearts and sell for 30 Rupees.

* Chilly Cream of Mushroom Soup: Made using Chillshrooms or other cold Ingredients. When eaten, these soups will increase Link's Heat Resistance by one level temporarily. They heal four of Link's Hearts, but they can also be sold for 30 Rupees.
