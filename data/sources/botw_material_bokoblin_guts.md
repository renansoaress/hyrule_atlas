Bokoblin Guts are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
In Breath of the Wild, Bokoblin Guts are Materials that are dropped by defeated Blue Bokoblins, Black Bokoblins, and Silver Bokoblins, as well as by Golden Bokoblins in Master Mode. They are also available to purchase at High Spirits Produce in Kakariko Village. They convulse on their own occasionally. Bokoblin Guts can be used with other ingredients to Cook Elixirs. At the Kochi Dye Shop, they can also be used to make Purple Dye, or they can be sold to Kilton at Fang and Bone for Mon.
