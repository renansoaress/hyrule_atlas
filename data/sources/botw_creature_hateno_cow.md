Hateno Cows are recurring Creatures in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Hateno Cow
Hateno Cows are domesticated versions of Water Buffalo that can be found grazing at settlements in East Necluda and the Faron Grasslands. They originate at Hateno Pasture in Hateno Village, and they are kept as livestock for their Fresh Milk. When they are out to pasture, their horns are decorated with bright colors. It is impossible for Link to damage or kill Hateno Cows, and as a result, they offer no drops.
