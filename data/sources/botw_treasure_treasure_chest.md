### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Treasure Chests, also known simply as Chests, are recurring Objects in série The Legend of Zelda, appearing in every title since A Link to the Past. Found in both the Overworld and in Dungeons, each Treasure Chest (with the exception of Traps) contains an item that will aid Link on his quest. Smaller Chests usually contain common items and collectibles such as Small Keys, Bombs, Arrows, and various amounts of Rupees. Larger Chests contain rarer, more important items, such as equipment, Big Keys, Pieces of Heart, and large sums of Rupees. Some Chests are locked or otherwise guarded; some will only appear after the completion of a Puzzle in the same room.
