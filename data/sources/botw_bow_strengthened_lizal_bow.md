Strengthened Lizal Bows are recurring Items and Objects in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Strengthened Lizal Bow
Strengthened Lizal Bows are upgraded Lizal Bows with a grip reinforced by metal. Their wooden body is made from flexible Trees that grow near Water, increasing their destructive power. They belong to the Lizalfos Gear equipment archetype, which includes all Weapons created by Lizalfos. They are commonly used by Blue Lizalfos on the Tabantha Frontier and in the Gerudo Desert, but they are also found in the hands of Fire-Breath Lizalfos, Ice-Breath Lizalfos, and Electric Lizalfos.

While they do have metallic components, Strengthened Lizal Bows are considered wooden Weapons in the game. Therefore, they will burn if they are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. They also float if they are dropped in Water, and they will not attract Lightning during Thunderstorms or conduct Electricity. Finally, they cannot be picked up using the Magnesis Rune.
