### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Prime Poultry Pilafs are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Prime Poultry Pilafs are Meals that are made by Cooking a Raw Bird Thigh, Hylian Rice, a Bird Egg, and Goat Butter together in a Cooking Pot. Eating them will heal some of Link's Hearts. These Pilafs are a favorite in the Region of Gerudo, and the rice used permeates the savory taste of the poultry.

Link can add a fifth Ingredient to the Cooking Pot when making Prime Poultry Pilaf, which can result in the following variations:

* Chilly Prime Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding a chilly Ingredient. Eating them will grant Link one level of Heat Resistance.

* Electro Prime Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding an electric Ingredient. If they're eaten, Link will gain one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Prime Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding an enduring Ingredient. These meals will completely replenish and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Prime Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding an energizing Ingredient. Consuming them will restore part of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Prime Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding a hasty Ingredient. Link can eat them to have his movement speed increased.

* Hearty Prime Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding a hearty Ingredient. When eaten, all of Link's Hearts will be healed and he will gain temporary Hearts.

* Mighty Prime Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding a mighty Ingredient. Link's attack power will be boosted when he eats these pilafs.

* Sneaky Prime Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding a sneaky Ingredient. When eaten, they will increase Link's Stealth abilities.

* Spicy Prime Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding a spicy Ingredient. Eating these pilafs will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.

* Tough Prime Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding a tough Ingredient. They will grant Link a boost to his Defense if he consumes them.
