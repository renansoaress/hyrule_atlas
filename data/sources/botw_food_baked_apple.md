Baked Apples are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Baked Apples are a type of Roasted Food that results from Apples making contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. They are softer and sweeter than regular Apples. By eating one, Link can heal three quarters of a Heart to his Life Gauge.

When Link first meets the Old Man at his Campfire, he can steal his Baked Apple and have the Old Man lecture him. The Old Man will then tell Link he was only teasing him, and he welcomes him to help himself to his Baked Apples.

If Link speaks to Juney, who can be found in Rito Village, she will explain that she is upset with her husband, Jogo, for bringing her there for her honeymoon, and they only way she will not divorce him is if he brings her a Baked Apple. This initiates "The Apple of My Eye" Side Quest. Returning to Juney with a Baked Apple in Link's Inventory will be able to smell it. She then asks if she can have one, promising a present for Link in return. If Link decides to give her one, she rewards him with a Silver Rupee. Juney laments that her husband does not Cook Baked Apples for her very often. She also asks Link to bring her more Baked Apples if he can.

When Link brings Baked Apples to Juney, she will pay a greater amount per Apple depending on how many are available. She buys Baked Apples in groups of one, five, 10, 30, 50, and 100.
