## Location and Uses
Great Thunderblade
Great Thunderblades are Electric, two-handed swords that Link can wield. They were forged by the Royal Family of Hyrule using Lightning from Hyrule Ridge. As a result of their elemental properties, they belong to the Magic Gear equipment archetype. Primarily, they are found in Hyrule Field and on the Tabantha Frontier. A Great Thunderblade can be found sticking out of the earth between the jutted rocks of Cuho Mountain's summit, and another can be found being wielded by a Black Moblin in the Coliseum Ruins. Great Thunderblades also have a possibility of spawning on Stalnoxes, which have to be defeated before Link can obtain them.

Being an Electric Weapon, Great Thunderblades can be used to shock Enemies and force them to drop any Weapons that they are using, making them easier to dispatch. Each swing of a Great Thunderblade temporarily drains the Electric power from it. When a Great Thunderblade is completely drained, the blade must regenerate its energy before it can cause Electric damage again. Using this sword while in this state also does not generate Electricity and pauses the regeneration for a second. Dropping a Great Thunderblade through the Inventory screen and picking it back up instantly recharges it.

In addition to shocking Enemies, Great Thunderblades can be used to temporarily fire Electric pulses into certain machines. Since they are made of metal, they also attract Lightning if equipped during Thunderstorms and conduct Electricity. They sink if dropped in Water, but they can be picked up using the Magnesis Rune. Finally, Great Thunderblades do not burn if exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate.
