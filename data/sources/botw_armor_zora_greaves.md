### Breath of the Wild
The Zora Greaves are given to Link by Laflat as a reward for completing the "Lynel Safari" Side Quest. Zora Greaves were originally made by ancient craftsman on behalf of an old Zora king. After the dam for East Reservoir Lake was finished, Zora began making them for visiting Hylians as a sign of friendship. The Zora Greaves that Laflat gives to Link were the last pair left in her father's armor shop after it closed.

Wearing the Zora Greaves will grant Swim Speed Up. They can also be worn with the rest of the Zora Set, the Zora Helm and Zora Armor, to give Link the Swim Dash Stamina Up Set Bonus. They're able to be enhanced by a Great Fairy and Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.
