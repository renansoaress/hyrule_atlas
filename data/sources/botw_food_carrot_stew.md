### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Carrot Stews are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Carrot Stews are made in Cooking Pots using Tabantha Wheat, Fresh Milk, Goat Butter, and either a Swift Carrot or an Endura Carrot. Regular Carrot Stew is made by either using both types of carrots or by using a filler as a fifth Ingredient. Consuming one will restore eleven of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge. They sell for 70 Rupees if a carrot and an additional Ingredient are used and for 120 Rupees when both carrots are used. These stews are left to simmer for a long time, which brings out the carrots' sweetness.

Using either type of carrot will result in one of two different variations of Carrot Stew:

* Enduring Carrot Stew: Made using an Endura Carrot. Eating one of these will completely replenish and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel. They also heal seven of his Hearts and sell for 100 Rupees.

* Hasty Carrot Stew: Made using a Swift Carrot. When consumed, Link's speed will temporarily increase. They also restore four of his Hearts and can be sold for 40 Rupees.
