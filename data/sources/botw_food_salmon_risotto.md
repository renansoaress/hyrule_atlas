### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Salmon Risottos are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Salmon Risotto can be made by Cooking a Hearty Salmon, Hylian Rice, Goat Butter, and Rock Salt together in a Cooking Pot. The only way to make regular Salmon Risotto is by adding a fifth ingredient that grants an effect other than hearty. Eating these Meals will heal some of Link's Hearts. This Risotto is known the be rich, as the rice permeates the light flavor of the Salmon.

In her diary, Ivee details a recipe for Mushroom Risotto. However, at the end, she suggests substituting the Hylian Shrooms used with Hearty Salmon for a "fish-lover's dish".

Link can only make one variation of Salmon Risotto:

* Hearty Salmon Risotto: Made by using the default recipe. When these meals are consumed, all of Link's Hearts will be healed, and he will gain temporary Hearts.
