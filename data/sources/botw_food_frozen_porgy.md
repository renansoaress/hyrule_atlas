### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Frozen Porgies are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Frozen Porgies are a type of Frozen Food that Link can make by freezing Armored Porgies or Mighty Porgies with Ice. Eating one will restore one of Link's Hearts and grant temporary Heat Resistance.
