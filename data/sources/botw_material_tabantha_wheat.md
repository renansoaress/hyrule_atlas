Tabantha Wheat are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Tabantha Wheat can be collected by cutting grass in the Tabantha Frontier. Alternatively, it can be purchased at The Slippery Falcon in Rito Village or from the Traveling Merchant Yammo. Eaten raw, Tabantha Wheat will restore one Heart to Link's Life Gauge. Tabantha Wheat is one of the ingredients that can be used at the Kochi Dye Shop to make Light Yellow Dye. It is grown on the Tabantha Plains.

When Cooking, Tabantha Wheat is needed to make stews and breads. In particular, it's necessary to make the following Meals:

- Apple Pie | Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter, Apple
- Carrot Cake | Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter, Any Carrot
- Carrot Stew | Any Carrot, Fresh Milk, Goat Butter, Tabantha Wheat
- Clam Chowder | Fresh Milk, Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, Hearty Blueshell Snail

- Fried Bananas | Mighty Bananas, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar
- Fruit Pie | Any Fruit (besides Apple), Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter
- Fruitcake | Apple or Wildberry, Any Fruit, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar
- Gourmet Meat Stew | Any Gourmet Meat, Tabantha Wheat, Fresh Milk, Goat Butter
- Honey Crepe | Fresh Milk, Bird Egg, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Courser Bee Honey
- Meat Pie | Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, Rock Salt, Any Meat
- Meat Stew | Raw Meat or Raw Bird Drumstick, Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, Fresh Milk
