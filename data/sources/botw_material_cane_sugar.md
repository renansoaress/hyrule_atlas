### Breath of the Wild
Cane Sugar is a Material that Link can purchase from The Slippery Falcon, the Goron Gusto Shop, the General Shoppe, and the travelling Merchant Yammo. At the Kochi Dye Shop, Cane Sugar is an ingredient that can be used to make Green Dye. When Cane Sugar is boiled, it becomes a sweet juice that can be used to make cake.

When Cooking, Cane Sugar is required for the following Meals:

- Apple Pie | Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter, Apple
- Carrot Cake | Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter, Any Carrot
- Egg Pudding | Fresh Milk, Bird Egg, Cane Sugar
- Egg Tart | Tabantha Wheat, Bird Egg, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter
- Fried Bananas | Mighty Bananas, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar
- Fruit Pie | Any Fruit (besides Apple), Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter
- Fruitcake | Apple or Wildberry, Any Fruit, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar
- Honey Crepe | Fresh Milk, Bird Egg, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Courser Bee Honey
- Monster Cake | Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter, Monster Extract
- Nutcake | Acorn or Chickaloo Tree Nut, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter
- Plain Crepe | Fresh Milk, Cane Sugar, Tabantha Wheat, Bird Egg, Goat Butter
- Pumpkin Pie | Fortified Pumpkin, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter
- Wildberry Crepe | Wildberry, Fresh Milk, Cane Sugar, Tabantha Wheat, Bird Egg
