## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Savage Lynel Spear
Savage Lynel Spears are Weapons used by White-Maned Lynels, Silver Lynels, and Golden Lynels. Due to the axe-like design of their spearheads and their exceptional weight, they have extreme destructive power. They are commonly used in the Gerudo Highlands and Hebra Mountains. These Spears belong to the Lynel Gear equipment archetype, which is a series of Weapons created by Lynels.

Being made of metal, Savage Lynel Spears conduct Electricity and attract Lightning during Thunderstorms, though they cannot burn when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. They will also sink if they are dropped in water, but can be manipulated and picked up using the Magnesis Rune.
