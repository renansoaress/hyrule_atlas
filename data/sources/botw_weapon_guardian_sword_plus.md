## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Guardian Sword+
Guardian Swords+ are Weapons wielded by Guardian Scouts III in "A Moderate Test of Strength" Ancient Shrines. They can be obtained by defeating the Guardian Scouts that use them. In addition to this, they have a chance to appear in Treasure Chests that spawn after scanning the Breath of the Wild Series Guardian amiibo with the amiibo Rune. Guardian Swords+ are more powerful than regular Guardian Swords, and they slightly more durability as well.

Being made from Ancient Technology, Guardian Swords+ will deal additional damage when used again Guardian-type Enemies. They will float if they are dropped in Water, and they will not burn if exposed to Fire or equipped in a Scorching Climate. Finally, the Magnesis Rune will not be able to pick them up, and they do not conduct Electricity or attract Lightning during Thunderstorms.

Guardian Swords+ belong to the Guardian Gear equipment archetype, which includes all Weapons used by Guardian Scouts in Ancient Shrines.
