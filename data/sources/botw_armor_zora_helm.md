### Breath of the Wild
The Zora Helm is a piece of Armor that can be found in Toto Lake. Wearing it will grant Link Swim Speed Up and allow him to execute a Spin Attack while swimming. The Zora Helm, alongside the Zora Armor and Zora Greaves, compose the Zora Set. It can be enhanced by a Great Fairy or Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.
