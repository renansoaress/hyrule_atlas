### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Egg Puddings are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Egg Puddings are made by combining Fresh Milk, a Bird Egg, and Cane Sugar in a Cooking Pot. When eaten, they heal six of Link's Hearts. They also sell for 20 Rupees. They are made by cooking eggs and milk in a special mold, and they are known to have a soft texture that melts in one's mouth.

Link can find a recipe for Egg Pudding written in Ivee's Diary. In it, Ivee lists the ingredients as some Bird Eggs, a few bottles of Fresh Milk, and several servings of Cane Sugar. Though she knows Cane Sugar is difficult to come by, she states that the dish will not work without it.

By using additional ingredients, different variations of Egg Pudding can be made:

* Hearty Egg Pudding: Eating one will heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum number of Hearts. Additionally, they sell for 40 Rupees.

* Energizing Egg Pudding: When eaten, Link's Stamina Wheel will be refilled. They also heal him for four Hearts and sell for 40 Rupees.

* Enduring Egg Pudding: Not only will these restore Link's Stamina Wheel when eaten, they will also overfill it. They heal Link for five Hearts and sell for 40 Rupees.

* Hasty Egg Pudding: If eaten, these will temporarily increase Link's movement speed. They restore three Hearts to Link's Life Gauge, and they'll sell for 50 Rupees.

* Spicy Egg Pudding: Eating one will temporarily increase Link's Cold Resistance by one level. They also heal four Hearts in his Life Gauge, and they can be sold for 30 Rupees.

* Electro Egg Pudding: They grant Link one level of Shock Resistance when consumed. They also heal him for four Hearts, but they sell for 40 Rupees.

* Mighty Egg Pudding: When eaten, they'll temporarily boost Link's attack power. They also heal for four Hearts, and they can be sold for 40 Rupees.

* Tough Egg Pudding: Eating one will temporarily increase Link's defense. In addition, they'll heal four Hearts in his Life Gauge, and they sell for 40 Rupees.

* Sneaky Egg Pudding: When eaten, they'll grant Link a temporary Stealth boost. They also restore four Hearts to his Life Gauge, and they're worth 30 Rupees when sold.

* Chilly Egg Pudding: Eating one increases Link's Heat Resistance by one level. They also heal Link for seven Hearts and sell for 40 Rupees.
