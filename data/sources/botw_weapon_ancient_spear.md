### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Ancient Spears are Items in Breath of the Wild.

## Location and Uses
Ancient Spear
Ancient Spears are Weapons that can be made at the Akkala Ancient Tech Lab using the Ancient Oven. To make one, Link will need to provide 15 Ancient Gears, five Ancient Shafts, and two Ancient Cores. They are the result of countless hours of research into Ancient Technology done by Robbie. This is common among many pieces of Ancient Soldier Gear, the equipment archetype to which Ancient Spears belong. The spearhead glows and has incredible piercing power.

Being made of Ancient Technology, Ancient Spears will deal additional damage when they are used against Guardians. They will not attract Lightning during Thunderstorms, will float on Water, and cannot burn if exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate.

## Trivia
* An unused icon and description for Ancient Spears exist within the files of Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity.
