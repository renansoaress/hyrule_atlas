Frost Taluses are recurring Bosses and Sub-Bosses in the série The Legend of Zelda.(BotW, TotK, CoH, AoC, AoI)

### Breath of the Wild
Frost Talus
Frost Taluses are Stone Taluses composed of enchanted Ice. They can be found in cold, snowy areas throughout the Hebra and Gerudo Regions, particularly the Hebra Mountains and Gerudo Highlands. These Taluses disguise themself as Icy rock formations. They are coated in Ice and will freeze Link upon contact, making it impossible for Link to climb them normally.

Link can climb a Frost Talus when the Unfreezable Set Bonus is active, which can be achieved by wearing the upgraded Snowquill Set or Zant's Helmet. Alternatively, he can melt its Ice using a Fire-based attack, such as Fire Arrows. The rock chunks that they launch at Link will always be coated in Ice. Link can defeat a Frost Talus the same way as every other variant of Talus, which is by inflicting damage to their Ore Deposits through melee attacks, firing Arrows, or using Urbosa's Fury.

As part of the "Face the Frost Talus" Side Quest, Gesane reveals that he saw a Frost Talus at Coldsnap Hollow in the Hebra Mountains, and he believes a Champion would be able to defeat it. To complete the Quest, Link must defeat this Frost Talus. Once it has been killed, Gesane will give Link a Silver Rupee as a reward.

After defeating Calamity Ganon for the first time, Link is able to ask Kilton about Taluses. Since he is unable to approach them due to their size, Kilton asks Link to kill all of the Taluses in Hyrule Kingdom so he can examine the scent they leave behind. This task includes all Frost Taluses as well. When Link has accomplished this task, Kilton will give him the Medal of Honor: Talus as a reward. From this research, Kilton is able to learn about Taluses' musculoskeletal structure and diet.
