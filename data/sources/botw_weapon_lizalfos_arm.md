### Breath of the Wild
Lizalfos Arm
Lizalfos Arms are the severed arms of Stalizalfos, and they can only be acquired by defeating them. When thrown, they will act as Boomerangs, and they will eventually loop back around to return to Link. They continue to move and struggle, even after being disconnected, and they are very brittle.

Being made of bone, Lizalfos Arms will float if they are dropped in the Water. They also do not attract Lightning during Thunderstorms or conduct Electricity, nor will they burn if exposed to Fire or equipped in a Scorching Climate.
