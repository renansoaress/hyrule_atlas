### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Sneaky River Escargots are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Sneaky River Escargots are a type of Roasted Food, and they can be made by burning a Sneaky River Snail near Fire or a Scorching Climate. They can found as a drop after defeating a Blue Hinox. Their meat is supposed to be soft and chewy, and pulling it out of the shell whole provides one with a sense of accomplishment. Eating one restores one and a half Hearts to Link.
