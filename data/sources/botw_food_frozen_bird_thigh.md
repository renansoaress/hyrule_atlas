### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Frozen Bird Thighs are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Frozen Bird Thighs are a type of Frozen Food, and they can be made by chilling Raw Bird Thighs in a Freezing Climate or Cold Water. They are frozen solid, which makes them difficult to eat. Eating one will restore one and a half Hearts and grant Link temporary Heat Resistance.
