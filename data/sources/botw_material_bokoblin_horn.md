Bokoblin Horns are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Bokoblin Horns are Monster Parts that are dropped by all types of Bokoblins and Stalkoblins upon defeat. They can be used with critters in a Cooking Pot to Cook Elixirs. The Kochi Dye Shop accepts Bokoblin Horns as ingredients for Gray Dye. They can also be sold to Kilton at Fang and Bone for three Mon.
