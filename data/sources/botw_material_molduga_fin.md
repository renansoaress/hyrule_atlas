Molduga Fins are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Molduga Fins are Monster Parts than are obtained by defeating Molduga. They can be used with critters in a Cooking Pot to make Elixirs. They can also be exchanged for Mon at the Fang and Bone or used as an ingredient for Gray Dye at the Kochi Dye Shop.

The Sand Boots require Molduga Fins in order to be upgraded to their first and second tiers at a Great Fairy Fountain.
