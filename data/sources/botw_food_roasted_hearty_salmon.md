### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Hearty Salmons are a type of Roasted Food, being the result of Hearty Salmons that have been exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. They have a nice aroma and edible skin. Blue Hinoxes sometimes drop them when killed by Link. Eating one restores four and a half Hearts to Link's Life Gauge.
