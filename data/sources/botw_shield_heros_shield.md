### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Sea-Breeze Shields,(Tears of the Kingdom) also known as Hero's Shields,(TWW, BotW) and Hylian Shields,(SSBB) are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda. The Sea-Breeze Shield goes unnamed in , Super Smash Bros. for Wii U/3DS, and Super Smash Bros. Ultimate.

### Breath of the Wild
The Hero's Shield from the Era of the Great Sea appears in Breath of the Wild. It is obtained randomly when 30th Anniversary series The Wind Waker-themed Princess Zelda amiibo is used is scanned using the amiibo Rune. Multiple can be obtained through repeatedly scanning the Rune. It is said this Shield was the favorite of a hero who travelled the open seas. This Shield was apparently a family heirloom, passed down through generations.

Being made of metal, the Hero's Shield will conduct Electricity and attract Lightning if it is equipped during a Thunderstorm. It will also sink if it is dropped in Water. However, it can be picked up with the Magnesis Rune, and it will not burn like wooden Shields do when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate.
