## Locations and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Spiked Boko Club
Spiked Boko Clubs are common Weapons throughout Hyrule. Sharpened bones have been added to them to increase their damage. These Clubs belong to the Bokoblin Gear equipment archetype, which includes all Weapons created by Bokoblins. Link may first see one in a Monster Stronghold near the Great Plateau Tower, where it is being wielded by a Blue Bokoblin. After that, Link will find them in various Bokoblin Monster Strongholds on his adventure, particularly in the Faron Grasslands and East Necluda. They can also appear in Treasure Chests that spawn after the Breath of the Wild Series Bokoblin amiibo has been scanned with the amiibo Rune.

Due to their wooden composition, Spiked Boko Clubs will burn if exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. Enemy Bokoblins will often ignite them at a Campfire before attacking to deal additional Fire damage. They also do not attract Lightning during Thunderstorms or conduct Electricity like metallic Weapons do, and they will float on Water if dropped. However, the Magnesis Rune is unable to pick them up.
