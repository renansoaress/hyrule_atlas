### Breath of the Wild
The Barbarian Leg Wraps are a piece of Armor located in Qaza Tokki Shrine at the center of the North Lomei Labyrinth. After obtaining them, Granté will begin selling the piece of Armor for 4,000 Rupees. According to their description, the Leg Wraps were worn by the warriors of an ancient warlike tribe from the Faron Region. When worn, they provide Link with one level of Attack Up. The Barbarian Leg Wraps can be enhanced by a Great Fairy and can be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Barbarian Leg Wraps, alongside the Barbarian Helm and Barbarian Armor, compose the Barbarian Set. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides a Charge Atk. Stamina Up Set Bonus.
