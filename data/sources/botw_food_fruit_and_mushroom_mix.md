### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Fruit and Mushroom Mixes are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Fruit and Mushroom mixes are made by Cooking any Mushroom and any Fruit together in a Cooking Pot. Their regular version is made by using either ingredients without status or Ingredients with differing effects. Ingredients lacking statuses that can be used are Apples, Palm Fruits, Wildberries, and Hylian Shrooms. Eating them will restore some Hearts to Link. The sweetness of Fruit in this dish is said to contrast with the savoriness of the Mushrooms.

Depending on the Ingredients used, several different versions of Fruit and Mushroom Mix can be made:

* Hearty Fruit and Mushroom Mix: Made by using Hearty Durians, Hearty Truffles, or Big Hearty Truffles. Eating one will completely heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.

* Energizing Fruit and Mushroom Mix: Made using Stamella Shrooms. When eaten, they will replenish a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Enduring Fruit and Mushroom Mix: Made using Endura Shrooms. Consuming these mixes completely refills and overfills Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Fruit and Mushroom Mix: Made using Fleet-Lotus Seeds or Rushrooms. If eaten, they will temporarily boost Link's movement speed.

* Spicy Fruit and Mushroom Mix: Made using Spicy Peppers or Sunshrooms. Eating them will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance for a short period of time.

* Electro Fruit and Mushroom Mix: Made using Voltfruits or Zapshrooms. They give Link one level of Shock Resistance when eaten.

* Mighty Fruit and Mushroom Mix: Made using Mighty Bananas or Razorshrooms. If eaten, they increase the power of Link's attacks for a time.

* Tough Fruit and Mushroom Mix: Made using Ironshrooms. Eating them increases Link's defense temporarily.

* Sneaky Fruit and Mushroom Mix: Made using Silent Shrooms. Eating these mixes will increase Link's Stealth for a short period.

* Chilly Fruit and Mushroom Mix: Made using Hydromelons or Chillshrooms. When eaten, they grant Link one level of Heat Resistance temporarily.
