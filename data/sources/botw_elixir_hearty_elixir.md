### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Hearty Elixirs are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Drinking Hearty Elixirs will restore all of Link's Hearts, in addition to granting him Extra Hearts. As Link takes damage, these Extra Hearts are lost.

Hearty Elixirs can be made by Cooking any of the following critters with Monster Parts in a Cooking Pot:
* Hearty Lizards

By adding any of these hearty Ingredients, Link can increase the number of temporary Hearts he receives:
* Hearty Bass
* Hearty Blueshell Snails
* Hearty Durians
* Hearty Radishes
* Big Hearty Radishes
* Hearty Salmons
* Hearty Truffles
* Big Hearty Truffles
