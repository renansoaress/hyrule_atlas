### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Hinox Guts are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Hinox Guts are dropped by all three varieties of Hinoxes when defeated. As is the case with other Monster Parts, they can be cooked in a Cooking Pot with critters to make Elixirs. They can also be used to make Yellow Dye at the Kochi Dye Shop or exchanged for Mon with Kilton at the Fang and Bone.
