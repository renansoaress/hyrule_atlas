### Breath of the Wild
Farmer's Pitchfork
Farmer's Pitchforks are sharp, light tools used to easily collect hay. However, Link can use them as Weapons against Enemies. They are most common around villages, such as Hateno Village, and Stables in East Necluda and West Necluda.

With their metal prongs, Farmer's Pitchforks will attract Lightning when they are equipped during a Thunderstorm and conduct Electricity. They will also sink in Water, but they can be picked up using the Magnesis Rune on the Sheikah Slate. Finally, they will not burn when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate.
