### Breath of the Wild
The Zora Set is an Armor Set consisting of the Zora Helm, Zora Armor, and Zora Greaves. All three pieces are related to quests in or near Zora's Domain:

*The Zora Helm is found in a sunken treasure chest at Toto Lake, hinted at during the "Zora Stone Monuments" Side Quest.
*The Zora Armor is received from King Dorephan during the "Divine Beast Vah Ruta" Main Quest.
*The Zora Greaves are obtained as a reward for showing Laflat a photo of a Lynel for the "Lynel Safari" Side Quest.

Each piece increases Link's movement speed while swimming. Furthermore, the Zora Armor enables Link to swim up waterfalls, while the Zora Helm enables Link to perform a Spin Attack while swimming.  If all three pieces are worn and have been upgraded at least twice by a Great Fairy, they provide the Swim Dash Stamina Up set bonus, significantly reducing the stamina cost for dashing or spin attack.
