## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Dragonbone Moblin Spear
Dragonbone Moblin Spears are Weapons favored by Black Moblins, Silver Moblins, and Golden Moblins. Their spearheads are made from fossilized bones with added spikes, increasing their attack power. They belong to the Moblin Gear equipment archetype, which is a series of Weapons made by Moblins. Most commonly, they can be found in Monster Strongholds in the Hebra Mountains and Eldin Mountains.

Being made of wood, Dragonbone Moblin Spears will burn if they are exposed to Fire or equipped in a Scorching Climate. They will also float when they are dropped in Water. Moblin Spears do not attract Lightning during Thunderstorms or conduct Electricity, and they cannot be picked up with the Magnesis Rune.
