Naydra's Claws are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Naydra's Claws can be obtained by shooting the claws of Naydra as it flies by. Naydra can be found on Mount Lanayru, from which it will fly down to either the Lanayru Promenade or Lanayru Bay. When Cooking with Naydraw's Claws, the resulting meal will always be Dubious Food. Naydra's Claws can be used at the Kochi Dye Shop to make Light Blue Dye.

Two Claws, as well as five Courser Bee Honeys, are needed to upgrade the Tunic of the Wild to its second tier at a Great Fairy Fountain. One can also be used to upgrade the Fierce Deity Armor to its second tier.
