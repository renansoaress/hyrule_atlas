### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Endura Carrots are a type of Roasted Food, being the result of Endura Carrots that have made contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. Roasted Endura Carrots are sweet and considered to be an extravagant dish. Link can heal four Hearts by eating a Roasted Endura Carrot.
