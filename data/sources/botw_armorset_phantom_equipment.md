### Breath of the Wild
The Phantom Equipment was added in  Downloadable Content pack. It is Armor Set composed of the Phantom Helmet, Phantom Armor, and Phantom Greaves. They're available through the Side Quest "EX Treasure: Phantasma", which can be obtained by reading Misko's EX Journal at the Outpost Ruins. They can be found at the following locations:
* The Coliseum Ruins
* The Sacred Ground Ruins
* The Hyrule Garrison Ruins

Wearing the Phantom Equipment does not provide a Set Bonus, though wearing the full set will "scare" people Link encounters. Each individual piece will increase Link's attack power. After each piece is obtained, Granté will begin to sell them in Tarrey Town. They cannot be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop nor can they be enhanced by a Great Fairy. Their design is based on Phantom Zelda from .
