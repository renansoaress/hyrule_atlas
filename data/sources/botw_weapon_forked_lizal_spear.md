## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Forked Lizal Spear
Forked Lizal Spears are Weapons used by high-tier Lizalfos, such as Black Lizalfos, Silver Lizalfos, and Golden Lizalfos. Fire-Breath Lizalfos, Ice-Breath Lizalfos, and Electric Lizalfos also use these Spears, especially in the Eldin Canyon and Akkala Highlands. They are wielded by skilled Lizalfos warriors, and their dual blades are known to inflict brutal wounds. They belong to the Lizalfos Gear equipment archetype, a series of Weapons created by Lizalfos.

Due to their metal spearheads, Forked Lizal Spears will attract Lightning if they are equipped during a Thunderstorm and conduct Electricity. They will also sink if dropped in Water, but they can be picked up again using the Magnesis Rune. If they are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate, Forked Lizal Spears will not burn like wooden Weapons do.
