### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Fried Eggs and Rice are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Fried Egg and Rice are Meals that can be made by Cooking predominantly Bird Eggs and Hylian Rice together. If consumed, they restore at least six of Link's Hearts. The soft egg yolks of this dish are known to pair well with the fresh rice.

Adding additional Ingredients can result in several different variations of Fried Eggs and Rice:

* Hearty Fried Egg and Rice: Made by adding hearty Ingredients. Eating these will completely heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum number of Hearts.

* Energizing Fried Egg and Rice: Made by adding stamina-related Ingredients. If eaten, they will refill of a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Enduring Fried Egg and Rice: Made by adding enduring Ingredients. Consuming these dishes will completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Fried Egg and Rice: Made by adding speed-boosting Ingredients. Eating them will increase Link's movement speed for a short time.

* Spicy Fried Egg and Rice: Made by adding warm Ingredients. Link will gain one level of Cold Resistance when these are eaten.

* Electro Fried Egg and Rice: Made by adding electricity-related Ingredients. When eaten, they will grant Link one level of Shock Resistance.

* Mighty Fried Egg and Rice: Made by adding mighty Ingredients. Eating one of these dishes will increase Link's attack power for a short period of time.

* Tough Fried Egg and Rice: Made by adding defensive Ingredients. If they are eaten, they will temporarily increase Link's defense.

* Sneaky Fried Egg and Rice: Made by adding stealthy Ingredients. When these dishes are consumed, they wil provide a temporary boost to Link's Stealth.

* Chilly Fried Egg and Rice: Made by adding cold Ingredients. Eating them will increase Link's Heat Resistance by one level.
