Toasty Razorshrooms are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Toasty Razorshrooms are a type of Roasted Food, and they are made by exposing Razorshrooms to Fire or a Scorching Climate. The roasting process softens the exterior of the Razorshroom. Eating one restores three-quarters of a Heart to Link.
