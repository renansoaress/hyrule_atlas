Silver Bokoblins are recurring Enemies in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
March 2020
Silver Bokoblin
Silver Bokoblins are much more clever, powerful, and dangerous than the other three lower-tiered Bokoblins. Silver Bokoblins tend to use similar Weapons to the Black Bokoblins, including the Dragonbone Boko Clubs, Bows, and Shields.

The Silver Bokoblin retains all the skills other Bokoblins have, like kicking and avoiding Remote Bombs. New to this Bokoblin is the faster reaction time and use of more powerful attack patterns. When first encountered, they tend to use a double slash attack and sometimes a slower single slash attack as well. They also use their jump and swinging attacks more often, lasting much longer than the weaker Bokoblins.

Silver Bokoblins are much stronger than all other Bokoblin variants due to being influenced by Ganon's Dark Magic, only being outclassed by the Golden Bokoblins introduced during Master Mode. They carry stronger Weapons than other Bokoblins and may drop Bokoblin Horns, Bokoblin Fangs, Bokoblin Guts, Amber, Opal, Topaz, Rubies, Sapphires, and Diamonds when defeated.
