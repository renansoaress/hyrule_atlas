### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Plain Crepes are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Plain Crepes can be made by Cooking Fresh Milk, Cane Sugar, Tabantha Wheat, a Bird Egg, and Goat Butter together in a Cooking Pot. When eaten, they will restore some of Link's Hearts. Because this dish is so simple, the flavor of the ingredients is able to shine.

Since Plain Crepes require five ingredients to make, no variations of them exist.
