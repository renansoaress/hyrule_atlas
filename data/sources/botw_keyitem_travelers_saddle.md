### Breath of the Wild
The Traveler's Saddle is a type of Saddle and Horse Gear. It can be obtained from a pair of Treasure Chests that appear when the Link (Rider) amiibo is scanned with the amiibo Rune. One of the Treasure Chests will contain the Traveler's Saddle, while the other Chest will contain the Traveler's Bridle.

Once obtained, the Traveler's Saddle will appear in Link's Key Item Inventory. Link can equip it to any Horse he has a maximum Bond with by talking to specific workers at Highland Stable, Outskirt Stable, South Akkala Stable, or Woodland Stable. However, like other Saddles, they cannot be used by Epona or the Giant Horse. Traveler's Saddles were designed for comfort and hold strong throughout all sorts of Weather, which has lead to them being favored by travelers.

In his Recovered Memories, Link is shown to have a Traveler's Bridle and Traveler's Saddle equipped to his Horse before the second Great Calamity.
