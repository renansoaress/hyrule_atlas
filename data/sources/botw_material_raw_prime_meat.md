### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Raw Prime Meats are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Raw Prime Meat can be obtained by killed Red-Tusked Boars, Mountain Bucks, Mountain Does, Water Buffalo, Snowcoat Foxes, Maraudo Wolves, Wasteland Coyotes, Cold-Footed Wolves, Tabantha Moose, Great-Horned Rhinoceros, Honeyvore Bears, and Grizzlemaw Bears. Alternatively, it can be purchased from the travelling merchant Mezer near Dueling Peaks Stable. When eaten, Raw Prime Meat heals one and a half Hearts. Raw Prime Meat can be burned by Fire to create Seared Prime Steak or frozen by Ice to create Icy Prime Meat.

Either Raw Prime Meat or a Raw Bird Thigh is required to make Prime Meat Stew, Salt-Grilled Prime Meat, and Prime Meat and Seafood Fry. Among other Meats, they can also be used to make Creamy Meat Soup, Glazed Meat, a Meat and Mushroom Skewer, Meat Pie, a Meat Skewer, Copious Meat Skewers, Meaty Rice Balls, Monster Stew, Pepper Steak, Steamed Meat, and a Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin.

It is required to Cook the following Meals:

- Prime Meat and Rice Bowl | Raw Prime Meat, Hylian Rice, Rock Salt
- Prime Meat Curry | Raw Prime Meat, Hylian Rice, Goron Spice
- Prime Spiced Meat Skewer | Raw Prime Meat, Goron Spice
