## Location and Uses
Frozen Crabs are a variety of Frozen Food. Link can make Frozen Crabs by exposing Razorclaw Crabs, Ironshell Crabs, or Bright-Eyed Crabs to Freezing Climates or Cold Water. They are noted not to offer much companionship, but they are guaranteed to not spoil. Eating one will restore one Heart to Link's Life Gauge and grant temporary Heat Resistance.
