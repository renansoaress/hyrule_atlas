### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Radishes are a type of Roasted Food. They are made when Hearty Radishes are toasted by Fire or a Scorching Climate. While they offer no Added Effects, they are considered to be fragrant and warm. Eating one recovers three and three-quarters Hearts to Link's Life Gauge.
