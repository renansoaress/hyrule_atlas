### Breath of the Wild
Chillshroom
Chillshrooms are found at the base of Trees in the Hebra Mountains and on Mount Lanayru. They can also be bought in Gerudo Town from Ardin's Stall. When eaten, they restore half of a Heart. A Chillshroom that is exposed to Fire will turn into a Toasty Chillshroom. Food made using a Chillshroom will grant Link Heat Resistance. They can also be used at the Kochi Dye Shop to create Blue Dye.

Chillshrooms, among other Mushrooms, can be used in a Cooking Pot to make the following Meals:

- Cream of Mushroom Soup | Any Mushroom, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Fish and Mushroom Skewer | Any Fish, Any Mushroom
- Fragrant Mushroom Sauté | Goron Spice, Any Mushroom
- Fruit and Mushroom Mix | Any Fruit, Any Mushroom
- Glazed Mushrooms | Courser Bee Honey, Any Mushroom
- Meat and Mushroom Skewer | Any Meat, Any Mushroom
- Mushroom Omelet | Any Mushroom, Bird Egg, Goat Butter, Rock Salt
- Mushroom Rice Balls | Hylian Rice, Any Mushroom
- Mushroom Risotto | Hylian Rice, Goat Butter, Rock Salt, Any Mushroom
- Mushroom Skewer | Any Mushroom
- Copious Mushroom Skewers | Any Mushrooms (4 different kinds)
- Salt-Grilled Mushrooms | Any Mushroom, Rock Salt
- Steamed Mushrooms | Any Mushroom, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
