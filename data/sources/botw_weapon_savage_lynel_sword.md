## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Savage Lynel Sword
Savage Lynel Swords are used by White-Maned Lynels, Silver Lynels, and Golden Lynels. As it takes time for Lynels to scale up, Savage Lynel Swords may not be available until later in the game. However, the White-Maned Lynel in the Second Gatehouse of Hyrule Castle does not scale, meaning it will always carry a Savage Lynel Sword. They belong to the Lynel Gear equipment archetype, a series of Weapons that are created and utilized by Lynels. The blades of this Sword are said to be strong enough to cut down any foe.

Since they are made of metal, Savage Lynel Swords will attract Lightning when equipped during Thunderstorm and conduct Electricity. They can also be picked up using the Magnesis Rune, and they will not burn if exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. If they are dropped in Water, Savage Lynel Swords will sink.
