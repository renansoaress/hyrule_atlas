Hearty Salmons are recurring Creatures and Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Hearty Salmon
Hearty Salmons inhabit cold bodies of Water within the Hebra Mountains and the Tabantha Frontier. Their bodies accrete layers of fat in response to this, insulating them from their cold surroundings. Eating them raw restores four Hearts to Link's Life Gauge. Exposed to fire, Hearty Salmons become Roasted Hearty Salmons. Alternatively, they can be exposed to Ice to become Frozen Hearty Salmon. Meals made using Hearty Salmons will grant Link Extra Hearts when eaten.

During the Shrine Quest "Recital at Warbler's Nest", Kotts can be found fishing for Hearty Salmon at the Fishpond in Front of the Village. When reminded of the recital, she will give Link a Hearty Salmon for Genli.

Hearty Salmons are required to use while Cooking to make the following Meals:

- Salmon Meunière | Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, Hearty Salmon
- Salmon Risotto | Hearty Salmon, Hylian Rice, Goat Butter, Rock Salt

As with other Fish, Hearty Salmons can be used to make the following Meals:

- Fish Skewer | Any Fish
- Fish and Mushroom Skewer | Any Fish, Any Mushroom
- Salt-Grilled Fish | Rock Salt, Any Fish or Snail

Hearty Salmons are also among the Seafood that can be used to make the following Meals:

- Creamy Seafood Soup | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Fish Pie | Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, Rock Salt, Any Seafood
- Glazed Seafood | Courser Bee Honey, Any Seafood
- Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Gourmet Meat, Any Seafood
- Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Meat, Any Seafood
- Monster Stew | Any Meat, Any Seafood, Monster Extract
- Pepper Seafood | Spicy Pepper, Any Seafood
- Prime Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Prime Meat, Any Seafood
- Seafood Meunière | Any Seafood (except Porgy or Salmon), Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter
- Seafood Rice Balls | Hylian Rice, Any Seafood
- Copious Seafood Skewers | Any Seafood (4 different kinds)
- Steamed Fish | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable,, Herb, or Flower
