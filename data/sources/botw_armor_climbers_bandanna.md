### Breath of the Wild
The Climber's Bandanna is a piece of Armor located in the Ree Dahee Shrine at Dueling Peaks. After obtaining it, Granté will begin selling the piece of Armor for 4,000 Rupees. It is infused with ancient technology, despite what its plain appearance would suggest. When worn, it provides one level of Climb Speed Up. The Climber's Bandanna can be Enhanced by Great Fairies and can be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Climber's Bandanna, alongside the Climbing Gear and Climbing Boots, compose the Climbing Set. When each piece of the Set has been Upgraded at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides a Climb Jump Stamina Up Set Bonus.
