### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Seafood Skewers are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Seafood Skewers are made by Cooking at least one Crab or Snail in a Cooking Pot. Link can make their default version can be made by utilizing Seafood that has contradicting effects. When eaten, they will restore some of Link's Hearts. The natural water in this Meal creates a delicious broth.

To achieve a status effect from Seafood Skewers, several Ingredients of the same effect must be utilized.

* Hearty Seafood Skewer: Made by using any combination of Hearty Bass or Hearty Salmons with Hearty Blueshell Snails. They will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him Extra Hearts when eaten.

* Energizing Seafood Skewer: Made by using Staminoka Basses and Bright-Eyed Crabs. Eating them will partially refill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Mighty Seafood Skewer: Made by using any combination of Mighty Carp or Mighty Porgies with Ironshell Crabs. When eaten, Link will receive a boost to the power of his attacks.

* Tough Seafood Skewer: Made by using any combination of Armored Carp or Armored Porgies with Ironshell Crabs. Link will gain a boost to his defensive abilities when he eats these skewers.

* Sneaky Seafood Skewer: Made by using Stealthfin Trouts and Sneaky River Snails. By eating them, Link will receive a boost to his Stealth abilities.
