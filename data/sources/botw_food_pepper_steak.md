Pepper Steaks are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.(BotW, TotK)

### Breath of the Wild
Pepper Steaks can be made by Cooking a Spicy Pepper with any Meat in a Cooking Pot. Their default version can be made by adding additional ingredients, such as Mushrooms or Vegetables, that provide effects that contradict the Spicy Peppers. Eating them will heal some of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge. The meat in these dishes is cooked in crushed peppers, which suppresses their gamy taste while highlighting their flavor.

Only one variation of Pepper Steak exists:

* Spicy Pepper Steak: Made by using the original recipe. When eaten, these steaks will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.
