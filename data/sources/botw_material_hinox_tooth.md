### Breath of the Wild
Hinox Teeth are Monster Parts dropped by Hinoxes and Stalnox upon their defeats. Like other Monster Parts, they can be used in a Cooking Pot to make Elixirs. They can also be used as an ingredient for Brown Dye at the Kochi Dye Shop or exchanged for Mon at the Fang and Bone.

The Fierce Deity Set requires Hinox Teeth in order to be upgraded to its second tier at a Great Fairy Fountain.
