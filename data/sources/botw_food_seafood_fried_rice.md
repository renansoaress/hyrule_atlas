### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Seafood Fried Rices are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Seafood Fried Rice can be made by Cooking Hylian Rice, a piece of Rock Salt, and either a Hearty Blueshell Snail or a Porgy in a Cooking Pot. To make the default version, Link must use two seafood Ingredients with contradicting effects. When they are eaten, Link will have some of his Hearts restored to his Life Gauge. These Meals are tastier when stronger Flames are used to cook them.

Based on the type of seafood used, the three following variations of Seafood Fried Rice can be made:

* Hearty Seafood Fried Rice: Made by using Hearty Blueshell Snails. These Meals will completely heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him Extra Hearts.

* Mighty Seafood Fried Rice: Made by using Mighty Porgies. If eaten, Link will receive a boost to the power of his attacks.

* Tough Seafood Fried Rice: Made by using Armored Porgies. Link can eat them to receive an increase to his defensive abilities.
