### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Trouts are a type of Roasted Food, and they can be made by exposing Chillfin Trouts, Sizzlefin Trouts, Voltfin Trout, or Stealthfin Trouts to a Fire or Scorching Climate. Blue Hinoxes also have a chance to drop Roasted Trouts when killed by Link. Their flesh is soft, flaky, and has a mild flavor. When eaten, Roasted Trouts restore one and a half Hearts to Link.
