Frozen Carps are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Frozen Carps are a type of Frozen Food, and they can be made by exposing Mighty Carp, Armored Carp, or Sanke Carp to Freezing Climates or Cold Water. Their icy texture makes them a delicacy. Eating one will restore one of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Heat Resistance.
