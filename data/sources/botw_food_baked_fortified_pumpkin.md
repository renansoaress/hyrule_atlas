Baked Fortified Pumpkins are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
In Breath of the Wild, Baked Fortified Pumpkins are a type of Roasted Food, and they are the result of Fortified Pumpkins that have been exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. They have a hard rind on the outside and steamy flesh on the inside. By eating one, Link can heal three-quarters of a Heart.
