### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Light Arrows,(OoT, MM, TWW, TP, BotW) also known as Arrows of Light,(OoT, AoC) are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.The Light Arrow is also known as the Arrow of Light in Ocarina of Time. However, this name was erased and retconned to Light Arrow in .

### Breath of the Wild
Link receives the Bow of Light from Zelda during the fight with Dark Beast Ganon in Breath of the Wild. By scanning the  Zelda amiibo, Link can also receive the Twilight Bow. Both Bows can shoot an unlimited amount of Light Arrows. They must be used to attack Ganon's glowing points in order to damage him.
