### Breath of the Wild
The Moblin Mask is a piece of Armor that can be purchased from Kilton at the Fang and Bone shop for 199 Mon after freeing one Divine Beast. It cannot be enhanced by a Great Fairy, nor can it be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. When worn, it grants Link the Disguise Added Effect, allowing him to blend in with Moblins. However, the Moblin Mask does not prevent other Enemies in the same area from attacking Link. It also makes quiet Moblin sounds as Link moves around. While wearing the Moblin Mask, Link adopts a new idle pose to match that of a Moblin.
