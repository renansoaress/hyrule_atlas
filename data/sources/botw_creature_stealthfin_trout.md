### Breath of the Wild
Stealthfin Trout
Stealthfin Trouts can be found in bodies of Water in both the Great Hyrule Forest and Eldin Mountains, though they are plentiful within Lake Mekar. They are easy to find at night due to the fact that they glow in the dark. If Link eats a Stealthfin Trout, he will regain one Heart in his Life Gauge. They can also be burned by Fire to become Roasted Trout or chilled by Ice to become Frozen Trout. Cooking with Stealthfin Trout results in a Meal that will temporarily grant Link Stealth Up when eaten.

Stealthfin Trouts, as well as other Fish, can be used in a Cooking Pot to make to make the following Meals:

- Fish Skewer | Any Fish
- Fish and Mushroom Skewer | Any Fish, Any Mushroom
- Salt-Grilled Fish | Rock Salt, Any Fish or Snail

Stealthfin Trout are also counted among the Seafood that can make the following Meals:

- Creamy Seafood Soup | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Fish Pie | Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, Rock Salt, Any Seafood
- Glazed Seafood | Courser Bee Honey, Any Seafood
- Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Gourmet Meat, Any Seafood
- Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Meat, Any Seafood
- Monster Stew | Any Meat, Any Seafood, Monster Extract
- Pepper Seafood | Spicy Pepper, Any Seafood
- Prime Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Prime Meat, Any Seafood
- Seafood Meunière | Any Seafood (except Porgy or Salmon), Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter
- Seafood Rice Balls | Hylian Rice, Any Seafood
- Copious Seafood Skewers | Any Seafood (4 different kinds)
- Steamed Fish | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable,, Herb, or Flower

In order to upgrade the Stealth Set to its final tier at a Great Fairy Fountain, Link needs to gather ten Stealthfin Trouts and five Silent Princesses for each piece.
