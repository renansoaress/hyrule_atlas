### Breath of the Wild
Opal Earrings are a type of Accessory, being Earrings that can be clipped onto Link's ears. They can be purchased at the Starlight Memories Jewelry Shop in Gerudo Town for 200 Rupees and eight Opals. When worn, they provide one level of Swim Speed Up.

Opal Earrings can be enhanced by a Great Fairy at a Great Fairy Fountain. However, they cannot be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.
