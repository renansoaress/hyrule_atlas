### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Fruit Pies are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Fruit Pies are made by Cooking Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter, and any Fruit together in a Cooking Pot. However, Apples are unable to be used in this Recipe, as the resulting dish will be Apple Pie. Regular Fruit Pie can be made by using either Palm Fruits or Wildberries. Eating these Pies will restore some of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge. Fruit Pies are used commonly in celebrations in Hyrule.

Different variations of Fruit Pie can be made by using specific Fruits or by adding additional Ingredients. These variations are:

* Chilly Fruit Pie: Made using Hydromelons or other chilly Ingredients. Eating them will increase Link's Heat Resistance by one level.

* Electro Fruit Pie: Made using Voltfruits or other electric Ingredients. If consumed, they grant Link one temporary level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Fruit Pie: Made using enduring Ingredients. If eaten, they will completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Fruit Pie: Made using energizing Ingredients. Eating these will refill a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Fruit Pie: Made using Fleet-Lotus Seeds or other hasty Ingredients. Consuming these pies will temporarily boost Link's movement speed.

* Hearty Fruit Pie: Made using Hearty Durians or other hearty Ingredients. When eaten, these Pies will completely heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.

* Mighty Fruit Pie: Made using Mighty Bananas or other mighty Ingredients. These pies temporarily increase Link's attack power when he eats them.

* Sneaky Fruit Pie: Made using sneaky Ingredients. When eaten, they'll increase Link's Stealth temporarily.

* Spicy Fruit Pie: Made using Spicy Peppers or other spicy Ingredients. They grant Link one level of Cold Resistance if they're eaten.

* Tough Fruit Pie: Made using tough Ingredients. Eating these dishes will boost Link's Defense for a short period of time.
