### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Farosh's Claws are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Farosh's Claws originate from Farosh, no longer containing its Electricity powers. Farosh can be found flying through Lake Hylia, Lake Floria, or the Gerudo Highlands. When Link shoots at Farosh's hands with an Arrow, the Claw will be surrounded by light and fall to the ground onearby.

When used in Cooking, Farosh's Claws will always result in Dubious Food. However, if they are Cooked with critters, the result will be an Elixir. They can also be used at the Kochi Dye Shop as an ingredient for Light Yellow Dye.
