### Breath of the Wild
The Trousers of Twilight are a piece of Armor which can be obtained using the amiibo Rune while scanning the Super Smash Bros. Link amiibo or Twilight Princess Link amiibo. Each time the figure is scanned, a Treasure Chest will appear that has a small chance to contain the Trousers of Twilight. It can be enhanced by a Great Fairy but cannot be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.

The Trousers of Twilight, alongside the Cap of Twilight and Tunic of Twilight, comprise the Hero of Twilight Set which resembles Link's outfit from Twilight Princess. When it has been enhanced by two levels, it provides the Master Sword Beam Up Set Bonus when worn with the full set.
