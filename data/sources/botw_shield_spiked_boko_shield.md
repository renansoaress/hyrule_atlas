### Breath of the Wild
Spiked Boko Shield
Spiked Boko Shields are Boko Shields that are made using slightly stronger wood and reinforced with Animal bones. They are commonly used by Blue Bokoblins in East Necluda and the Faron Grasslands. They belong to the Bokoblin Gear equipment archetype, a series of Weapons created by Bokoblins. If scanned with the amiibo Rune, the Breath of the Wild Series Bokoblin amiibo has a chance to spawn a Treasure Chest containing a Spiked Boko Shield.

Because of their wooden composition, Spiked Boko Shields will burn if exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. However, they float if they are dropped in Water, and do not conduct Electricity or attract Lightning during Thunderstorms like metal Shields do. Finally, they are unable to be picked up with the Magnesis Rune.
