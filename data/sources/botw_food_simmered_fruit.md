### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Simmered Fruits are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Simmered Fruits are Meals that can be made by Cooking a maximum of three different Fruits together in a Cooking Pot. There are two ways to make this dish's default version. First, either Apples, Palm Fruits, or Wildberries must be used without any status-altering Fruits. Alternatively, Link can use multiple Fruits with contradicting status effects. If consumed, Simmered Fruits will restore at least one of his Hearts. They have a sweet taste, and the Fruits are simmered until tender.

Link can use certain Fruits, in addition to extra Ingredients, to make different variations of Simmered Fruits:

* Hearty Simmered Fruit: Made by using Hearty Durians or other hearty Ingredients. By eating them, Link can have all of his Hearts healed and his maximum Hearts temporarily increased.

* Energizing Simmered Fruit: Made by using energizing Ingredients. When eaten, they will refill part of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Enduring Simmered Fruit: Made by using enduring Ingredients. Link can eat these fruits to completely restore and overfill his Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Simmered Fruit: Made by using Fleet-Lotus Seeds or other hasty Ingredients. If Link eats them, Link's movement speed will be increased.

* Spicy Simmered Fruit: Made by using Spicy Peppers or other spicy Ingredients. When eaten, they will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.

* Electro Simmered Fruit: Made by using Voltfruits or other electric Ingredients. They can be eaten to give Link one level of Shock Resistance.

* Mighty Simmered Fruit: Made by using Mighty Bananas or other mighty Ingredients. These fruits will boost the power of Link's attacks when eaten.

* Tough Simmered Fruit: Made by using tough Ingredients. Eating these meals will increase Link's defensive abilities.

* Sneaky Simmered Fruit: Made by using sneaky Ingredients. When eaten, they'll increase Link's Stealth.

* Chilly Simmered Fruit: Made by using Hydromelons or other chilly Ingredients. Consuming them will grant Link one level of Heat Resistance.
