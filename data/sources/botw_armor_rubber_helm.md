### Breath of the Wild
The Rubber Helm, along with the Rubber Armor and Rubber Tights, comprise the Rubber Armor set. The Helm can be acquired by completing the "Thunder Magnet" Side Quest at Lakeside Stable. It provides Link with three defense and a level of Shock Resistance. After completing the quest, it will be available to purchase from Granté in Tarrey Town.
