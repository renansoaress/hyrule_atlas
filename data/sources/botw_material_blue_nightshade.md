### Breath of the Wild
Blue Nightshade
Blue Nightshades can be found growing in the Regions of West Necluda and Lanayru Great Spring in Hyrule Kingdom. Many Blue Nightshades grow near Cotera's Great Fairy Fountain outside of Kakariko Village. These Flowers glow at night, making them easier to find. When Blue Nightshade is Cooked into a Meal in a Cooking Pot, it will grant Link Stealth Up when eaten. At the Kochi Dye Shop, Link can use Blue Nightshades to make Blue Dye.

As part of the Side Quest "A Gift of Nightshade", Link must give Wabbin a Blue Nightshade that he can gift to Perda. However, once he obtains one, Wabbin will become nervous and ask Link to deliver the Blue Nightshade on his behalf. After he does this, Perda will confront Wabbin, asking how he knew that Blue Nightshades were her favorite Flower. Wabbin reveals that Blue Nightshades are his favorite Flowers as well, and he decided he would give them to his true love. Once the two of them confess their feelings, Wabbin will give Link 20 Rupees.

Like other Flowers, Blue Nightshades can be used in a Cooking Pot to make the following Meals:

- Cream of Mushroom Soup | Any Mushroom, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Cream of Vegetable Soup | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Creamy Meat Soup | Any Meat, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Creamy Seafood Soup | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Fried Wild Greens | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Copious Fried Wild Greens | Any Vegetables, Herbs, or Flowers(4 different kinds)
- Glazed Veggies | Courser Bee Honey, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Herb Sauté | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Goron Spice
- Salt-Grilled Greens | Rock Salt, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Steamed Fish | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable,, Herb, or Flower
- Steamed Fruit | Any Fruit, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Steamed Meat | Any Meat, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Pumpkins)
- Steamed Mushrooms | Any Mushroom, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
- Vegetable Omelet | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Bird Egg, Goat Butter, Rock Salt
- Veggie Rice Balls | Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower, Hylian Rice
