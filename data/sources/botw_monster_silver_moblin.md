### Breath of the Wild
Silver Moblin
Silver Moblins are tall Monsters that can be found all over Hyrule. Due to Calamity Ganon's magic, they are a powerful variant of Moblin, surpassing even Black Moblins, but they are outclassed by Golden Moblins. When defeated, they may drop Moblin Horns, Moblin Fangs, Moblin Guts, Amber, Opal, Topaz, Rubies, Sapphires, and Diamonds.
