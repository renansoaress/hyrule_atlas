Monster Extracts are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Monster Extracts are sold by Kilton at the Fang and Bone during the night. They can also be acquired as a reward for rescuing Chabi, who is searching for the Fang and Bone. Though Monster Extracts cannot be eaten directly, Meals made in a Cooking Pot with Monster Extract will have their effects randomly increased or decreased. They are also one of the ingredients that can be used at the Kochi Dye Shop to make Purple Dye.

Using Monster Extract while Cooking will result in monstrous Meals. They are required to make the following Meals:

- Monster Cake | Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter, Monster Extract
- Monster Curry | Hylian Rice, Goron Spice, Monster Extract
- Monster Rice Balls | Hylian Rice, Rock Salt, Monster Extract
- Monster Soup | Monster Extract, Tabantha Wheat, Fresh Milk, Goat Butter
- Monster Stew | Any Meat, Any Seafood, Monster Extract
