### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Lotus Seeds are a type of Roasted Food, and they are the result of Fleet-Lotus Seeds that have made contact with Fire or a Scorching Climate. Roasted Lotus Seeds are said to have a soft and flaky center inside their outer shell. When eaten, they restore three-quarters of a Heart to Link.
