Hydromelons are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.(BotW, TotK, AoC, AoI)

### Breath of the Wild
Hydromelon
Hydromelons are found growing from vines in the Gerudo Desert, as well as on certain parts of Satori Mountain. They are also available being sold by Emri at Kara Kara Bazaar and Lorn's Fruit Stand in Gerudo Town. They can be used by Link to restore half of a Heart. When Hydromelons are cooked into food, it will give Link Heat Resistance when eaten. If a Hydromelon comes into contact with Fire, it will turn into Roasted Hydromelon, which restores three-quarters of a Heart. At the Kochi Dye Shop, Hydromelons can be used to make Green Dye. Hydromelons are not required to upgrade any pieces of Armor at a Great Fairy Fountain.

Hydromelons are required to make Creamy Heart Soup in a Cooking Pot:

- Creamy Heart Soup | Hydromelon, Voltfruit, Any Radish, Fresh Milk

They can also be used in regular Fruit-based Meals:

- Fruit and Mushroom Mix | Any Fruit, Any Mushroom
- Fruit Pie | Any Fruit (besides Apple), Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter
- Fruitcake | Apple or Wildberry, Any Fruit, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar
- Honeyed Fruits | Courser Bee Honey, Any Fruit (besides Apple)
- Simmered Fruit | Any Fruit
- Copious Simmered Fruit | Any Fruit (4 different kinds)
- Steamed Fruit | Any Fruit, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
