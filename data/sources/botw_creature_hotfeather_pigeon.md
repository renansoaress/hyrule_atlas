### Breath of the Wild
Hotfeather Pigeon
Hotfeather Pigeons are rare breed of Pigeon. They can mainly be found in areas surrounding Death Mountain, particularly the Eldin Canyon and Eldin Mountains. They drop Raw Bird Thighs when they are killed. While their feathers are fireproof and used to make clothing, their Meat will burn when exposed to a Scorching Climate.
