### Breath of the Wild
Knight's Shield
Knight's Shields are mid-tier Shields once favored by the Knights of Hyrule who guarded the Royal Family of Hyrule. They belong to the Knight's Gear equipment archetype, a series of Weapons manufactured for Knights. They boast a great defense and Durability, comparable to that of even the strongest Enemy Shields. Though their metal construction makes them Durable, their weight makes it so they require more skill to wield.

They can be found around the northern half of Hyrule Kingdom, wielded by Enemies in the Hebra Mountains and Eldin Mountains. There are a few notable exceptions, however, including a Treasure Chest in Gerudo Canyon Pass. Another Treasure Chest is located near Aquame Bridge leading to the Coliseum Ruins. It can be obtained as a reward from Selmie for completing the Shield-Surfing Mini-Game with a time between 1:45 and 2:00. Finally, Russ sells a Knight's Shield on Trilby Plain for 300 Rupees. If Link buys it from him, he will call it a sturdy Shield, which reminds him of the rumored Durability of the Hylian Shield.

The following amiibo have a chance to spawn a Treasure Chest containing a Knight's Shield when they are scanned with the amiibo Rune:
* Super Smash Bros. Series Link amiibo
* série The Legend of Zelda Series Link (The Legend of Zelda) amiibo
* The Legend of Zelda Series Toon Zelda (The Wind Waker) amiibo
* The Legend of Zelda Series Link (Twilight Princess) amiibo
* The Legend of Zelda Series Link (Skyward Sword) amiibo
* Breath of the Wild Series Zelda amiibo

Being made of metal, Knight's Shields conduct Electricity and will attract Lightning if equipped during a Thunderstorm. In addition, they sink if they are dropped in Water, and will not burn when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. Finally, Knight's Shields can be picked up and manipulated with the Magnesis Rune on the Sheikah Slate.
