## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Enhanced Lizal Spear
Lizal Spears are Weapons used by Lizalfos for both fishing and combat. They can commonly be found on the Tabantha Frontier and in the Gerudo Desert, where they are often wielded by Blue Lizalfos. They can also be found in the hands of Fire-Breath Lizalfos, Ice-Breath Lizalfos, and Electric Lizalfos. These Spears are part of the Lizalfos Gear equipment archetype, a series of Weapons created by Lizalfos.

Because of their metallic spearheads, Enhanced Lizal Spears will attract Lightning during Thunderstorms and conduct Electricity. They will sink if they are dropped in Water, but they can be picked up again using the Magnesis Rune. In addition, they will not burn if they are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate.
