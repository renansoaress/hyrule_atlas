### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Fairy Tonics are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Link can make Fairy Tonics by using at least one Fairy on a Cooking Pot. These elixirs harness the power of Fairies, making them able to heal some of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge. Therefore, the more Fairies used, the stronger their effects will be. Their fragrance is known to be sweet.
