### Breath of the Wild
Tree Branch
Tree Branches are One-Handed Weapons that can be found near Trees or bushes. They are flammable and will catch Fire if exposed to open flames or a Scorching Climate. Tree Branches are also not made of magnetic materials and cannot be manipulated with Magnesis. They do not attract Lightning during Thunderstorms, and they do not conduct Electricity either. They will float in sufficiently deep bodies of Water. When thrown with the R Button, Tree Branches will leave Link's hand.

On one of the central supports of the Bridge of Hylia, Link can find a Tree Branch serving as a Natural Hiding Spot. When he picks this Tree Branch up, a Korok will appear and give him a Korok Seed.
