### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Gourmet Poultry Curries are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Gourmet Poultry Curries are made by Cooking a Raw Whole Bird, Goron Spice, and Hylian Rice together in a Cooking Pot. When eaten, they will heal several of Link's Hearts. Prior to the second Great Calamity, these Meals were served in Hyrule Castle, and the poultry used in making it is of high quality.

By adding more Ingredients, different variations of Gourmet Poultry Curry can be made:

* Chilly Gourmet Poultry Curry: Made by adding cold Ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of Heat Resistance temporarily.

* Electro Gourmet Poultry Curry: Made by adding electric Ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Gourmet Poultry Curry: Made by adding enduring Ingredients. When consumed, they will completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Gourmet Poultry Curry: Made by adding energizing Ingredients. If eaten, they will replenish a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Gourmet Poultry Curry: Made by adding hasty Ingredients. Link's movement speed is boosted by eating these Curries.

* Hearty Gourmet Poultry Curry: Made by adding hearty Ingredients. Eating these Curries will completely heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.

* Mighty Gourmet Poultry Curry: Made by adding mighty Ingredients. If eaten, they will boost the power of Link's attacks.

* Sneaky Gourmet Poultry Curry: Made by adding sneaky Ingredients. When Link eats these, he will receive an increase to his Stealth for a short period of time.

* Spicy Gourmet Poultry Curry: Made by adding spicy Ingredients. They grant Link one level of Cold Resistance when eaten.

* Tough Gourmet Poultry Curry: Made by adding tough Ingredients. They increase Link's Defense if consumed.
