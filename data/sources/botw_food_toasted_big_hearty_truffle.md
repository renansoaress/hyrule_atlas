### Breath of the Wild
Toasted Big Hearty Truffles are a type of Roasted Food, and they are made when Big Hearty Truffles are exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. They have better nutrition and a greater aroma than a regular Big Hearty Truffle. Eating a Toasted Big Hearty Truffle restores four and a half Hearts to Link's Life Gauge.
