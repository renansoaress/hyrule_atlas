## Location and Uses

### Breath of the Wild
Double Axe
Double Axes are Axes that have been designed with fighting in mind, though they require practice to use efficiently. They can be found either abandoned or being used by Monsters in West Necluda and the Gerudo Highlands. As with the Woodcutter's Axe, the Double Axe has a damage multiplier when used against Trees, allowing them to be felled in one swing.

Being made of metal, Double Axes will attract Lightning if they are equipped during a Thunderstorm and conduct Electricity. However, they will not burn when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate, and they can be picked up using the Magnesis Rune.
