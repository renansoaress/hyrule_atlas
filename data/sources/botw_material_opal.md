Opals are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Opals can be found by breaking Ore Deposits and Rare Ore Deposits. They are also sold by Kairo, who travels along the Mountain Road between Foothill Stable and the Maw of Death Mountain. The Kochi Dye Shop accepts Opals as an ingredient for Light Yellow Dye.
