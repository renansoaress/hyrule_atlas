### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Monster Saddles are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
The Monster Saddle can be purchased from Kilton at the Fang and Bone for 299 Mon. Kilton made it himself, though it was intended for a more Monster-like Horse. Despite their strange appearance, they are still useable for Horses.

The Monster Saddle is part of the Monster Horse Gear set, which is a group of Horse Gear. Link can equip his Horses with the Monster Saddle by speaking to specific workers at either Highland Stable, Outskirt Stable, South Akkala Stable, or Woodland Stable. As is the case with other Saddles, they are unable to be utilized by Epona or the Giant Horse.
