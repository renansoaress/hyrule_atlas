### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Electric Darners are recurring Creatures and Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Electric Darner
Electric Darners can be found on Hyrule Ridge and in the Gerudo Desert. Beedle also sells them at Riverside Stable. They can be used with Monster Parts in a Cooking Pot to make an Electro Elixir, which will temporarily provide Shock Resistance to Link. The Kochi Dye Shop accepts Electric Darners as an ingredient for Yellow Dye.

During the Side Quest "Little Sister's Big Request", Link finds out that Gleema likes Dragonflies, despite her sister Jana's fear of them. When Jana hears this, she asks Link to obtain an Electric Darner and other Dragonflies for Gleema's birthday on her behalf. Upon delivery, however, Jana will become disgusted by the Dragonflies and asks Link to give them to Gleema for her. Gleema will be excited about her Dragonflies and rush to show Jana, which causes her sister to shriek in terror. Afterwards, Jana gives him a Silver Rupee as a reward.
