### Breath of the Wild
Flamebreaker Helms are pieces of Armor that can be purchased in the Ripped and Shredded shop at Goron City. When worn, they provide one level of Flame Guard. Flamebreaker Helms can be enhanced by a Great Fairy and can be Hateno Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Flamebreaker Helm, alongside the Flamebreaker Armor and Flamebreaker Boots, compose the Fireproof Armor. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides a Fireproof Set Bonus.
