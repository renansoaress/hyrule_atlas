### Breath of the Wild
Armored Porgy
Armored Porgies are saltwater Fish that can be found in the waters of the Necluda Sea and Lanayru Sea. They are also sold by Mubs at the fish market in Lurelin Village and by the traveling Merchant Chumin. As is the case with both Armored Porgies and Mighty Porgies, they can be chilled by Ice to make Frozen Porgies or burned by Fire to make Roasted Porgies. Link can eat one raw to restore one of his Hearts. They have hard scales with a compound that, when Cooked, will make a Meal that temporarily grants Link Defense Up.

Either an Armored Porgy or a Mighty Porgy are required to make the following Meals:

- Porgy Meunière | Any Porgy, Goat Butter, Tabantha Wheat
- Seafood Curry | Goron Spice, Hylian Rice, Hearty Blueshell Snail or Any Porgy
- Seafood Fried Rice | Hylian Rice, Rock Salt, Hearty Blueshell Snail or Any Porgy
- Seafood Paella | Hearty Blueshell Snail, Any Porgy, Hylian Rice, Goat Butter, Rock Salt

A Porgy or a Hearty Blueshell Snail can be used to make the following Meals:

- Porgy Meunière | Any Porgy, Goat Butter, Tabantha Wheat
- Seafood Curry | Goron Spice, Hylian Rice, Hearty Blueshell Snail or Any Porgy
- Seafood Fried Rice | Hylian Rice, Rock Salt, Hearty Blueshell Snail or Any Porgy
- Seafood Paella | Hearty Blueshell Snail, Any Porgy, Hylian Rice, Goat Butter, Rock Salt

Like other Fish, Armored Porgies can be Cooked to make the following Meals:

- Fish Skewer | Any Fish
- Fish and Mushroom Skewer | Any Fish, Any Mushroom
- Salt-Grilled Fish | Rock Salt, Any Fish or Snail

Armored Porgies count as Seafood when making the following Meals:

- Creamy Seafood Soup | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower (except Carrots or Pumpkins), Fresh Milk, Rock Salt
- Fish Pie | Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, Rock Salt, Any Seafood
- Glazed Seafood | Courser Bee Honey, Any Seafood
- Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Gourmet Meat, Any Seafood
- Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Meat, Any Seafood
- Monster Stew | Any Meat, Any Seafood, Monster Extract
- Pepper Seafood | Spicy Pepper, Any Seafood
- Prime Meat and Seafood Fry | Any Prime Meat, Any Seafood
- Seafood Meunière | Any Seafood (except Porgy or Salmon), Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter
- Seafood Rice Balls | Hylian Rice, Any Seafood
- Copious Seafood Skewers | Any Seafood (4 different kinds)
- Steamed Fish | Any Seafood, Any Vegetable,, Herb, or Flower
