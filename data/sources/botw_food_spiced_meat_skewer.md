### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Spiced Meat Skewers are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Spiced Meat Skewers can be Cooked by combining Raw Meat and Goron Spice together in a Cooking Pot. By eating them, Link can replenish some of his lost Hearts. The Goron Spice covers up the scent of the Meat, which allows its flavor to shine.

Link can add extra Ingredients while making Spice Meat Skewers, which results in the following variations:

* Hearty Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding hearty ingredients. Eating these Meals will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him Extra Hearts.

* Energizing Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding energizing ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be partially refilled if he eats them.

* Enduring Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding enduring Ingredients. When eaten, they completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding hasty Ingredients. They can be eaten to increase Link's movement speed.

* Spicy Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding spicy Ingredients. Link will gain one level of Cold Resistance when he eats these meals.

* Electro Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding electric Ingredients. If eaten, they grant Link one level of Shock Resistance.

* Mighty Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding mighty Ingredients. Eating them will provide Link with a boost to his attack power.

* Tough Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding tough Ingredients. By eating them, Link's Defense will increase.

* Sneaky Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding sneaky Ingredients. Link's Stealth will be boosted when he eats them.

* Chilly Spiced Meat Skewer: Made by adding chilly Ingredients. When eaten, they will grant Link one level of Heat Resistance.
