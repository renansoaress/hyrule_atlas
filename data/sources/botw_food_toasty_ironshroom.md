### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Toasty Ironshrooms are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Toasty Ironshrooms are a type of Roasted Food, and they are made when Ironshrooms are burned using a Fire or a Scorching Climate. The exterior of Toasty Ironshrooms is noted to be crunchy. Eating one will restore three-quarters of one of Link's Hearts.
