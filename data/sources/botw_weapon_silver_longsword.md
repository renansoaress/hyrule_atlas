### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Zora Longswords, also known as Silver Longswords, are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Silver Longsword
Silver Longswords are two-handed swords made by the Zora, in spite of their preference for Spears. Their unique design have made them popular among the Hylians. This Weapon shares its elegant, silver style with the other pieces of Zora Gear, such as the Silverscale Spear. Silver Longswords are commonly found around the Lanayru Great Spring and Lanayru Wetlands. A Silver Longsword can be found behind the Ne'ez Yohma Shrine in Zora's Domain.

Silver Longswords are made of metal and as such cannot be burned when exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate, nor will they float in Water. However, they do attract Lightning during Thunderstorms and conduct Electricity, and they can be picked up and manipulated with the Magnesis Rune.
