### Breath of the Wild
Spicy Pepper
Spicy Peppers grow from bushes on the Great Plateau, near the Gerudo Canyon Stable, and in the Tabantha Frontier. They can be eaten to restore half a Heart. When cooked, the resulting dish will grant Link Cold Resistance when eaten. Spicy Peppers exposed to a Fire turn into Charred Peppers, which restore three-quarters of a Heart. Link can use Spicy Peppers at the Kochi Dye Shop as Red Dye.

Spicy Peppers are required to make Pepper Seafood, Spicy Pepper Steak, and Spicy Sautéed Peppers.

- Pepper Seafood | Spicy Pepper, Any Seafood
- Pepper Steak | Spicy Pepper, Any Meat
- Sautéed Peppers | Spicy Pepper

Like other Fruits, Spicy Peppers can be used to make the following Meals:

- Fruit and Mushroom Mix | Any Fruit, Any Mushroom
- Fruit Pie | Any Fruit (besides Apple), Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter
- Fruitcake | Apple or Wildberry, Any Fruit, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar
- Honeyed Fruits | Courser Bee Honey, Any Fruit (besides Apple)
- Simmered Fruit | Any Fruit
- Copious Simmered Fruit | Any Fruit (4 different kinds)
- Steamed Fruit | Any Fruit, Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower
