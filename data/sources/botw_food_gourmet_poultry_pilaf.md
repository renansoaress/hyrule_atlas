### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Gourmet Poultry Pilafs are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Gourmet Poultry Pilafs are meals made by Cooking a Raw Whole Bird, Hylian Rice, Goat Butter, and a Bird Egg together in a Cooking Pot. When eaten, they restore a few Hearts to Link's Life Gauge. Not only are they made with high-quality poultry, but they also flood one's mouth with flavor with every bite.

Additional ingredients can be added to Gourmet Poultry Pilaf, which can result in one of the following variations:

* Chilly Gourmet Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding cold ingredients. When they are eaten, Link gains one level of Heat Resistance.

* Electro Gourmet Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding electric ingredients. Eating them will grant Link on level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Gourmet Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding enduring ingredients. Consuming these will completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Gourmet Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding energizing ingredients. If eaten, they will refill a part of Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Hasty Gourmet Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding hasty ingredients. If they are eaten, Link's movement speed will receive a short boost.

* Hearty Gourmet Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding hearty ingredients. Eating these Pilafs will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.

* Mighty Gourmet Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding mighty ingredients. Link gains an increase to the power of his attacks when he eats these.

* Sneaky Gourmet Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding sneaky ingredients. If eaten, Link will receive a temporary boost to his Stealth.

* Spicy Gourmet Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding spicy ingredients. When eaten, they will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance.

* Tough Gourmet Poultry Pilaf: Made by adding tough ingredients. When eaten, they increase Link's defense.
