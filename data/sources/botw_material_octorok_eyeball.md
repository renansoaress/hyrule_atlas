Octorok Eyeballs are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Octorok Eyeballs are Monster Parts that are dropped by Forest Octoroks, Rock Octoroks, Snow Octoroks, Treasure Octoroks, and Water Octoroks when killed. They can be combined with critters in a Cooking Pot to make Elixirs. The Kochi Dye Shop accepts Octorok Eyeballs as an ingredient for Yellow Dye, or they can be exchanged at the Fang and Bone for Mon.
