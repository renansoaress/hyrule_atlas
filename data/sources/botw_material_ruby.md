### Breath of the Wild
Rubies are rare Materials occasionally obtained from breaking Ore Deposits. They are also dropped by various types of Stone Taluses, Silver Enemy variants and Yiga Blademasters when killed. They are sold at Ore and More in Tarrey Town and by Kairo at Foothill Stable. Rubies are said to contain the power of fire within them, granting warmth in colder temperatures when used in Armor.

Link can sell Rubies for 210 Rupees in most Shops, though the merchant Ramella will occasionally pay more for them in groups of 10 after completing the Side Quest "The Jewel Trade". Rubies are required to purchase the Ruby Circlet from "Starlight Memories" Shop. Additionally, the description of the Snowquill Headdress reveals it is adorned with a Ruby.
