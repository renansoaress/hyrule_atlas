### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Hasty Elixirs are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Link can drink Hasty Elixirs to receive a boost to his movement speed. This haste effect will influence his running, Swimming, and Climbing speed.

Hasty Elixirs come in three varying levels of potency, granting low-, mid-, and high-level improvements to his speed. These Elixirs can be made by Cooking any of the following critters with Monster Parts in a Cooking Pot:

* Hightail Lizard

* Hot-Footed Frog

By adding any of these hasty ingredients, Link can make a Hasty Elixir that will also heal him:

* Fleet-Lotus Seeds

* Rushroom

* Swift Violet

* Swift Carrot
