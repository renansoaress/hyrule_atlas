### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Toasty Silent Shrooms are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Toasty Silent Shrooms are a type of Roasted Food. Link can make Toasty Silent Shrooms by burning Silent Shrooms with Fire or a Scorching Climate. While they have lost their glowing properties, they are noted to be fragrant. When Link eats a Toasty Silent Shroom, he will heal three-quarters of a Heart.
