Fish and Mushroom Skewers are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Fish and Mushroom Skewers are made by Cooking any Mushroom with any Fish. Eating one will restore some of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge. The Fish used in these skewers are fresh, while the Mushrooms are fragrant.

Several different variations of Fish and Mushroom Skewers exist, depending on both the Ingredients are used and whether any additional Ingredients are added:

* Hearty Fish and Mushroom Skewer: Made using Hearty Salmons, Hearty Bass, Hearty Truffles, Big Hearty Truffles, or Hearty Durian. When eaten, will heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.

* Enduring Fish and Mushroom Skewer: Made using Endura Shrooms. Eating them will refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Fish and Mushroom Skewer: Made using Staminoka Basses, Stamella Shrooms, or Bright-Eyed Crabs. If eaten, Link's Stamina Wheel will be partially refilled.

* Hasty Fish and Mushroom Skewer: Made using Rushrooms or Fleet-Lotus Seeds. Eating one will grant Link a temporary boost to his movement speed.

* Spicy Fish and Mushroom Skewer: Made using Sizzlefin Trouts or Sunshrooms. Link will gain one level of Cold Resistance when these are eaten.

* Electro Fish and Mushroom Skewer: Made using Voltfin Trout, Zapshrooms, or Voltfruits. Eating these Skewers will give Link one level of Shock Resistance.

* Mighty Fish and Mushroom Skewer: Made using Mighty Carp, Mighty Porgies, Razorshrooms, or Mighty Bananas. Eating them will boost Link's attacks for a short time.

* Tough Fish and Mushroom Skewer: Made using Armored Carp, Armored Porgies, or Ironshrooms. If eaten, Link's Defense will increase.

* Sneaky Fish and Mushroom Skewer: Made using Stealthfin Trouts or Silent Shrooms. Eating one will temporarily increase Link's Stealth.

* Chilly Fish and Mushroom Skewer: Made using Chillfin Trouts, Chillshrooms, or Hydromelons. When eaten, Link will gain one level of Heat Resistance temporarily.
