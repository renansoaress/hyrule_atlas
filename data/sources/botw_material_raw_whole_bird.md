### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Raw Whole Birds are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Raw Whole Birds are dropped when White Pigeons and Eldin Ostriches are killed. Eating a Raw Whole Bird will heal three Hearts. Raw Whole Birds can be burned with Fire to make Roasted Whole Birds or chilled with Ice to make Frozen Whole Birds.

A Raw Whole Bird is required to make Gourmet Poultry Curry and Gourmet Poultry Pilaf in a Cooking Pot. Either a Raw Whole Bird or a Raw Gourmet Meat is needed to make Gourmet Meat Stew, Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl, Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat, and Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry. They are one of the types of Meat that can be used to make Creamy Meat Soup, Glazed Meat, a Meat and Mushroom Skewer, Meat Pie, a Meat Skewer, Copious Meat Skewers, Meaty Rice Balls, Monster Stew, Pepper Steak, Steamed Meat, and a Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin.
