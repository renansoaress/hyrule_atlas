## Characteristics
Great-Horned Rhinoceros
Great-Horned Rhinoceros live in the Hebra Mountains and the Gerudo Highlands in Hyrule where they are well adapted to their cold environment, having evolved from arid-region rhinoceros who migrated to Freezing Climates. They have a particularly thick, dark blue hide with a lighter blue underbelly, and two curved horns on their snouts, the longest of which is half the length of its own body. Due to the potential deadliness of this horn and the fact that they are aggressive towards people, they are only hunted by experienced hunters.

Like most Creatures, they can be killed for Foods, dropping either Raw Prime Meat or Raw Gourmet Meat when killed. Bokoblins will occasionally hunt Great-Horned Rhinoceros.
