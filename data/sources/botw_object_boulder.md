Boulders,(LA, MM, TMC, ALBW, BotW, TotK, EoW, NL, CoH) also known as Rocks,(The Legend of Zelda) Huge Rocks,(The Wind Waker) and ,(TFoE, TWoG) are recurring Enemies and Objects in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Boulders in Breath of the Wild are sometimes used in puzzles to reveal Koroks that are scattered across Hyrule Kingdom where Link must push a Boulder into a hole some distance away. Sometimes the holes are at the bottom of a slope with the Boulders at the top, and sometimes they are on level ground and must be launched using Stasis. A variation of this puzzle involves using Magnesis on an Iron Ball to get it into the hole. After at least one Boulder makes it into the hole, a Korok will appear and give Link a Korok Seed.
