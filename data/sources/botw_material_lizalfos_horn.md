Lizalfos Horns are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Lizalfos Horns are winding Monster Horns dropped by all varieties of Lizalfos as well as Stalizalfos when defeated. They are too hard to Cook Food with, but they can be used in a Cooking Pot with critters to make Elixirs with an additional minute and 10 seconds to their duration. The Kochi Dye Shop also accepts Lizalfos Horns as an ingredient for Gray Dye. Finally, Kilton at the Fang and Bone will take Lizalfos Horns in exchange for Mon.

Three Lizalfos Horns are used to upgrade each piece of the Zora Set to its first tier at a Great Fairy Fountain.
