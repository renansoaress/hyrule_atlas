### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Rainbow Sparrows are recurring Creatures in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Rainbow Sparrow
Rainbow Sparrows are a brightly colored variety of Sparrow, with more vivid coloring than their closer relatives. Typical Rainbow Sparrows have purple wings, backs and tails, pink necks and tail tips, red heads, creamy throats and green bellies. They chiefly inhabit tropical forests within Faron, such as the Faron Grasslands. They also live in areas of West Necluda. In addition to this, Rainbow Sparrows are omnivores, feeding on a mixture of nuts and small Insects. They gather in small groups, but they will fly away if they are disturbed. A Rainbow Sparrow that is killed will drop a Chickaloo Tree Nut.
