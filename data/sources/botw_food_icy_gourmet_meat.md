Icy Gourmet Meats are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Icy Gourmet Meats are a type of Frozen Food, which is made by freezing Raw Gourmet Meat in a Freezing Climate or Cold Water. Being frozen, Icy Gourmet Meats are easier to transport, and they are able to retain their flavor. Eating one will restore three Hearts to Link's Life Gauge and grant one level of temporary Heat Resistance.
