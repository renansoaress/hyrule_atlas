## Characteristics
Sentry
Sentries are a type of flying Guardian, similar to Guardian Skywatchers, that only appear on Death Mountain during the "Divine Beast Vah Rudania" Main Quest. Divine Beast Vah Rudania dispatches Sentries as Link and Yunobo approach Death Mountain to hinder them as they attempt to disable the Divine Beast. Sentries use red searchlights along the path up the mountain which, should Link or Yunobo be spotted, will cause the Divine Beast to furiously unleash Magma Bombs at them.

While Sentries have a high amount of health at 1000, they are extremely weak to Remote Bombs and physics damage and are one of the few Enemies that can be taken down with a single Remote Bomb. When defeated, they will drop a random Ancient Material.

## Trivia
*Even though Sentries are under the control of Calamity Ganon, the Master Sword's full power does not activate when near one.BotW
