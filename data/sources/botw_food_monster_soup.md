### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Monster Soups are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Monster Soups can be made by Cooking Monster Extract, Tabantha Wheat, Fresh Milk, and Goat Butter together in a Cooking Pot. Due to Monster Extract being required to make them, the amount of Hearts restored to Link is random each time these soups are made. The same also applies to the strength and duration of any status effects. Since Monster Extract is used as a base, the soup is distinctly gamy.

By adding more Ingredients, Link can create several different variations of Monster Soup:

* Chilly Monster Soup: Made by adding chilly Ingredients. These soups will grant Link one level of Heat Resistance when eaten.

* Electro Monster Soup: Made by adding electric Ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of Shock Resistance.

* Enduring Monster Soup: Made by adding enduring Ingredients. Link can eat them to completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.

* Energizing Monster Soup: Made by adding energizing Ingredients. When eaten, a portion of Link's Stamina Wheel will be refilled.

* Hasty Monster Soup: Made by adding hasty Ingredients. They can be eaten to increase Link's movement speed.

* Hearty Monster Soup: Made by adding hearty Ingredients. Eating these soups will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him Extra Hearts.

* Mighty Monster Soup: Made by adding mighty Ingredients. Link can consume these soups to increase the power of his attacks.

* Sneaky Monster Soup: Made by adding sneaky Ingredients. When eaten, Link will receive a boost to his Stealth.

* Spicy Monster Soup: Made by adding spicy Ingredients. If they are eaten, Link will gain one level of Cold Resistance.

* Tough Monster Soup: Made by adding tough Ingredients. By eating them, Link can gain a boost to his Defense.
