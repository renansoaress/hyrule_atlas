### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Pumpkin Pies are recurring Meals in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Link can make Pumpkin Pies by Cooking a Fortified Pumpkin, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, and Goat Butter together in a Cooking Pot. The default version can only be made by adding either a Fruit or a Vegetable with an effect that differs from Fortified Pumpkins. When eaten, they will restore some of his Hearts. Due to the sweetness of Fortified Pumpkins, these desserts are popular among children.

Only one variation of Pumpkin Pie exists:

* Tough Pumpkin Pie: Made by adding no additional ingredients. By consuming these Pies, Link's defense will be temporarily boosted.
