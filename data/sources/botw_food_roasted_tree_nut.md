Roasted Tree Nuts are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Tree Nuts are a type of Roasted Food. Link can make them by exposing Chickaloo Tree Nuts to Fire or a Scorching Climate. These Nuts have a better fragrance than their uncooked counterpart. Eating one restores one Heart to Link's Life Gauge.
