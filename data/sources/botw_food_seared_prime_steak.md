### Breath of the Wild
Seared Prime Steaks are a type of Roasted Food. Link can make Seared Prime Steaks by burning Raw Prime Meats with a Fire or a Scorching Climate. Upon their death, Black Hinox may also drop this item, and they can be purchased from Estan's Stall in Gerudo Town. The outsides of Seared Prime Steaks are considered perfectly browned, while their insides are juicy. Eating one restores two and one quarter of Link's Hearts.
