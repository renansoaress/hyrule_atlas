Roasted Mighty Bananas are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Roasted Mighty Bananas are a type of Roasted Food. They are the result of Mighty Bananas that have been exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate. Roasted Mighty Bananas have a sticky, syrupy consistency and a rich, aromatic smell. Link can restore three quarters of a Heart to his Life Gauge by eating Roasted Mighty Bananas.
