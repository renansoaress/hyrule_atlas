### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Honey Crepes are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Honey Crepes are dishes made by Cooking Fresh Milk, a Bird Egg, Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, and Courser Bee Honey together in a Cooking Pot. They can be eaten to refill two fifths of Link's Stamina Wheel and restore ten Hearts of his Life Gauge. The Courser Bee Honey drizzled over them brings out their natural sweetness and flavor. Since five ingredients are necessary to make Honey Crepes, only their energizing version can be made.
