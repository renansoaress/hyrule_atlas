### Breath of the Wild
Red Sparrow
Red Sparrows are a type of Sparrow. They live among the mountains of the Hebra Region, specifically on the Hebra Mountains proper and the Tabantha Frontier. These Sparrows gather in groups, but they will fly away if they are disturbed. They maintain a herbivorous diet, feeding mainly on nuts and wild plants, and their feathers are commonly used for decorations. When killed, Red Sparrows will drop Chickaloo Tree Nuts.
