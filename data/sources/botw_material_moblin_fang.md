Moblin Fangs are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Moblin Fangs are Monster Parts that are dropped by all types of Moblins and Stalmoblins when they're defeated. They are sharp and too hard to be reshaped. They can be used in a Cooking Pot with critters to make Elixirs. The Kochi Dye Shop can also use Moblin Fangs to make Gray Dye, and Kilton at the Fang and Bone accepts them in exchange for Mon.

The Fireproof Armor Armor Set needs four Moblin Fangs to upgrade each of its pieces to their second tier at a Great Fairy Fountain.
