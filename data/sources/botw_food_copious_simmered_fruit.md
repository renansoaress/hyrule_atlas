### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Copious Simmered Fruits are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Link can make Copious Simmered Fruits by Cooking four different Fruits together in a Cooking Pot. Their default version can be made by using at least two Fruits with differing Added Effects. Eating them will heal some of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge. The flavors of the Fruits used in these Meals exist in perfect harmony.

If Link uses an Apple, a Wildberry, and a Palm Fruit when making Copious Simmered Fruits, the fourth Fruit that he uses will grant the dish a status effect. This can result in the following variations:

* Hearty Copious Simmered Fruit: Made using Hearty Durians. When eaten, they'll heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.

* Hasty Copious Simmered Fruit: Made using Fleet-Lotus Seeds. Link's movement speed will receive a boost when he eats these.

* Spicy Copious Simmered Fruit: Made using Spicy Peppers. These fruits will grant Link one level of Cold Resistance if he eats them.

* Electro Copious Simmered Fruit: Made using Voltfruits. By eating them, Link can gain one level of Shock Resistance.

* Mighty Copious Simmered Fruit: Made using Mighty Bananas. If they're eaten, they'll grant Link a boost to the power of his attacks.

* Chilly Copious Simmered Fruit: Made using Hydromelons. These meals can be eaten to provide Link one level of Heat Resistance.
