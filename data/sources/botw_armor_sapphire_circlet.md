### Breath of the Wild
Sapphire Circlets are a type of Circlet Accessory that is worn as head Armor. They can be purchased in the Starlight Memories Jewelry Shop in Gerudo Town. They cost 800 Rupees and three Sapphires to buy. Alternatively, at the end of the "Tools of the Trade" Side Quest, one of the Accessories that Isha offers to make Link as a reward is a Sapphire Circlet. When worn, they provide Link with one level of Heat Resistance. They also provide the hidden Added Effect Ancient Pow Up, increasing damage done by Ancient Soldier Gear and Guardian Gear by 20%.

Sapphire Circlets can be enhanced by a Great Fairy at a Great Fairy Fountain. However, they cannot be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.
