### Breath of the Wild
The Dark Trousers are a piece of Armor that can be purchased from Kilton at his Fang and Bone shop after all Divine Beasts have been freed. They cannot be enhanced by a Great Fairy, nor can they be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Dark Trousers, alongside the Dark Hood and Dark Tunic, compose the Dark Series. When the full set is worn, it provides a Night Speed Up bonus. Also, characters get scared when they see Link wearing it.
