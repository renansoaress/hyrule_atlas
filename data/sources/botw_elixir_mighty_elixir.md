Mighty Elixirs are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.(BotW, TotK)

### Breath of the Wild
Link can drink Mighty Elixirs to receive a boost to his attack power. This effect works by strengthening one's body and mind, and it applies to all Weapons.

Mighty Elixirs can be made by Cooking any of the following critters with Monster Parts in a Cooking Pot:

* Bladed Rhino Beetle

By adding any of these mighty ingredients, Link can make a Mighty Elixir that will also heal him:

* Mighty Bananas

* Razorshroom

* Mighty Thistle

* Mighty Carp

* Mighty Porgy

* Razorclaw Crab
