### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Enduring Elixirs are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.(BotW, TotK)

### Breath of the Wild
Enduring Elixirs will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel when he drinks them. The excess stamina granted to Link will disappear as he uses it.

Enduring Elixirs can be made by Cooking any of the following critters with Monster Parts in a Cooking Pot:

* Tireless Frog

By adding any of these enduring ingredients, Link can make an Enduring Elixir that will also heal him:

* Endura Shroom

* Endura Carrot
