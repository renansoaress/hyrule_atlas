### Breath of the Wild
Fire Keese Wings are Materials that are dropped by Fire Keese, which can be found flying in the Eldin and Akkala Regions. They can be exchanged for six Mon at the Fang and Bone or sold for six Rupees. Like other Monster Parts, Fire Keese Wings can be combined with critters in a Cooking Pot to make Elixirs. Fire Keese Wings can also be used to produce Red Dye at the Kochi Dye Shop.
