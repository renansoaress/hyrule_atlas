### Texto (somente seções de BotW)
Sky Octoroks are recurring Enemies in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Sky Octorok
Sky Octoroks only appear in Master Mode included in DLC Pack 1, , in Breath of the Wild. They can be found all around Hyrule soaring through the skies next to bridges and Sheikah Towers, or above bodies of water. Sky Octoroks appear in groups of three or four holding planks containing Treasure Chests, and enemies. They ascend when they hear a Bokoblin Horn or Link whistling. Sky Octoroks can be defeated with a single Arrow, dropping the contents of their planks when defeated. They are harmless to Link as they cannot attack like other Octoroks. Unlike other enemies, Sky Octoroks do not need the Blood Moon to respawn and do not drop Materials when defeated.
