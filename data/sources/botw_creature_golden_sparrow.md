### Breath of the Wild
Golden Sparrow
Golden Sparrows are a variety of Sparrow that inhabit the mountains of the Eldin Region, primarily the slopes of Death Mountain itself. They survive in this harsh environment thanks to their body feathers being resistant to burning, which protects them from Death Mountain's Scorching Climate. They are primarily predators in diet, and feed on small Insects that they search for among rocks. They gather in flocks, but fly away if disturbed. Golden Sparrows drop a single Chickaloo Tree Nut when killed.
