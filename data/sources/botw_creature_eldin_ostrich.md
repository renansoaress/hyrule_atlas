### Breath of the Wild
Eldin Ostrich
Eldin Ostriches are Ostriches that can be found in the Eldin Region of the Hyrule Kingdom, particularly the Scorching Climate surrounding Death Mountain. They have heat resistant feathers that allow them to live comfortably in the Region. Although they are unable to fly, Eldin Ostriches can run fast. When killed, they drop either a Raw Bird Thigh or a Raw Whole Bird.
