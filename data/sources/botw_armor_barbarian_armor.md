### Breath of the Wild
The Barbarian Armor is a piece of Armor located in the Dila Maag Shrine at South Lomei Labyrinth. After obtaining it, Granté will begin selling the piece of Armor for 4,000 Rupees. According to its description, the Armor was worn by the warriors of an ancient warlike tribe from the Faron Region. When worn, it provides one level of Attack Up. The Barbarian Armor can be enhanced by a Great Fairy and can be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop. The Barbarian Armor, alongside the Barbarian Helm and Barbarian Leg Wraps, compose the Barbarian Set. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides a Charge Atk. Stamina Up Set Bonus.
