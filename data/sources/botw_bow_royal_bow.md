Royal Bows are recurring Items in série The Legend of Zelda.

### Breath of the Wild
Royal Bow
Royal Bows are Bows that King Rhoam used to gift to the most talented archers in Hyrule. Both their combat ability and design are noted to be impressive. They are commonly found in Treasure Chests or in use by high-tier Enemies on the Tabantha Frontier and in the Hebra Mountains.

When scanned with the amiibo Rune, the following amiibo have a chance to spawn a Treasure Chest containing a Royal Bow:
* Super Smash Bros. Series Zelda amiibo
* Breath of the Wild Series Link (Archer) amiibo

Due to their metallic composition, Royal Bows will conduct Electricity and attract Lightning if they are outdoors during a Thunderstorm. They will also sink if they are dropped in Water, but they can be picked up using the Magnesis Rune. Finally, they will not burn if exposed to Fire or a Scorching Climate.

Royal Bows belong to the Royal Gear equipment archetype, which is associated with the Royal Family of Hyrule.
