### Breath of the Wild
Topaz Earrings are a type of Accessory, specifically being Earrings that can be clipped onto Link's ears. They can be purchased at the Starlight Memories Jewelry Shop in Gerudo Town for 500 Rupees and five Topazes. Alternatively, at the end of the "Tools of the Trade" Side Quest, one of the Accessories that Isha offers Link as a reward are the Topaz Earrings. When worn, Topaz Earrings provide Link with one level of Shock Resistance. They also provide the hidden Added Effect Ancient Pow Up, increasing damage done by Ancient Soldier Gear and Guardian Gear by 20%.

Topaz Earrings can be enhanced by a Great Fairy at a Great Fairy Fountain. However, they cannot be Dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.
